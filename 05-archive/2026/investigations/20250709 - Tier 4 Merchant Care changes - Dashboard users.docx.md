**Email preview:** We’re phasing out email as a support channel from August 2

**`REMINDER – CHANGES TO CHECKOUT.COM SUPPORT`**

We’d like to remind you that from **August 2, 2025**, we’re phasing out email as a support channel.

This follows the recent launch of [live chat](https://dashboard.checkout.com/) for real-time human support with your queries if our AI assistant can’t help you. In addition to our existing [webform](https://dashboard.checkout.com/support), you now have flexible, more secure help options through authenticated Dashboard channels.

By phasing out email, we’ll be able to dedicate more resources to these options, so you can get quicker and more secure support when you need it. 

**`Questions?`**

Questions about this update? Give live chat a go by [logging into the Dashboard](https://dashboard.checkout.com/) and looking for the chat icon in the bottom right.

Best,  
Checkout.com support team
