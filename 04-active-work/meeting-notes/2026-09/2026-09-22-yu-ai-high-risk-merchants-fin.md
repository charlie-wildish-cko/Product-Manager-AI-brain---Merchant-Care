## Context

Yu Ai had escalated to Jenny after Bet365 received a poor Fin response during the Mastercard payout incident. The call covered what went wrong and a request to exempt sensitive merchants from AI-first.

## Key Points

**Why newly live merchants are sensitive**
- They compare Checkout against an incumbent PSP that often still holds 90% of their volume and will fight to keep them.
- Tier-3-level volume, high strategic weight. AMs cannot cover weekends or after-hours.
- Yu Ai: "what matters most to every commercial person is the experience that the customer had."

**Bet365 case**
- Fin classified the Mastercard incident as a pending payouts issue and told the merchant to fill out the RFI form, because it was not using outage data.
- Not detected as an incident, so the ticket was not bumped up the queue.
- Fix: on email, suspected outages now skip Fin and go straight to a human.
- Some complaints about "AI" responses turned out to be from human agents. Separate thread with Ashvine's team on agent response quality.

**How Charlie described Fin**
- First-point triage replying in 1-2 minutes. About 80% of inbound is simple and self-servable. Complex cases auto-escalate.
- Content is verified public documentation. Errors mostly come from misdetecting the question when merchants use unfamiliar terms.
- Escalated tickets can stall in the human backlog. That is not an AI problem.

**Expectation setting**
- Yu Ai (ex-Flywire customer service): context and reasons for delays improve reactions. Charlie agreed. The right work happens internally, but there are no proactive updates. This sits with Ashvine's team.

**Exemption request**
- Yu Ai wants a small set of sensitive merchants taken out of AI-first: "10 globally" at one point, then no more than 3. Named: Bet365, probably Polymarket. Crypto and iGaming merchants fall into the unusual 20% of requests.
- Asked whether frustration triggers ("let me speak to a human") route straight to a human.
- Charlie: send client IDs, and Charlie will assess their volume and impact on AI resolution goals, and whether their query types are answered correctly. A merchant exception list adds complexity, and many Care issues are unfixed product issues.
- Charlie has told leadership that an automation-percentage goal is a cost goal, not an experience goal. Experience must not degrade as automation rises.
