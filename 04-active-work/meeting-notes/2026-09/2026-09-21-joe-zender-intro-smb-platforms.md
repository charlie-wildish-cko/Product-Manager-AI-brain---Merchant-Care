## Context

Introductory call with Joe, who started the previous week and covers the Platforms and SMB side under Milan. Covered Care history, segmentation, cost to serve and tooling.

## Key Points

**Care history**
- Before 2023 Care was badly underinvested: support SLO at 30%, commercial teams complaining. Agents mainly in Mauritius (long tenure, average 2-3 years, some 5+), small groups in London and Mexico, working as "detectives" without tools.
- 2023 brought ops and product investment. Salesforce to Zendesk migration was one of Charlie's first moves. Zendesk is now being re-evaluated. Enterprise support debt remains.

**Scale and AI-first**
- B2B (SMB, Platforms) plus B2C takes contacts from about 50-60k a year into the millions. Requires AI-first support with humans in the loop.
- Zilch comparison: six-figure monthly contacts, very high AI resolution. Charlie's distinction: Zilch work is customer-generated. Much of Checkout's is Checkout-generated (risk, compliance, fraud alerts, scheduled processes) and needs proactive outreach.
- At Zilch: 100k customers onboarded a month, 280 FTE, one market, one product.

**Tooling**
- Joe: Zendesk is a case management tool, not an outbound CRM. Checkout's only outbound tool is Mailchimp. The support tool must integrate with a CRM for proactive outreach (for example SMB sellers stuck in onboarding needing documents).
- Charlie's principle: API-first, no vendor lock-in, no opinionated marketplace apps.

**Segmentation (Charlie's draft)**
- White glove: B2B enterprise, including platform operators.
- Non-white glove: includes direct SMB as a sub-segment. SMB is closer to the consumer experience (app, mobile, chat) but kept separate for business data rules and back-office segmentation.
- Consumer as third group. Framing is B2B vs B2C, not "merchant". Customers identified up front and routed.

**Cost to serve**
- Median support cost per ticket: $20-25 (ops partners' calculation).
- All-in estimate with back office and check automation: about $50 per customer per year.
- Earlier platform model: about $3-4 per platform seller for support plus checks, significant against low revenue per seller.
- Joe prefers a threshold as a percentage of net revenue, tolerating higher cost where SMBs adopt margin products (working capital, issuing). A full cost-to-serve model is needed. Charlie owns the support part.

**Team and 2027 goal**
- Fraser (EM), 5 experienced engineers, 1 graduate engineer, 1 graduate PM. Growth budget signed off, timing with Milan.
- 2027 goal: support infrastructure that onboards new product launches automatically, detected through the MCAP process, rather than manual assessment.

**Other**
- Platforms experience feels tacked onto the direct-merchant Dashboard and drives contacts. Improvements should target the end-to-end experience, not acquisition funnels.
- 2030 target: 368,000 sellers, about 120x current clients. Joe: small sellers generate similar contact levels to large ones.
- The 2023 SMB attempt (led by Umang, Charlie on the integration side) paused over operational burden and tight coupling. More viable now after three years of re-architecture.
