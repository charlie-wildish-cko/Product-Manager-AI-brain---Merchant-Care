## Context

Q4 roadmap readout for Platforms and OCS, planned backwards from outcomes. Charlie presented the operations platform segment. Transcript runs about 1h15m. Gemini transcribes Plain as "Plane".

## Key Points

**Headline goals**
- ISV platform scalability means onboarding at scale. Target: over 90% automation per check, and learn to say no quickly.
- ARC enablement: risk check automation, automated configuration and activation, a unified config-driven workflow. Decision: unlock the front book for all platform products in the US, UAE, EA and UK. EA scope to be restated as front book only.

**Capacity**
- Infrastructure capacity is 60-70% on top priorities. Engineering capacity came in lower than assumed.
- Interest and card payouts pushed to Q1, which Milan flagged as heavily stacked. New items: replacing savings accounts with Ray, capturing e-invoicing data. ISV onboarding discovery timeboxed to 4 weeks, or 2.
- Milan argued ISV onboarding and a full UAE IP rollout outrank European expansion.
- Risk-check automation tool: about 78 points of work against 50-55 capacity. Proposed cuts include some Ray controls (needs further discussion).
- Planning runway of 6-9 months. H1 objectives, dependencies and capacity locked by early November.

**Other tracks**
- IDV Q4: customisable KYC UI, new UK certification, identity hub for European rules, 90% automation, first-level automation for EA expansion. IDV data ingestion for Ray readiness is in scope.
- Configuration Q4: pre-dashboard support, product registry PoC, self-serve rails for MPGs and CyberSource, network token centralisation, configuration-as-a-service.
- Sanctions: decision to build an in-house screening engine led by Vasilev. Transaction screening moves to TRM Labs. Name screening stays with a vendor. Lack of payin and AFT screening is a high risk.

**Operations platform (Charlie)**
- Operational foundations are about 80% of this team's work, Ray readiness about 20%.
- Decision to move off Zendesk to a flexible, system-agnostic hub for support and operations. "No-regret" architecture changes are making systems pluggable. Reusable components (customer details up front) serve every ops team.
- Ray deliverable: Fin inside the Ray mobile app.
- Payment agent component gives support agents (Mauritius, Mexico) the same data customers get via Fin. Different data between agents and Fin was a past deflection blocker.
- A 2027 operations roadmap will be published with milestones around the Zendesk contract expiry. First migration is Zendesk to Plain for Care, kept generic for wider operations. New flows such as legal T&Cs require moving teams off Salesforce.
- Milan asked about Fin automation: Charlie said 40% at year end is on track. Platforms do not affect Care until they scale next year, Issuing is not a concern, and Ray is the main readiness risk (little volume this year, a Q1 launch could bring a lot).

## Insights

- Care's Plain migration is framed as step one of a company-wide operations platform. Other ops teams will onboard and expect shared components.
- Contact impact from Platforms onboarding automation and entity cleanup is a 2027 effect.
- The 40% Fin automation target for end 2026 was restated to senior stakeholders. Ray Q1 volume is the main contact risk.
- The Zendesk contract expiry is the forcing date for the 2027 roadmap.
