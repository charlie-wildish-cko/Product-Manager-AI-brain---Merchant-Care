## Context

Care planning discussion for Finalsite, an education SaaS platform going live in early August. Wider context: defining Charlie's approach for early-stage platform onboarding.

## Key Points

- Care is not involved during integration/sandbox phases. Risk team leads onboarding. Care's involvement starts once the platform is live and at volume.
- Goal: avoid reactive support for failed onboardings. Target: an API/data source that lets merchants look up onboarding incident details and get meaningful explanations rather than generic errors. Automated care workflow lookup to follow once product-side data is available.
- Feedback loop: Michael to maintain a running log of observations and issues from sandbox testing. Charlie confirmed he can use Claude to summarise it periodically.
- **Merchant insights tooling**: Charlie described per-merchant topical insights from support data (beyond label-level taxonomy — actual reasons). Michael confirmed this would be valuable for AMs/TAMs in quarterly business reviews.
- **Welcome pack**: two-format resource — (1) microsite for sales to customise and password-protect for specific merchants; (2) public filterable version on support site segmented by merchant type. Covering production go-live operations and relevant support content.
- A second integration running in parallel (existing merchant expanding from merchant-of-record to ISV model) — more advanced technical questions expected.

## Insights

- Michael confirms from prior experience that onboarding is where 95% of issues arise. Validates Care's focus on self-service onboarding support.
- Per-merchant insight reporting (top 5 issues over 12 months) is the specific use case Michael named for Reflex-type data — this is exactly the "accountability signal" the Reflex product should deliver to TAMs.
- MBway resurfaced as a notoriously poor APM for refunds (no decline message, short refund window, merchants retry without knowing why it failed).
- The two-format welcome pack aligns with the March 18 welcome pack session — the microsite variant adds a new ISV-specific distribution model.
