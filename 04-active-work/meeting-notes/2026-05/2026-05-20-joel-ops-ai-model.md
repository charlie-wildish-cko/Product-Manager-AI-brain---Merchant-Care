## Context

~58-minute planning session on the AI Ops organisational model, consumer launch model, and long-term AI stack for Care.

## Key Points

**AI Ops home and boundary**
- AI Ops to sit within Operational Excellence as a hub-and-spoke function. Engineering retains ownership of technical integrations, API enhancements, and deployments. AI Ops owns day-to-day performance and outcomes.

**Workforce model**
- Fin primarily removes L1 work. L2 is largely unaffected short-term (L2 escalation currently ~11%). L2 tasks are complex reconciliation and multi-source queries — AI unlikely to reduce this before 2028.
- Goal is internal reallocation of displaced L1 staff, not headcount reduction. Target roles: AI Ops, QA, Knowledge Management, Process roles, payments/payouts/risk ops onboarding.
- The skills required for AI Ops roles (proactive, consultative, strong quality judgment) do not match the displaced L1 profile — active planning required, not just job postings.

**Consumer launch model**
- Separate team from Mauritius B2B team. BPO proposed for initial launch.
- 60% Fin resolution rate set as external launch target (conservative).
- Phone support excluded from Q4 consumer scope.
- Requirements: Fin on consumer app, Zendesk processes configured from Fin escalation, all content written, agent SOPs written, CRM integration into Zendesk.

**AI governance — Monzo case study**
- Monzo over-indexed on resolution rate (GPT-4, claimed 700 agents saved). Disputes and hardship cases resolved incorrectly led to Consumer Duty exposure.
- Key takeaway: always maintain human escalation paths; never assume AI handles complex edge cases without review. Quality signals alongside resolution rate are non-negotiable.

**Long-term AI stack**
- Fin retained as primary AI agent through ~2027. 2028 is the vendor re-evaluation milestone.
- Sierra AI noted — requires 1M+ contacts/year. Checkout may qualify by 2028/2029.
- Charlie's buying principle: buy AI agent and channel layers; build everything else in-house (data, knowledge integrations, analytics).

## Insights

- The AI Ops / Engineering boundary (outcomes vs. integrations) is a clean model that warrants formalising in a RACI.
- L2 escalation at ~11% is below a 20% steady-state target — L2 complexity is already high and AI is unlikely to reduce L2 volume before 2028. Headcount planning should not assume AI displaces L2.
- The Monzo case study is the strongest argument in the industry for Fin QA automation and quality-gated resolution metrics.
- The 2028 vendor evaluation milestone aligns with the Zendesk replacement RFC timeline — these decisions are coupled.
