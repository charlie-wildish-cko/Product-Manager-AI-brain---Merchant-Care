## Context

AM perspective on Zendesk routing gaps and dashboard adoption — additional evidence for Charlie's cross-team communications proposal.

## Key Points

- Cross-team ticket routing via Zendesk lacks visibility for AMs. When Care routes to Risk or Disputes, AMs get no notification unless they manually check the ticket.
- Risk/Disputes sometimes reject referred cases after Care has already closed the ticket — merchants left confused with no follow-up.
- ~50% of all support contacts have no dashboard record — blocks authentication and automated support flows.
- Merchants don't proactively manage their own dashboard users. Some owners/CEOs never log in.
- Checkout managed user access on merchants' behalf last year — that stopped, creating confusion.
- Salesforce contact lists manually maintained by AMs — fragile and labour-intensive. Example: "Flow" upgrade campaign where a merchant (Group LDLC) missed urgency because the email looked like marketing.

**Decision**
- Single entry point principle confirmed: merchants should not need to re-raise or track multiple threads.
- Charlie to run marketing outreach in July targeting merchants with no dashboard records.
- Dashboard-record detection to be embedded into AI support flows (prompt users to create a record if missing).

**Richay's proposal**
- Dashboard-based consent checklist: merchant opts in to comms types → auto-updates Salesforce. Self-service model.

## Insights

- The 50% no-dashboard-record figure appears again (also in the Jun 10 Perrin session). This is becoming a baseline number for the authentication and self-service gap.
- AM bandwidth fills the gap between fragmented systems — but is finite and will not scale.
- The ticket routing visibility gap (AMs not notified when Care routes to another team) is a specific, solvable UX problem in Zendesk — Slack side conversation macros (discussed in the Jun 17 Dashboard support flows session) directly address this.
