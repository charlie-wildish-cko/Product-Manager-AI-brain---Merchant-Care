## Context

Working session to investigate a solution for scheme proof of payment requests — ~5–6% of total contact volume (~200 requests/month).

## Key Points

- Merchants request proof that a payment, refund, or card payout was processed successfully. Standard resolution: provide the ARN (Acquirer Reference Number) or RRN.
- However, merchants often specifically request the Visa or Mastercard scheme proof document — a formal document issued from the scheme portal.
- Scheme proof retrieval is entirely manual: agents log into Visa/Mastercard portals, search, and download. No API exists for either scheme — cannot be automated.
- Cost: Visa fee is low (less than ~$10). Mastercard substantially higher — minimum €25 in France/GB.
- Root cause of requests: issuing banks put refund/payout funds "in suspense" (closed accounts, suspicious activity) or cannot share transaction details due to regulation. Cardholders ask merchants for proof to present to their bank.
- Current agent workaround: screenshots of transaction logs — not official documents.
- Charlie proposed: official Checkout-branded proof of payment document accessible via the merchant Dashboard, eliminating reliance on scheme portals for the majority of cases.
- Current policy: absorb scheme costs as "cost of doing business." Becomes unsustainable at SMB scale — charging must be introduced at SMB launch.
- Omair is running an SFTP initiative with Visa for invoice data — potential pathway to programmatic access.

## Insights

- 5–6% of volume on a single issue type with no automation path. A Checkout-branded Dashboard proof document would eliminate the majority of these contacts and remove the scheme portal process entirely.
- The ARN/RRN data is already generated by Checkout and transmitted to schemes — surfacing it as a downloadable document is a relatively contained product change with high contact reduction impact.
- This must be in place before SMB launches — the cost absorption model breaks at SMB volumes.
- Omair Mirza (International Partnerships) is the key stakeholder for scheme API/programmatic access conversations.
