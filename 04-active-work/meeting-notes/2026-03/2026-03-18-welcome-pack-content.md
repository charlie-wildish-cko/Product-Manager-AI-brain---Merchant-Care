## Context

Working session to align on content structure for a "Payments Fundamentals" merchant welcome pack. Driven by the first ISV onboarding planned for June/July 2026 (US ISV launch).

## Key Points

- Proposed structure: (1) Getting started / working with Checkout; (2) Payment methods (card networks, local payment methods, regional snapshots); (3) Managing payments performance (lifecycle, pricing, AR, dashboard usage); (4) Protecting your business (compliance, fraud, authentication, IDV).
- Most content already exists — needs reshuffling and nuance additions, not net-new creation.
- Content update cadence agreed: maximum once per quarter, focused on new dashboard features and new payment methods.
- Two versions needed: ISV (sub-merchant functionality) and direct merchant.
- Gap identified: no good external materials explaining the dashboard and available reports. Charlie building a dashboard tutorial to link from the pack.
- Sales team (Tom et al.) to validate content before finalising.
- Target: version ready for first ISV onboarding June/July 2026.

## Insights

- Good onboarding reduces contacts. Merchants who understand payment fundamentals, the dashboard, and performance management generate fewer "how do I..." queries.
- The ISV-specific version is directly relevant to the US ISV launch — poor ISV onboarding creates L2 support burden on Checkout (as L2 for ISV platforms).
- The dashboard documentation gap is a contact risk: merchants who can't find this information will raise contacts instead.
- The dashboard tutorial Charlie is building could also feed into Fin knowledge base articles and the knowledge management workstream.
