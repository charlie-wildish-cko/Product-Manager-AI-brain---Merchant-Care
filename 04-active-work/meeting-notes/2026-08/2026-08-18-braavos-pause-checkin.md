**Date:** 2026-08-18

## Context

Short-notice call announcing that Braavos is paused. Decision taken by Guillaume and the E-team, announced by Meron Colbeci. Framed as a pause, not a stop.

## Key Points

- Reasoning as stated:
  1. 2027 planning showed the combined cost profile of the big projects (SMB, platforms, consumer) ahead of the revenue profile. Company targets close to 1bn revenue next year and revenue this year is soft versus plan, reducing confidence in carrying three big bets instead of two.
  2. Braavos is less adjacent to the core business and has a much longer revenue horizon than platforms and SMB.
  3. Regulation is the cost driver. Being UK-regulated requires a very robust compliance team and a very robust Care team. Meron contrasted this with a non-regulated crypto wallet, where there is more leeway and you can grow into it over time.
- The product was effectively built and about to launch internally.
- Reuse: engineering, product, design, marketing, ops prep and resource modelling carry forward to Ray, open banking (part of 2027) and a potential unregulated product. Consumer Duty work stays partly relevant through open banking.
- Remember Me and Flow focus is unchanged and, per Meron, becomes more important.
- Long-term company sequence unchanged: enterprise, platforms, SMB, consumer. Precedent cited: pausing SMB plus pausing crypto a few years ago is credited with reviving growth over the last 2-3 years, and SMB is now back on the agenda.
- Comms: Guillaume sends an all-employee email. Leaders told to cascade immediately using the context from the call.
- Bank relationships: multiple banks are at advanced stages, some presenting to internal committees. Agreed line is that this is purely a business decision with nothing to do with a regulator asking questions. Sean drafts messaging, Jenny and Meron review with legal.
- Umang has compiled a list of all external dependencies (banking partnerships, vendors) and will run per-team 1:1s for a graceful rather than abrupt pause.

## Insights

- Primary-source confirmation of the pause and the reasoning: cost of compliance and Care against revenue timing, soft 2026 revenue, two big bets not three.
- Direct read-across for Care: the cost of standing up a robust regulated Care function was named at exec level as a reason a product was paused. Regulated consumer Care overhead is now a business-case input, not a downstream detail. Ray's non-regulated status is exactly the "grow into it over time" leeway Braavos lacked.
- Consumer is deferred, not abandoned. Care's consumer capability planning should be preserved, with the trigger being platforms and SMB scale.
- Consumer Duty analysis retains value through open banking in 2027. Do not archive it as dead.
- Ray is the named inheritor of Braavos platform work, which supports treating Ray Care scoping as the live consumer workstream.
