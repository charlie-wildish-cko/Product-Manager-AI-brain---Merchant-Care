## Context

Extended product leadership session on 2027 planning ahead of the September board review, plus the MCAP process move onto Hive.

## Key Points

**2027 planning**
- Submissions are in finance review. Board review is early September.
- The consumer financial services effort is paused for 2027, saving approximately $20M in opex and reducing strain on operations and compliance. Driven by projected cost structures plus softer recent revenue performance threatening profitability.
- 2027 revenue target close to $1 billion, with aggressive profitability and free cash flow targets.
- 30 incremental headcount requested across teams, pending final approval and September board approval.
- FX incremental investment being evaluated as a future revenue driver after strong growth this year. No large incremental headcount requested there.
- P&L leads recalibrated revenue forecasts with deliberate risk haircuts as buffers. Most 2027 revenue depends on 2026 bookings. H2 2027 is driven by US platforms.
- Concern raised about a disconnect between P&L revenue projections and the capacity of stakeholder and dependency teams: front-loading H1 risks missed targets or team burnout.
- Unbudgeted last-minute revenue requests force trade-offs and descoping because there is no contingency.
- 2026 tactics for softer end-of-year revenue: accelerate the commercial pipeline, pursue short-term revenue in IIA and FTP, manual disputes, protect initiatives like US Cash App, drive ARR via platforms and sales management.

**MCAP on Hive**
- MCAP (Major Change Approval Process) on the Hive platform covers beta launch readiness, scoping, and cross-functional approvals. Pilot underway, including Max's standalone network tokens.
- Patrick Kohler raised friction and efficiency concerns. Meron acknowledged the UX is broken initially but asked for a leap of faith while documentation and cross-functional responsiveness are fixed, with a commitment to revisit if it fails.

**Team and events**
- Peakon engagement scores up 2 points for the product team, while Checkout overall dipped slightly. Improvements in manager support, strategy, and compensation. The team is no longer last in the company.
- Thrive conference: 13 days away, West Coast near Los Angeles, US-market focus, product spotlights on Platforms, US Issuing, and the MAL launch. 70% of attendees are new prospects.

Prioritise APMs at Level 3/4 with real career paths over direct senior replacements. Cited wins: a graduate producing 200+ Figma screens and synthesising 90+ RFPs, and an internal data-to-APM transition.
- Calendar: Thrive and board meeting in September, Q3 roadmap publication 30 September, exec and CLT summits, Money20/20, product summits, HBR, external 2027 roadmap publication mid-December.

**Decisions**
- Consumer financial services effort paused for 2027, saving ~$20M opex.
- Hive is mandatory for MCAP on all cross-functional initiatives from mid-September. All pillars must upload initiatives and give feedback within two weeks.
- Project Ray remains on track: friends-and-family in five weeks (approximately early October 2026), general availability in Q1 2027.
