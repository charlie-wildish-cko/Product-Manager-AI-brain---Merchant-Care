## Context

~80-minute workshop to develop a strategic vision for Care through 2030, timed to meet a 2027 planning submission deadline. Followed by a planned second session to build the sequenced roadmap.

## Key Points

**Three strategic goals**
1. Eliminate contacts where possible (no/minimal contact support).
2. Route remaining contacts through AI — fast, high quality.
3. Achieve a sustainable cost structure.

**Volume forcing function**
- Volume is manageable through 2026–early 2027. From 2027+, consumer (B2C digital wallet) volume creates major scale pressure — projected 1–2M support contacts/year.
- Support cost as % of Net Revenue (NR) is the financial framing. B2C benchmark: under 5% NR. Current blended rate (including AM/TAM volume of ~40,000 tickets/year) could reach ~2.5% NR. Oliver and Jenny to validate.

**Agentic support stack (technology model)**
- Continuous, self-improving cycle: receive query → AI/human routing → data/knowledge layer → resolution → insights/prevention → feeds back to product.
- 80% AI automation target for B2B; higher for B2C.
- Four principles: solve root causes; majority through AI; Agent Consultant for human-in-the-loop; Reflex for automated insights; knowledge embedded in data (payment ID query returns pre-annotated field explanations — same concept as the Apr 17 payments data explanations work).

**Zendesk replacement**
- Charlie stated explicitly: "Definitely not having Zendesk in my opinion... it would not enable most of these capabilities to work very well."
- Preferred model: flexible, vendor-agnostic infrastructure.

**Taxonomy and data gaps**
- Joel: most functions lack a unified taxonomy, making it hard to structure solutions. The 10–20% of tickets not solved by AI require engaging teams who hold gatekept data.
- Inter-team silos will persist into consumer support unless addressed structurally from day one of consumer team hiring.

**AI Ops function**
- Proposed: dedicated AI Operations function within Operational Excellence, hub-and-spoke with dotted lines to each operational team.
- Key roles: AI Operations Lead, Knowledge Manager (AI), Support Automation Specialist, Conversation Designer.
- "1% slip at scale" requires daily performance monitoring.
- When Fin goes live, it may immediately deflect 40–50% of volume, leaving the current team underutilised. Spare capacity redeploys to: QA on Fin resolutions, SOP writing, content writing.
- Upskill vs. hire for AI Ops roles: unresolved. Joel notes these feel like L2-type skills and represent career progression.

## Insights

- Zendesk replacement is now an explicit strategic position — this accelerates and validates the Zendesk platform decision RFC.
- Reflex and Agent Consultant are both named components in the 2030 technology cycle. Reflex surfaces clusters, drives root cause resolution, feeds agentic fix-loops to engineering.
- Consumer volume (1–2M contacts/year) is the primary strategic forcing function. The regulatory/compliance tail (Consumer Duty, vulnerable customers) is the hardest part.
- "Embedded knowledge in data" is the architectural concept that runs through Fin Procedures, the payments data explanations work, and the Reflex data product.
- AI Ops as a dedicated function is the single largest org/people decision in the strategy. 2027 proposed start; upskill vs. hire unresolved.
- Phone support excluded from channel strategy for B2C digital wallet — high digital literacy demographic, everything routes through the app (note: this may conflict with Consumer Duty regulatory requirements discussed elsewhere).
