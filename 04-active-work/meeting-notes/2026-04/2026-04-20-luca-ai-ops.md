## Context

Cross-functional alignment. Charlie walked Luca through a framework for AI-driven operational transformation, using Care as the worked example but framing it as a blueprint applicable across all ops functions (underwriting, fraud, disputes, AML/KYC).

## Key Points

**Charlie's three AI model types**
1. Assistive: optional tools (current state for most functions).
2. Augmentative: AI completes a defined task end-to-end, escalates exceptions — the target ops model. E.g. 80% of disputes or AML due diligence automated.
3. Autonomous: AI-driven with minimal human involvement — closest existing example is KYC/KYB onboarding.

**Operational model changes driven by AI**
- Specialisation replaces volume management.
- New skills focus on optimisation, not task execution.
- L1/L2/L3 structure compresses — L2 becomes a sharper specialist tier.
- Metrics shift: from human time management to AI performance (solve rate, escalation quality, false positive rates, auditability).
- Charlie: "Sometimes we've actually exhausted tickets for a day" — Care is already hitting early signs of this transition.

**AI team structure**
- Agreed: domain-embedded AI teams, not centralised. Luca: "If it was a centralised function you'd be splitting the knowledge too much."
- New roles needed: AI Ops Owner (accountable for AI effectiveness), Knowledge person (converts human SOPs to AI SOPs), Analyst/AI QA (monitors decision quality), Escalation specialist (complex cases + tunes frontline AI).

**Centralised data capture as the foundational blocker**
- Luca flagged: much of Risk Ops receives merchant information via email with no centralised storage. This is the same structural problem as Care's authentication-over-email blocker.
- Both converged: "We're thinking about individual use cases, but what we're discussing are the foundations to unlock all the use cases around it." Ownership of building this layer unresolved — "Milan keeps getting asked this question."

## Insights

- Care is being explicitly positioned as the blueprint for the broader ops AI transformation across Checkout. This raises the stakes for the Care product strategy document.
- Centralised merchant data capture is the prerequisite for all AI use cases — not just Care's Fin authentication problem.
- Ownership of AI outputs is unresolved across the business — a governance gap the Care product model will need to address.
- The metrics evolution (human time → AI performance) aligns with the Care 2030 operating model discussion.
