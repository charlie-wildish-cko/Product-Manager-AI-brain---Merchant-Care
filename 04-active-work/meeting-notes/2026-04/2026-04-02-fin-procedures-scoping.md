## Context

Planning session for Fin Procedures work in Q2, with assistance from an Intercom ProServ workshop. Focus: prioritise high-impact Procedures that Fin can resolve end-to-end.

## Key Points

- Strategy: select a small high-impact focus area (specific payin/payout reasons) that covers 50–60% of total volume. Prioritise issues Fin can resolve end-to-end — these are the only ones that reduce cost.
- Taxonomy-based opportunity sizing: automation candidates identified using filters for cost, feasibility, and tickets that didn't go to a dependent team. Total opportunity: 36.9% (including IM/internal) to 43% (addressable tickets only).
- LLM work ongoing: classifying agent actions (giving information, gathering data, fixing, escalating) and clustering merchant asks to refine the bottom-up approach.
- Key exclusion: APMs — scenarios requiring manual external portal intervention must be excluded to avoid overstating automation potential. "Product requirements" for acquiring data per domain are being mapped before Procedures are built.
- Workshop: Intercom ProServ to help design the first Procedure and create a template for future ones. SOW signature needed before workshop.

## Insights

- The 36.9–43% automation opportunity is the most precise bottom-up estimate of Fin's addressable volume at this point. It's grounded in the new taxonomy.
- Excluding APMs from automation estimates is important — they inflate the apparent opportunity but can't be delivered. The Procedures roadmap must be honest about this ceiling.
- Mapping agent data sources before building Procedures is the right sequencing — building a Procedure without the underlying data access creates a dead-end.
