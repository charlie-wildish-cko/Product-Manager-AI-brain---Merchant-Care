**Date:** 2026-02-13

## Context

Working session with PMO to review the product launch readiness framework and align on how Care/Ops should be embedded in it. Charlie also walked Orrin through his contact volume forecasting model.

## Key Points

**Product approval framework**
- The existing 19-page launch readiness template is used inconsistently — effectively only triggered when PMO is involved or a PM has been non-compliant before.
- Operations is absent from Toll Gate Zero (ideation/sign-off). Currently only partially covered via compliance. Charlie flagged this as a structural gap — Care has no formal input before execution planning begins.
- Charlie's three care-readiness requirements for any product launch: (1) what is the customer feedback loop and success metric? (2) has relevant knowledge and troubleshooting guidance been provided? (3) is there a data source for technical performance diagnostics? These should be answered at Toll Gate One or beta.
- Proposed pull structure: stakeholders self-subscribe to roadmap items rather than waiting for product teams to push updates. Legal, compliance, etc. opt in to roadmap feeds.
- AI opportunity: auto-generate stakeholder-specific artifacts from a single PRD on Confluence status change (e.g. "Toll Gate Zero Ready").

**Contact volume forecasting model**
- By end of 2027: projected 10x volume increase from current, driven by Platforms and Consumer.
- By 2028: forecasts reach 2–3 million contacts/year.
- B2B breakdown: ~70% payment queries (50%) and settlement/"where's my money" queries (20%). These don't go away — they scale with every new segment.
- Tier 3/4 merchants cost ~2x more per ticket than Tier 1 and generate exponentially more contacts per transaction. A Tier 3/4 merchant costs ~$600/year to support; at 100k Platform sub-merchants that becomes a significant cost line.
- Fin modelled as the primary cost lever. Even conservative Fin deployment creates ~50% cost reduction vs "do nothing."
- Consumer contacts will represent >50% of volume by 2030, with enterprise/platforms holding most revenue — AI-first is existential for consumer, not optional.

## Insights

- Operations has no formal seat at product Toll Gate Zero — new products are designed without a mandatory Care sign-off. This is a governance gap that scales badly as the product portfolio grows.
- The forecasting model is being socialised across the business to force product teams to internalise the cost implications of their decisions. Care is a cost centre that scales with every product choice other teams make.
- Fin is not a nice-to-have in the model — it is the primary mechanism for managing cost at scale. Removing it from the scenario roughly doubles projected headcount cost.
