## Merchant Profile

Large Tier 1 OTA with multiple entities, multiple currencies, high volume. ~40-person payments department. Has its own data sets and APIs. Uses the Checkout dashboard primarily for reporting, disputes, and — recently for the first time — entity onboarding. Payments team is rated highly by AM; finance team is lower sophistication and drives disproportionate ticket volume. Rated 10/10 (payments) vs 6–7/10 (finance) by Konstantinos.

---

## Support Channels Used

- Email (primary for finance-generated queries)
- Dashboard (reporting, disputes, limited operational use)

---

## Primary Support Needs and Pain Points

**OTA adjustment and reconciliation queries**
The core issue is Etraveli's business model: refunds and disputes arise 8–12 months after the original payment (due to the nature of travel bookings). Finance team contacts support when they see an adjustment for a transaction that is months old, asking for details.
> "Mostly has to do with adjustments… you may have a refund or a dispute after eight or one year… they see an adjustment happened eight months back, can you please give me more information about the details?"

The care team cannot process these adjustments directly — all require manual Treasury involvement.
> "The care team can't do the adjustment for example and it can't be automated at the moment. So everything has to manually… everything has to go to Treasury." — Charlie

**Proof of payment / proof of adjustment documents**
Standard query type. Requires manual retrieval and sending.

**Performance queries**
Payments team (sophisticated) asks non-trivial questions about AR anomalies. AM pays close attention to these.
> "When they say something, I always pay attention and I know that somebody should jump into it because it's an issue."

---

## Key Insights

- Etraveli's ticket volume is driven by the finance team, not the payments team. Finance staff have low payments knowledge and use email as the default channel even for queries the dashboard could answer.
- Long-tail OTA adjustment queries (8–12 month lag) are structurally difficult to automate — the care team lacks the access and permissions to resolve them directly, and Treasury is required.
- Dashboard adoption is uneven within a single large merchant. Making the dashboard better would incentivise migration from email, but historically this has not been prioritised.
> "There's a case of making the dashboard better so that people want to use it and historically we haven't done that." — Charlie
- Forcing merchants off email to the dashboard creates friction and resistance. A blended/nudge model is needed rather than a hard channel switch.
