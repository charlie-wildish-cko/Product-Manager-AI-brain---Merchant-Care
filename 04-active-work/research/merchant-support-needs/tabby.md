## Merchant Profile

One of the largest Tier 1 merchants in the MENA portfolio. BNPL provider. Active dashboard users (~70–80 users logging in per week). Considered sophisticated by AM — has dedicated people monitoring payments data. Also processes with Mada in KSA via TPA (same structure as Invygo). Tends to contact the AM team before raising formal tickets.

---

## Support Channels Used

- AM direct contact (first point of call)
- Email / tickets (formal channel, secondary)
- Dashboard (active users)

---

## Primary Support Needs and Pain Points

**Authorization failures, payment confirmation, proof of payment**
~100 tickets in the last 3–6 months across three buckets: authorization failures, payment/refund confirmation, and proof of payment. Charlie assessed the root cause as likely the same as Invygo — Mada/TPA processing in KSA.
> "If they're processing with Mada, which they probably are, it's probably a similar root cause — just the nuances of Mada and having why they have a 30-day refund thing."

**AM-first contact pattern**
Tabby reaches out to Gabriele before raising formal care tickets, meaning some queries are resolved off-ticket and do not appear in care data.

---

## Key Insights

- Tabby is sophisticated and dashboard-active, but still generates ~100 tickets per quarter from a small set of structural issues — primarily TPA-related.
- The AM-first contact pattern suggests Tabby has more confidence in the AM channel than formal support, and that the real contact volume is higher than tickets suggest.
- If Mada/TPA is confirmed as the root cause, the fix is the same as for Invygo — addressing the TPA refund process, not the merchant's behaviour.
- Gabriele's plan to "guide Tabby toward self-serve" is unlikely to resolve the core issue if the underlying problem is a TPA process limitation.
