## Primary Support Needs and Pain Points

**Proof of payout documents**
Tango's primary support need is a scheme-issued proof of payout document — equivalent to a remittance advice for payouts. This is not the same as an ARN and must be retrieved manually from the scheme portal. Each document incurs a per-document fee (~$10). No API exists to automate retrieval.
> "They need a proof of payout because they are doing payouts."
> "They insisted on the proof of payout document and even agreed to pay the associated fee."

**Pending payout status queries**
Standard status check queries for payouts in progress.

## Key Insights

- Proof of payout is a recurring, manual, fee-incurring process with no automation path. Scheme portal access and per-document fees make this structurally expensive to scale.
- Tango's direct-to-AM contact pattern means its real support volume does not appear in care metrics.
- Working day mismatch (Sunday–Thursday) means Tango's queries arrive on days when UK/EU support teams may be less available, driving escalations to the AM.
- Low ticket count (63 in 6 months) reflects AM absorption, not low need.
