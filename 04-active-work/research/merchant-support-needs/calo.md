## Primary Support Needs and Pain Points

**Mada/TPA refund failures**
Heavy volume of TPA-related issues. Mada refunds frequently fail — even before the 30-day window — requiring manual processing. Checkout agents manually collect spreadsheets and send to TPAs up to 3× per week.
> "A high batch of refunds actually doesn't work and needs to be done manually... even before the 30 days, even like after 10, 15 days."
> "Our agents had to collect a spreadsheet and send a spreadsheet every three times a week. Like it's stupid. In 2026 we have to do those."

**TPA ownership is contested but commercially necessary**
Merchants in KSA/GCC never interact directly with the acquiring bank — Checkout holds that relationship entirely. Checkout must act as intermediary to protect the NR relationship.
> "In a lot of these cases so we can handle the full relationship we don't even have this kind of relation like this connection between the merchant and the bank so they can handle it. We have to be there to solve this for them."

**Payment status mismatches during outages**
When NPG has an outage, Checkout reports a decline but NPG recaptures the payment — leaving conflicting statuses across systems. Triggers care queries about whether a payment has been taken.

**Meta-related transaction issues**
Specific issues relating to Meta transactions referenced but not detailed.

**AM as informal first-line support**
Ahmad Jabr receives approximately the same volume of Calo queries as the formal support team — a sign of how broken the formal channel feels for this merchant's complex TPA issues.

## Key Insights

- TPA issues represent ~25% of all annual support tickets across the portfolio — Calo is one of the clearest examples. The manual spreadsheet process for Mada refunds is unsustainable and a strong automation candidate, but blocked by TPA technology limitations (no API to receive batches).
- Ahmad's recommendation: automate everything except TPA first. Build local knowledge of TPA-related issues with AM teams before attempting automation.
- The AM absorbing formal support queries signals the care channel isn't working for complex issues. Calo's primary support need isn't self-serve access — it's faster, more capable resolution of structurally hard problems.
- Calo is a strategic Tier 2 (100% SOW, high growth trajectory) — it should receive Enterprise-level support treatment even though its tier classification doesn't currently reflect that.
- The new NPG licence in Saudi Arabia may resolve the Mada/SAB refund issues. Status at time of meeting: to be confirmed.
