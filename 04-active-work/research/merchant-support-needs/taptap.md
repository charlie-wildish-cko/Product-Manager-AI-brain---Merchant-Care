## Merchant Profile

Remittance company (peer-to-peer transfers). Second largest merchant by TPV after Clara. One of the biggest ticket generators after Kareem. Processes payins via Checkout; payouts via local African banking partners. Large distributed front-line support team (18 pages of dashboard users). 24/7 multi-region coverage. Expanding into TPA markets (Qatar, Bahrain, KSA — Q3/Q4 2026).

## Support Channels Used

- Email (primary, despite dashboard access)
- Dashboard (access exists but underutilised)

---

## Primary Support Needs and Pain Points

**Payment confirmation and status queries (self-resolving)**
Majority of TapTap's tickets are payment status queries submitted via email. Many resolve before agents action them — customers contact Checkout too early.
> "The majority of Tap Tap's support tickets are sent via email, often regarding charges or payment statuses that resolve themselves by the time an agent reviews them."
> "A lot of basically the scenarios are charges not being taken or charges being taken but looks like the status is wrong but by the time they get back to it the status is updated."

Characterised as "probably highly solvable volume" by Charlie.

**Payout status queries**
TapTap's support teams ask about payout status, but Checkout only sees the payin leg of the transaction. Checkout cannot trace the payout — leaving agents unable to give complete answers.

**Low dashboard proficiency**
TapTap has widespread dashboard access but front-line support staff lack the knowledge to use it for support workflows. Queries come by email not because access is missing but because staff don't know what to look for.
> "I don't know how to use it. I'm just going to send an email."

**Knowledge fragmentation**
Payments domain knowledge sits with TapTap's payments specialists but is not shared with the distributed front-line customer support teams who raise care tickets.

---

## Key Insights

- TapTap is a significant opportunity for deflection: the dominant query type (payment status) is dashboard-visible and self-resolvable with minimal knowledge. The blocker is education, not access.
- Dashboard education or guided workflows specifically for "check payment status" and "understand a response code" would address the core query type.
- The payout traceability gap is a product limitation — Checkout cannot answer payout queries for a remittance business processing payouts outside Checkout's network.
- TapTap's GCC expansion (Qatar, Bahrain, KSA Q3/Q4) will introduce Mada/TPA constraints — specifically Mastercard refund-after-30-day rules. Expectations need to be set before the expansion.
- High email volume despite dashboard access suggests the Fin AI agent (email channel) could be effective here even before dashboard education is addressed.
