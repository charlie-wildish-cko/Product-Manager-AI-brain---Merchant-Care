# Internal vs External Support Content: Gap Analysis

## Method and caveats

Compared the internal Care Agent SOP KB (`01-knowledge-base/Support content/checkout-support-site-internal/Support articles/`, 528 articles, ~30 top-level categories) against the public support site (`01-knowledge-base/Support content/checkout-support-site-main/Support articles/`, 908 articles, 15 top-level categories), both pulled from `cko-web` (staging) on 2026-08-10.

Approach: enumerated internal articles category by category, triaged titles for merchant self-serve relevance (excluding internal-process content by design), then spot-checked candidates against external content using targeted `grep`/`find` keyword searches across the external tree rather than reading all 908 external articles. A topical match (even worded differently) was treated as "covered" and excluded.

**Caveats**: This is a sampled review, not an exhaustive line-by-line audit.
- `Settlement, Invoices & Fees` (~90 internal articles, including a MENA-mirror substructure) was triaged by title and spot-checked; not every leaf article was read in full.
- `Payments In` (~60 articles, heavily MENA/scheme-response-code/internal-ops flavoured) and `Card Processing` scheme-decline articles were sampled rather than read exhaustively — most of this category is internal (acquirer-side investigation, internal escalation) by nature.
- `Integration` and `Card Issuing` were reviewed at the title/structure level with targeted external keyword checks per subfolder, not full-text reads of every article.
- Categories judged to be internal-process-only by design (see closing section) were scanned for titles only, not read in full, since their scope excludes them from this exercise regardless.

## What wasn't fully covered / excluded categories

- **Settlement, Invoices & Fees** and **Payments In** are the two largest internal categories (~90 and ~60 articles respectively) and were triaged, not exhaustively read; both contain large MENA-region mirror subtrees that were spot-checked rather than reviewed article-by-article. There may be additional gap candidates in unreviewed corners of these two categories.
- **Fraud Detection** and **Issuing (SDK/API)** external content is dense and well-matched to most internal equivalents — treated as largely covered, but only sampled via keyword search rather than full comparison.
- The following categories were confirmed (or are near-certain) to be internal-process content with no legitimate external equivalent by design, and were excluded outright rather than forced into the candidate list: **Case Management, Agent Toolkit, Announcements, Knowledge Champions, MCR, Non-merchant Requests, Collections** (internal collections-process handling for unmanaged merchants), and the bulk of **General Inquiry** and **Instant Messenger**. These were scanned at the title level only.
- **Terminations** and **Vault** were reviewed but yielded no strong candidates — Terminations content is almost entirely about internal reactivation/fund-release procedures tied to internal case handling, and Vault's two articles are thin internal L2 reference notes.
- **Dashboard**, **Platforms**, **Gateway**, **APMs**, **Card Processing** (beyond billing descriptor/scheme-decline-index) and **Acceptance Rate** were reviewed at title level with spot-check searches; most topics here already have a reasonably close external equivalent (e.g. Dashboard user permissions, processing channel requests, Gateway product overview, APM FAQs), so they didn't surface strong gap candidates in this pass but were not exhaustively verified line-by-line.
