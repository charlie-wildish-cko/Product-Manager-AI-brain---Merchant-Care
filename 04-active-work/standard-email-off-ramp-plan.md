# Plan:  Standard segment to use Dashboard for support

**Audience**: COO, CPO  
**Owner**: Charlie Wildish  
**Date**: February 2026  


## Intro

We will deprecate email support for Standard-tier merchants and direct them to Fin and the Dashboard. Goal: **increase Fin's involvement rate** and overall resolution rate.

We did this for Tier 4 in August 2025: comms plan, Commercial AM awareness, Zendesk rule that closes emails and points to Dashboard.

Standard makes up ~36% of annual contact volume. Current Fin involvement rate is 50%; Fin resolution rate = 70%. This shift has potential to shift Fin's overall resolution rate up by 10-15% to 20-25% overall.

**Merchant comms**: Template — `20250709 - Tier 4 Merchant Care changes`. Draft — `standard-email-off-ramp-merchant-comms.md`


## Success criteria/monitoring

- 100% Emails from Standard merchants (excluding Terminated merchants) are being closed by the rule
- Increase in Fin overall resolution rate by 10-15%
- Maintain support CSAT levels post switch (80%+)
- No. AM escalations about this change
