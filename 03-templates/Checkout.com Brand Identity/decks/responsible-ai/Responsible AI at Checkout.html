The guardrails

Four habits keep
our use of AI compliant.

Speed is only an advantage if it's safe. These apply every single time you open an AI tool.

LIFT
06

Guardrail 01

Protect sensitive data and PII

Never input confidential company data, proprietary source code, or PII into public AI models.

Regulated data

Stays controlled

Technical, verification, cardholder, and transaction data remains strictly controlled under GDPR and PCI-DSS.

Corporate secrets

Stay internal

Keep unreleased strategy, internal financials, and security or infrastructure details out of external chatbots.

PII

Never in a prompt

Anything that can identify, contact, or locate an individual — alone or combined. It never goes into a prompt.

LIFT
07

Guardrail 02

Maintain human accountability

Fact-check & verify

AI hallucinates. Verify technical answers, legal interpretations, and data analysis against official Checkout documentation.

Run security reviews

AI-assisted code must clear standard peer review and security testing.

Own the decision

100% of automated risk flags, closures, and reports need a clear path to human validation. Humans own the final call.

Apply judgement

Empathy, nuance, and regulatory accountability can't be automated. You remain responsible for the final output.

LIFT
08

Guardrail 03

Guard against bias

Used well, AI removes unconscious bias. Used carelessly, it amplifies it — so human oversight stays on.

Eliminate bias

Objective by design

Checkout leverages AI to remove unconscious bias in areas like talent acquisition — keeping assessments focused on objective criteria.

Review every output

Proactively check

When AI analyses data, drafts communications, or evaluates metrics, review the output to ensure no bias was introduced or amplified.

LIFT
09

Guardrail 04

Use approved tools only

Use the specific AI applications and enterprise tiers formally vetted by IT and Security.

Enterprise tiers

Stricter by default

Enterprise versions carry tighter data-privacy agreements that stop your inputs from training public models.

The official list

Check before you start

We continually trial new models. Confirm a tool is on the approved list — Confluence or the IT portal — before you use it.

A real-life lesson

When AI replaces judgement

01 · The bold bet

Cut to scale

Klarna replaced 700 customer-service agents with AI to cut cost and absorb volume.

02 · The friction

Trust dropped

Routine response times improved — but satisfaction collapsed on complex billing and fraud disputes.

03 · The empathy gap

Edge cases failed

The AI lacked empathy and trapped distressed customers in frustrating resolution loops.

04 · The reversal

Hybrid wins

Klarna pivoted: AI for efficiency, trained humans for empathy and complex problem-solving.

Key lessons

How we get the balance right

Automate

Automate the routine

Let AI do the heavy lifting on initial data gathering, standard tracking, and routine risk triage.

Escalate the complex

Multi-step disputes, fraud investigations, and policy exceptions go to a human analyst — immediately.

Empathy is key

Monitor the outputs
