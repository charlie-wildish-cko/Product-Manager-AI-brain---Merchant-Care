Governance

Every model follows the same lifecycle

1

Propose

Define the use case, data, and the decision the model will influence.

2

Review

Risk, fairness, privacy, and security assessed by an independent panel.

3

Approve

A named owner signs off and accepts ongoing accountability.

4

Monitor

Performance and drift tracked continuously against agreed thresholds.

5

Retire

Models are retrained or retired before they degrade — never left to drift.

09

What this means for operations

Your role keeps the system honest

Flag what looks off

You see the edge cases models miss. A surprising decline or odd pattern is worth raising — that signal is how we catch drift early.

Keep the human in the loop

On high-impact actions, the model recommends and you decide. Override when your judgement says so, and note why.

Document the decision

If you can't explain why an outcome happened, escalate to the model owner. Every decision should be traceable.
