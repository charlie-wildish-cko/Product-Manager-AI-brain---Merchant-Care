The big picture

Incident management is more than fixing the issue

Any employee must identify and immediately log an incident.

The Operations Center (OC) is the initial point of contact for logging.

OC sets initial severity and ensures correct categorisation.

The process spans severity, investigation, documentation, remediation and closure.

Roles & responsibilities

Know who does what

Any employee
Identify and log the incident immediately.
Operations Center (OC)
Log, set severity, categorise and create the PagerDuty incident.
Incident Owner
Lead investigation and resolution; enrich the record in Riskonnect.
Legal
Regulatory advice; included in all Sev 1 and Sev 2 incidents.
ERM (2LOD) & Incident Commander
Oversight and final closure of the incident.

Notify the OC in their public Slack channel to log an incident.

Some tech teams have alerts that auto-create incidents in PagerDuty.

PagerDuty creates the incident and an Incident Response Slack channel.

Immediate priority is containment to prevent further impact.

“Resolved” does not mean “Closed.”

OC updates PagerDuty to Resolved once the immediate issue is fixed; PagerDuty auto-updates Riskonnect to Resolved (not closed). Final closure is a separate step performed later.

The most common misunderstanding — watch for it.
Documentation

The Incident Owner enriches the record in Riskonnect

Complete all required fields across Information, Impacts, and Financial Loss / Recoverability.

Attach any files related to the incident.

Link the Post Incident Review document in the relevant field.

Data enrichment continues after active firefighting ends.

to complete and upload

PIRs are mandatory for Sev 2 and above

Any incident of Severity 2 or higher requires a Post Incident Review.

The PIR must be completed within 2 weeks of severity classification.

The Incident Owner uploads it to Riskonnect.

Missing or late PIRs are a key compliance gap.

Betterment

Turn learnings into Incident Actions

Draft → Ready for Review → Active · OC / 2LOD only

Incident Actions capture follow-up improvement (betterment) work.

The Incident Owner creates them in “Draft” or “Ready for Review”.

Only 2LOD (ERM) and OC can set an Incident Action to “Active”.

Example: after a human-error incident, draft revised SOPs to close the gap.

Closing out

Remediation and final closure

For Sev 1 and Sev 2, Legal and Regulatory Compliance must assess.

Incident Owner finalises Riskonnect data and sets “Ready for Review”.

Incident Commander (OC / ERM) reviews and performs final closure.

Can close once all actions are created and active — even if execution is ongoing.

Watch out

Avoid these common mistakes

×

Assuming “Resolved” means the incident is closed.

×

Forgetting PIRs are mandatory for Sev 2+ within 2 weeks.

×

Treating Incident Actions as optional.

×

Forgetting only OC and 2LOD can activate Incident Actions.

In summary

Remember the flow

Log fast, contain quickly, document properly.

Create the right follow-up Incident Actions.

Do not confuse operational resolution with formal closure.
