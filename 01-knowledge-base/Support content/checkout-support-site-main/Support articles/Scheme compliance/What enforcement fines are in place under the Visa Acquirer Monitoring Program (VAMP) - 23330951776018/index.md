The following table lists the enforcement fines in place under the Visa Acquirer Monitoring Program (VAMP):

<td><strong>Effective date</strong></td>
<td><strong>Acquirer breaches above standard ratio</strong></td>
<td><strong>Merchant breaches excessive ratio</strong></td>
<td><strong>Acquirer breaches excessive ratio and merchant ratio is &gt;=0.5%</strong></td>
<td><strong>Merchant breaches excessive enumeration ratio</strong></td>

<tr>

<td>Not applicable</td>

<td>8 USD per dispute or transaction reported as fraud</td>

<tr>

<td>4 USD per dispute or transaction reported as fraud</td>
<td>8 USD per dispute or transaction reported as fraud</td>
<td>8 USD per dispute or transaction reported as fraud</td>
<td>8 USD per dispute or transaction reported as fraud</td>
</tr>

In the AP and Europe regions, VAMP fees do not apply where a chargeback also incurs an unsecured dispute fee. Visa applies unsecured dispute fees to fraud chargebacks where the merchant did not authenticate the disputed transaction.

**Note:** Visa made a significant update to how they calculate the program ratios, with corresponding increases to the thresholds at which these ratios result in scheme fees. 

For more information, see [Update to the Visa Acquirer Monitoring Program (VAMP)](https://support.checkout.com/hc/en-us/articles/26990138066450).
