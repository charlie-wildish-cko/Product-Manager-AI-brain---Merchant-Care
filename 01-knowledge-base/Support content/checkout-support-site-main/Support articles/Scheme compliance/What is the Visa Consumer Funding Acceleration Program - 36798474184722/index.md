The Visa Consumer Funding Acceleration Program (CFAP) is a new Europe interchange fee program that takes effect from **October 24, 2026**.  
  
This program offers capped interchange fees on consumer debit and prepaid Account Funding Transactions (AFTs) for the following funds transfer types:

- Account-to-account transactions
- P2P Money Transfers
- Prepaid Card Load
- Wallet Transfer
- Funds Transfer

**Note:** From October 1, Visa will also introduce a new Consumer Funding Fee of 0.01 EUR in the same jurisdictions and for the same use cases as the CFAP.

## Registration process

If you’re interested in registering under CFAP, please contact your Account Manager or raise a support request in our Dashboard. We’ll work with you to arrange registration with Visa.

**If you already have specific rates based on your Merchant Verification Value (MVV) registration program, these programs remain in place.** You may wish to remain under your existing program, or migrate to CFAP and withdraw from your existing program. Note that CFAP has different rates and may apply to different jurisdictions than the existing MVV-based program.

You’ll also need to ensure your website and checkout meets the following CFAP requirements:

- The Visa Direct Service is presented wherever Money Movement Networks are offered as options, including, without limitation: (i) when a user initiates a payment transaction and is prompted to provide payment credential; (ii) when a user sets, changes or confirms a default payment method  
 
- The Visa Direct Service is displayed in a manner equal to, and in no less prominent position than, any other Money Movement Network, including, without limitation: (i) placement in any list, (ii) font size and (iii) visual emphasis or highlighting  
 
- The total cost to the user for using Visa Direct for AFTs is reasonably set in relation to the direct processing costs for the transaction. Your fee practices must always follow applicable law  
 
- If you provide support services (e.g., customer service support services, exception handling) for other Money Movement Networks, then you must provide the same services for the Visa Direct Service  
 
- The Visa Direct value proposition is actively and clearly articulated wherever you reference the Visa Direct Service (e.g., a Real-Time payment option providing users access to funds in real time or quickly, seven days a week, including public holidays and weekends)
