**When to use this article**

This guide provides a step-by-step process for effectively managing personal data deletion requests. The primary action is to escalate to the Account Manager. 

Category is Data Privacy.

### **The issue 💬**

Users may contact us to request the deletion of their data. In this case, you can report the information to the Account Managers, who will be responsible for contacting the relevant clients to initiate the data deletion procedure.

Check the sender's email address. Data privacy inquiries typically come from the end user to the DPO team via [DPO@checkout.com](mailto:DPO@checkout.com) and are then forwarded by the DPO team.

**Immediate Transfer Mandate:** The main responsibility of the team is to immediately identify and transfer any data privacy-related tickets upon receipt. Care Agents should not attempt to answer these tickets directly.

**Required Escalation Details:** To properly escalate the issue, agents need to ensure the ticket includes two pieces of information:

- The end user’s email address (as this is sometimes missing when the DPO team forwards the message).

- The organization for which the end user performed Identity Verification (IDV).
