**When to use this article**

This article provides a foundational overview of the IDV product, its importance in the digital economy, and how it helps our clients stay secure and compliant.INTRODUCTION TO IDV 🆔
At its core, IDV is a process used to confirm that a user is exactly who they claim to be. While traditional methods relied on passwords or PINs-which are easily forgotten or stolen, our IDV product uses **biometric verification**. 
 
This involves analyzing unique physical characteristics, such as an individual’s face to verify their identity during activities like logging in, making payments or applying for credit.

### **How the Product Works: The User Journey**

Our IDV solution is designed to be a fast, secure, and seamless digital flow that typically takes **less than two minutes**. The standard journey includes:
 

- 
**Document Capture:** The user records the front and back of a government-issued ID (such as a passport or driver’s license).

- 
**Liveness Detection:** The user takes a short video of their face to ensure they are a real person and not a static image or a deep fake.

- 
**Matching and AI Analysis:** Our AI analyzes movement, tilting, and holographic elements on the ID while performing facial mapping to ensure the user matches the document.

- 
**Instant Results:** Results, including data extraction and authenticity confirmation-are delivered in seconds.
