**When to use this article**

This guide provides a step-by-step process for efficiently handling IDV tickets in Zendesk, specifically focusing on obtaining and responding with a Reason for Outage (RFO). 

Category is Technical & Platform, and the main action is to escalate to the appropriate team.

### **The issue 💬**

For every technical incident, we must produce a reason for outage **RFO** that summarizes the causes of the incident and the steps to be taken to prevent it from happening again. 

Clients may ask for the RFO regarding a specific incident, in this case, you can ask our technical team for the RFO of a specific incident before forwarding it to the clients.
