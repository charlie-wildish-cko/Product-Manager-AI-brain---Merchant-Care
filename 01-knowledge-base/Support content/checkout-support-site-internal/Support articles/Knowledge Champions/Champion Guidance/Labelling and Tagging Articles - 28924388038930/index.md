 **LABELS & TAGS INTRODUCTION 💬**

 

## What are Zendesk labels? 🏷️

Labels improve searchability within Zendesk's help center and help our copilot Fin find and surface the right answers to agents. 

**Best Practices for Labelling an article:**

- Think like the user and use natural search terms

- Include tags for the issue type, product, topic, team, content type and region

- Includes common synonyms/acronyms

- 
Limit articles to 3-5 relevant labels

## What are Zendesk tags? 🔖

Content tags help group different types of content within Zendesk. Once an article is published, these tags appear as clickable links under a "Related to" section.**Best Practices for Tags:**

- Consider the features, problems, topics, or products the article covers

- A tag should be relevant to multiple articles

- Limit articles to 5-7 relevant tags

- Be specific and use precise, meaningful words
