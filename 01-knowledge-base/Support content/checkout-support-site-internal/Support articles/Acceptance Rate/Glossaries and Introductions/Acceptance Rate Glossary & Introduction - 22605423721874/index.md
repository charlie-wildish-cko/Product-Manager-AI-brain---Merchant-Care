## Acceptance Rate Process Flow

## Key Terms & Definitions

| **Term** | **Definition** |
| --- | --- |
| **Acceptance Rate** | Percentage of Authorised payment over requested payment |
| **Requested Payment** | All attempts combining authentication and authorisation |

## Roles, Levels, and Responsibilities

| **Roles** | **Responsibility** |
| --- | --- |
| **Merchant Care L1** | Investigate the approval rate queries and update the requestor on the progress toward resolution. If necessary, escalate to the relevant team. |
| **Merchant Care L2** | A deeper investigation into the approval rate issue where the Merchant Care L1 team encountered a blocker. Assist the merchants if there is a need to adjust the integration to improve or solve an approval rate request. |
| **Performance Team** | The Performance Team investigates and provides recommendations on improving the acceptance rate based on global data analysis. If the merchant is enrolled for Intelligence acceptance, they can apply some logic based on the findings to improve the acceptance rate through intelligence acceptance. |
| **Issuer Outreach Team** | The Issuer Outreach Team is responsible to initiate and maintain the relationship with issuer banks. For known issues with the banks, they can contact them for clarification on the root cause and recommendations for resolution. |
| **Operation Center (OC)** | The Operation Center Team investigates real-time performance to identify potential incidents and the root cause. They also contact external stakeholders such as Schemes and ACS providers to investigate further. |
| **Configuration Team** | The Configuration Team is responsible to make change to Client’s configuration on the CKO side. They investigate the Client’s setup for any potential errors and apply changes based on recommendations to improve the approval rate. |
