**Use this article when**

A merchant requires a formal Statement of Account or Balance Confirmation for financial records. The steps depend on whether the merchant is on the **HUB** or **NAS** platform.

### DESCRIBE THE ISSUE 💬

The merchant is requesting a financial document, specifically a Statement of Account (SOA) or a Balance Confirmation, typically for financial reporting purposes like an end-of-year audit. The required action depends on the platform the merchant uses (HUB or NAS) and the specific date requested. 

The Statement of Account (SOA) is a document detailing a merchant's outstanding invoices with Checkout.com as of a specific date. A Balance Confirmation is essentially a detailed breakdown of the report for the NAS platform, or a request sent to the Billing team for the HUB platform.

### KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- The process for obtaining an SOA or Balance Confirmation varies based on the merchant's platform: HUB or NAS.

- If the requested date is before 2022, the platform is always considered HUB.

- For a HUB, the internal team can generally generate it using the Finance MBC Statements (HUB report).

- For the HUB platform, if the merchant requires the Checkout.com format for the SOA, or if a Balance Confirmation is requested, the request must be sent to the Billing team.

- For the NAS platform, the merchant can often download the Fees summary report or the invoice for detailed breakdowns.

- 
To request a Balance Confirmation on the NAS platform, the request is sent to the Billing team and  require a specific "as of" date.

### PROCESS FOR REQUESTING STATEMENT OF ACCOUNT (SOA) AND BALANCE CONFIRMATION 🖊️

Step 1. Determine the Merchant Platform and Document Type**Check the merchant's platform:** Is the merchant on **HUB** or **NAS**?⚠️ If the "as of" date requested by the merchant is **before 2022**, treat the request as a **HUB** request, regardless of the current platform.**Identify the required document:**

-  Statement of Account (SOA) or,

- Balance Confirmation

Step 2. Follow the Platform-Specific ProcessIf the Merchant is on the HUB Platform:**For a standard SOA:** Use the ****[Finance MBC Statements (HUB report)](https://checkoutinternal.eu.looker.com/x/ZjSBtHH7XwdngkGSOwGx2Z) to generate the document.**For Balance Confirmation or Checkout.com formatted SOA:** Proceed to **Step 3** to submit a request to the Billing team.

### If the Merchant is on the NAS Platform:

 **For a detailed fees breakdown (often sufficient for SOA):** Advise the merchant to download the Fees summary report or the invoice.**For a formal Balance Confirmation:** Proceed to **Step 3** to submit a request to the Billing team. The Balance Confirmation is a detailed breakdown of the Fees summary report.

## Step 3. Submit a Request to the Billing Team (if required)

This step is required for: **HUB Balance Confirmation**, **HUB SOA in Checkout.com format**, and **NAS Balance Confirmation**.**Gather required information:**

- **Merchant ID**

- 
**Business ID** (required for HUB)

- The **"as of" date** for the document (crucial for both platforms)

- 
**Entity ID** (if the request is for a single entity)

- 
**Client ID** (if only a client-level confirmation is needed)

- 
**Send the request:** Submit the request to the **Billing team**.

The specific team or regional contact depends on the merchant's location.
