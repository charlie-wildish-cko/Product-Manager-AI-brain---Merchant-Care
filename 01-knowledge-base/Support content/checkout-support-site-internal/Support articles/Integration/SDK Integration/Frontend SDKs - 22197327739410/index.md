## Process Steps

**Web SDKs**

- 
**Description**: SDKs for JS libraries like React and React Native are available.

- 
**Usage**: Merchants integrate these libraries into their projects to modify Frames in their checkout pages efficiently.

**Mobile SDKs**

- 
**Description**: SDKs for iOS and Android platforms.

- 
**Usage**: Enables merchants to integrate Frames SDKs into their mobile apps for displaying card detail inputs and facilitating payments securely.
