**Use this article when**

Troubleshooting issues with Webhooks. INTRODUCTION TO WEBHOOK IP ADDRESS ALLOWLISTING 💬

If a merchant has restricted their server's traffic, their firewall may be blocking our webhook notifications- you must ensure that our IP addresses are on their allowlist.PROCESS TO TROUBLESHOOT WEBHOOK IP ADDRESS ALLOWLISTING 🖊️

- Ask the merchant if their server uses **IP address allowlisting**

- If so, confirm that all relevant Checkout.com IP addresses are on their allowlist

- Direct them to the ****[IP Address page](https://www.checkout.com/docs/developer-resources/webhooks/manage-webhooks/set-up-your-webhook-receiver#IP_address_allowlisting) to get the current list

- Advise them to update their allowlist if the list has changed, as this can lead to delivery failures

💡 After the update, recommend they test the webhook delivery to ensure it is working correctly
