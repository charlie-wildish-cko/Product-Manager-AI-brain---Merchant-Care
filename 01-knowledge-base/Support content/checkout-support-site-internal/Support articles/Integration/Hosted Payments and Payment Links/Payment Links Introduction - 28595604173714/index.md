## INTRODUCTION TO PAYMENT LINKS** 💬**

Payment Links is a feature that allows merchants to create a shareable link that directs a customer to a secure, Checkout.com-hosted page to complete a payment. This tool simplifies the process of getting paid by handling the end-to-end payment flow.

Payment Links are designed to:

- 
**Show the right payment options:** The payment page displays the best ways for a customer to pay based on their device, location, and the transaction currency.

- 
**Capture necessary information:** The feature ensures all required payment details are collected correctly, including extra inputs for specific payment methods (e.g., an IBAN for iDEAL).

- 
**Handle the entire payment flow:** It manages complex processes like 3D Secure (3DS) authentication and redirects to third-party payment pages automatically.

### How It Works: The Three-Step Flow

The process for using Payment Links is straightforward for both the merchant and their customer.

1. 
**Create:** A merchant can generate Payment Links directly from the Checkout.com Dashboard user interface. For merchants who need to automate this process, links can also be generated via the Payment Links API.

2. 
**Share:** Once the link is created, the merchant can share it with their customer through any channel they prefer, such as SMS, email, or other messaging applications.

3. 
**Pay:** The customer clicks the link and is directed to a secure payment page hosted by Checkout.com. On this page, they see the transaction details and can choose from a variety of payment methods to complete their purchase.

 
 
**KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑**

 

Payment Links offer a powerful solution for merchants with specific needs, combining ease of use with robust payment processing.

- 
**No Technical Expertise Needed:** Merchants can create links entirely through the self-serve Dashboard without any coding.

- 
**Simplified PCI Compliance:** Because Checkout.com captures, stores, and processes all sensitive payment information on a hosted page, the merchant's PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) compliance is simplified to the SAQ-A level.

- 
**Global Payment Methods:** The feature dynamically displays relevant Alternative Payment Methods (APMs) based on the customer's geography, and new payment methods can be deployed without requiring any code changes from the merchant.

- 
**Optimized for Performance:** The payment page design is continuously evolved to provide the best format, error handling, and high-performing payment methods to drive conversion and authorization rates.
