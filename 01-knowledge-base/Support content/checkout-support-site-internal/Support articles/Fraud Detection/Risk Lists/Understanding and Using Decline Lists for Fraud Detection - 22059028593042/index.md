## INTRODUCTION TO DECLINE LISTS 💬

Decline lists are collections of specific attributes like card numbers or email addresses, that can be used to immediately decline a transaction if it matches an item on the list. 

Decline lists are highly effective for blocking unwanted or fraudulent payments. Merchants can create decline lists for the following six fields:

- 
**Card number**: The full 16-digit number of the card

- 
**BIN**: The first 6 to 8 digits of the card number, which identifies the card's issuing bank

- 
**Email address**: The customer's full email address

- 
**Phone**: The customer's phone number

- 
**Payment IP**: The customer's IP address

- 
**Email domain**: The part of the email address that comes after the "@" symbol

⚠️ Only owners, risk managers and admins can edit lists, including adding or removing entries.MERCHANT PROCESS TO UPDATE A DECLINE LIST 🖊️

Merchants can add to a decline list through the dashboard:

**From a Transaction**:

- Log into the  [Dashboard](https://dashboard.checkout.com/)

- Go to the **Payment details** view for a specific transaction in the Dashboard

- Select the **Decline list** button

💡 This method only adds the **card number** to the decline listRESOURCES 📍
