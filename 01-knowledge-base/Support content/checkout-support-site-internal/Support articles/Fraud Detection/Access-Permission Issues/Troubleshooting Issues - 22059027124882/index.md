## Process Steps

**Issue: The customer cannot find the ‘Fraud Detection’ tab in the dashboard.**

**Steps to Resolve:**

1. Confirm the level (client or entity) that the merchant is viewing the dashboard with

2. If they are at the entity level, advise the customer to switch their view to the client level to access the ‘Fraud Detection’ tab.

3. If the tab is still not visible, ensure the customer has the necessary permissions as outlined in the [user permissions documentation](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/use-the-dashboard/user-permissions).

4. If you have checked and amended the above and they are still unable to view the tab, please escalate this to the Dashboard team by raising a ticket [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/111/group/462/create/277).

**Issue: The customer encounters an error when trying to access a specific section on Fraud Detection.**

**Steps to Resolve:**

1. Verify the specific section the customer is trying to access.

2. Confirm that the error is related to insufficient permissions by checking their permission via the dashboard → settings → user permissions (Documentation Link: [User Permissions Guide](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/use-the-dashboard/user-permissions))

3. Advise the customer to contact the dashboard owner to update their permissions if necessary.

**Issue:  Merchant is not able to add items to list(s)**

**Steps to Resolve:**

1. Request a screenshot from the customer to verify their current view and access level.

2. Confirm whether the user has the necessary permissions to add items at the client level. If not, ask them to contact the dashboard owner.

3. If they do have the necessary access, Instruct the customer to switch their view from the entity level to the client level.

4. If the issue persists, refer the customer to the [user permissions documentation](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/use-the-dashboard/user-permissions) or escalate the issue via the #ask-fraud-detection channel if permissions are confirmed to be correct or raise a Fraud Detection Support Request [via Jira](https://checkout.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/111/group/462/create/277).
