**When to use this article**

To identify if the reason a settlement has bounced back is because they have a high threshold set on their settlement amount.

**Problem/Symptom: **A merchant contacts us because their settlement has bouced back, this is usually due to a high settlement threshold

⚠️ You must get approval before requesting a removal of the threshold

## INTRODUCTION TO HIGH THRESHOLDS 💬

A payout threshold may be placed on a merchant’s account for various reasons, like during bank changes or due to a request from our Risk Monitoring team. 

If a high threshold is set, a payout will not be automatically triggered, even if a settlement is due. 

If a merchant reports a payout delay, you can follow these steps to check for and remove a threshold if necessary.

💡 For example, if the threshold is GBP 1000 and the settlement is GBP 950, the funds will not be paid out.

 

## PROCESS TO CHECK FOR A HIGH THRESHOLD 🖊️

- Log in to the **Client Admin Tool (CAT)** and search for the merchant by name:

- 
**Select the correct entity** for which the missing payout was reported:

- In the left navigation panel, select **Payout Schedules**:

- Choose the currency account that was reported as not paid:

- Check the **Threshold** **amount** field:

- 
If you see a high number (e.g 1,000,000,000), it means that an automatic payout will not be triggered until that amount is reached
 

⚒️ RESOLUTION

⚠️ If a payout threshold is present, you must determine why it was put in place before we can remove it.

- 
**For Tier 1, 2, 3, or 4 managed accounts: **Contact the Account Manager to understand the reason for the threshold

- Do not contact the Risk team immediately, as this team may not have requested the high threshold

After you have confirmed that the threshold is no longer necessary, you can contact the Merchant Configuration team to remove it.

1. From your merchant’s ticket in **Zendesk,** create a side conversation

2. 
Use the relevant regional **Merchant Configuration** macro to contact the team
