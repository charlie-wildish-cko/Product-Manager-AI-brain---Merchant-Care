## Process Steps

1. 
Check the Dashboard to make sure that the settlement that is being referred to should have been settled as per the Dashboard. To do this:

  1. In Dashboard, click on Funds

  2. Select Settlements

  3. Select the respective settlement report and click on it for more details as required

2. Contact the Payments team to request the MT103 document

  1. To contact the Payments team, use the relevant regional “Treasury Payments” macro to create a side conversation in Zendesk from your merchant’s ticket. For further information, please see [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CHEC/pages/5991727266/Case+Handling+-+SOP#Emailing-internal-partner-teams%3A)

3. Once the Payments team replies with the MT103, send this to the merchant. This document is proof that CKO made the settlement payment to the merchant

4. The merchant will use the MT103 (see format sample below) to check for the payment with their bank The MT103 has SWIFT data which the merchant’s bank can use to identify and find the payment on their system

  1. If the merchant’s bank still cannot identify the payment, go back to the Payments Team

**Note: **For cases where a merchant reaches out as they believe that their settlement is delayed, the Merchant Care team should also reach out to the Payments team as described above**What is an MT103?**MT103 is a standardised SWIFT message format used for international wire transfers. It contains essential transaction details, including the transaction reference, instruction code, value date, currency code, and payment amount. This format is crucial for ensuring accurate and efficient processing of cross-border payments, and it may be requested for payment confirmation or tracing purposes.**When is it needed?**MT103 is issued after a payment has been processed and funds have been sent. It can be provided to a merchant for tracking, compliance, dispute resolution, or as proof of payment, especially in large, international transactions.**MT103 format sample:**
