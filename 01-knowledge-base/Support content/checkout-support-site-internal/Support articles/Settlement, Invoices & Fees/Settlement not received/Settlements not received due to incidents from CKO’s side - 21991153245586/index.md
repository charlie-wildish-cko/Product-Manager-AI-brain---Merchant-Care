## Process Steps

1. 
Go to Dashboard and confirm the issue that the merchant is seeing by checking the settlement ID. To do this:

  1. In Dashboard, click on Funds

  2. Select Settlements

  3. Select the respective settlement report and check as required

2. Once you have identified the issue that the merchant has reported, check the Operations Centre slack channel, **#oc**, to check if this is already a known issue

  1. If so, advise the merchant that we are aware of the issue and we are working on this, and provide the merchant with any additional information/guidance as per the OC team’s advice.

  2. If the issue is not known, continue to the below step:

3. Contact the Payments team for assistance

  1. To contact the Payments team, use the relevant regional “Treasury Payments” macro to create a side conversation in Zendesk from your merchant’s ticket. For further information, please see [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CHEC/pages/5991727266/Case+Handling+-+SOP#Emailing-internal-partner-teams%3A)

4. After the payments team has investigated the issue, they will reply to Merchant Care. Respond to the merchant advising the merchant of the case’s outcome and close the loop with the merchant
