## Process Steps

1. Bank holidays in each country affect the merchants onboarded in that country and could cause delays in the merchant’s settlements. These delays are expected and both merchants and Merchant Care should already be aware of these delays:

  1. Merchant Communications informs merchants ahead of time

  2. The Finance or Treasury team informs Merchant Care ahead of time

2. If a merchant contacts Merchant Care due to these delays, remind them that this is an expected delay due to the bank holiday
