**Use this article when**

A terminated merchant contacts you with questions about their final settlement payment.INTRODUCTION TO SETTLEMENT INQUIRIES FROM TERMINATED MERCHANTS💬

 

Merchants who have been terminated often contact Merchant Care with questions about their final settlement payment. This article provides a clear, step-by-step process for handling these inquiries, focusing on confirming the merchant's termination status and escalating the case to the appropriate team for a final resolution.PROCESS 🖊️

### Step 1. Check for Termination Status in Salesforce

- First, you must verify if the merchant has been officially terminated

- Go to Salesforce and search for the merchant's account

- Once you are on the merchant’s account page, click the **"**Activity" tab

- Scroll down to find the **"Termination letter"** activity in the email history and confirm that the termination letter has been sent

## Step 2. Escalate the Case to the Risk Team

- Once you have confirmed that the merchant has been terminated, do not provide an estimate for the settlement yourself

- Use the transfer macro template on Zendesk to escalate the case to the Risks team

- The Risk team is best equipped to review and advise the merchant directly on their final settlement or an early payout - as they have specific criteria they must check

FAQs⁉️

**Q: Can I give the merchant a date for when they will receive their settlement?****A:** No, you should not give a specific date or estimate for the settlement. The Risks team has specific criteria to check and must advise the merchant directly. Your role is to confirm their termination status and then escalate the case to the appropriate team.
