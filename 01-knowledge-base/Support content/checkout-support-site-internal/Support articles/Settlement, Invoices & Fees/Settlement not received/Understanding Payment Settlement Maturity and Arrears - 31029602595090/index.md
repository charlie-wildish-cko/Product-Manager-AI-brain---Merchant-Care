**When to use this article**

To understand payment settlement maturity and arrears and to help explain to a customer why they're missing or experiencing delays with their Monday settlement.

## INTRODUCTION 💬

This article outlines the current policy governing payment settlement maturity and how it affects the timing of funds being credited to a customer account. We will explain the shift to the **3-Day (3d) arrears model**, detail how your daily processing corresponds to the settlement schedule, and clarify the key differences from the previous 2-day model.**What is the current policy for payment settlement maturity (3d arrears)?**

The current settlement policy uses a 3-day (3d) arrears model, which dictates when funds from processing will mature and be included in the settlement. This policy is correct and aligns with scheme settlement delays.

⚠️ Funds do not mature over the weekend, as maturity only occurs during working days.**How do my processing days correspond to the maturity and settlement days under the 3 Days arrears model?**

Under the 3-day arrears model, the maturity and settlement schedule is as follows:

| **Processing Day** | **Current Maturity Day** | **Current Settlement Inclusion** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wednesday** | **Monday** | Included in **Monday** settlement |
| **Thursday** | **Tuesday** | Included in **Tuesday** settlement |
| **Friday** | **Wednesday** | Included in **Wednesday** settlement |
| **Saturday** | **Wednesday** | Included in **Wednesday** settlement |
| **Sunday** | **Wednesday** | Included in **Wednesday** settlement |

**Why was the previous 2-Day arrears model changed?**

The previous 2-day (2d) arrears model caused an artificially larger settlement on Monday because all processing from Wednesday, Thursday and Friday would mature on the same day (Monday).

Under the old system:

| **Processing Day** | **Previous Maturity Day** | **Previous Settlement Impact** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wednesday (Weds) | Friday | Included in Friday Settlement |
| Thursday (Thurs) | Saturday | **Paid on Monday** |
| Friday (Fri) | Sunday | **Paid on Monday** |

All of this processing was paid on **Monday**, as maturity only occurs during working days. This meant the Monday settlement was larger than it should have been relative to the available balance.**Why do weekend transactions (Friday, Saturday, and Sunday) all mature on Wednesday?**

Funds do not mature over the weekend, because of scheme settlement delays and the rule that maturity only occurs during working days. Friday transactions should not be paid on Monday as the funds will not have been received by the scheme until later in the week.

Therefore, under the 3-Day arrears model:

- Friday transactions mature on Wednesday

- Saturday and Sunday transactions also mature on Wednesday due to the working-day only rule for maturity.

WHAT TO TELL THE CUSTOMER 🗣️

Use these points when a customer asks about a change or delay in their settlement timing, particularly for Friday/Weekend processing.**If a customer asks about a "missing" or "delayed" Monday settlement:**

| **Area** | **Talking Point/Action** |
| --- | --- |
| **Acknowledge** | "Thank you for raising this. We recently updated our settlement schedule to correctly align with how the scheme handles payments." |
| **Explain Change** | "Previously, funds processed on Friday would appear to mature on Monday, leading to a large settlement. The scheme rules state that maturity only happens on working days." |
| **Provide Current Status** | "Under the current rules, processing done over the weekend (Friday, Saturday, and Sunday) will now all mature and be included in your Wednesday settlement." |
| **Reassure** | "The funds are not lost; they are being held to align with the scheme's standard working-day settlement cycle. You will see them in your account on Wednesday." |

**If a customer asks **_**why**_** the change happened:**

| **Area** | **Talking Point/Action** |
| --- | --- |
| **Focus on Compliance** | "The previous method created an artificially large settlement on Monday because it assumed maturity over the weekend.  The new system ensures we are fully compliant with scheme settlement delays, as funds do not mature on non-working days." |
| **Simplified Explanation** | "Money processed late on Friday cannot be fully received by the scheme until later in the week, so we are now reflecting that official delay in our settlement to you." |
