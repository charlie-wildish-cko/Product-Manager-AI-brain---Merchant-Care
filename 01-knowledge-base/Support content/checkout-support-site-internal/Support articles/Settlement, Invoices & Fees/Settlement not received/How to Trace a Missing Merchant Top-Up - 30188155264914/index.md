**When to use this article**

This guide outlines the process for assisting a merchant whose self-served top-up is not reflected in their live balance.

## INTRODUCTION TO THE ISSUE 💬

A **top-up** is when a merchant adds funds to their account, they do this to make sure they have enough money to handle transactions and payouts. If a merchant contacts us to ask why their top-up hasn't been reflected on the balance, please follow these steps.

## TRACING A MISSING TOP-UP PROCESS 🖊️

**Obtain Proof of Top-Up:** Ask the merchant to provide documentation of their top-up transaction. This can include a screenshot of their bank statement or a transaction ID.

**Contact Regional Treasury:** Forward the proof of top-up and relevant merchant details to the regional treasury team by using the macro:

| Macro: Transfer - Treasury Payments - Regional team |
| --- |

**Confirm Balance Update:** Await confirmation from the regional treasury team regarding the date the merchant's balance was updated.

**Confirm with the Merchant:** Once you have the updated information, direct the merchant to their **Balance Report** on the dashboard to verify the updated balance. This is a self-serve report that provides a detailed breakdown of all top-ups.
