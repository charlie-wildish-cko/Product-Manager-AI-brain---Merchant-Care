## Process Steps

1. In this scenario, the most common root cause is that the merchant has more than one account, e.g. they could have two MBC accounts and one NAS account

  1. Merchant Care should look out for this as the root cause, however, if they are not able to resolve the issue, the agent should contact the Payments team for assistance

    1. To contact the Payments team, use the relevant regional “Treasury Payments” macro to create a side conversation in Zendesk from your merchant’s ticket. For further information, please see [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CHEC/pages/5991727266/Case+Handling+-+SOP#Emailing-internal-partner-teams%3A)

2. After the payments team has investigated the issue, they will reply to Merchant Care. Respond to the merchant advising of the case’s outcome and close the loop with the merchant
