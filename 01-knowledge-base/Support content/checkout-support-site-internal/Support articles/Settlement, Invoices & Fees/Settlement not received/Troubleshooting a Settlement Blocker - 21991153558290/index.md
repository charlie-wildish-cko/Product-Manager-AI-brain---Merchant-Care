**When to use this article**

To investigate why a settlement amount is delayed and to escalate to the payments team.INTRODUCTION TO THE ISSUE 💬

A settlement blocker is when the transfer of funds from Checkout.com to a merchant's bank account is delayed. This can happen due to an issue with either the merchant's bank or Checkout.com's bank. 

If a merchant reports a missing settlement, use this guide to confirm the issue and contact the Payments team. PROCESS FOR TROUBLESHOOTING A SETTLEMENT BLOCKER 🖊️

### Step 1. Confirm the missing settlement amount

To do this, check the settlement statement in the Dashboard.

- In the **Dashboard**, click on **Funds**

- Select **Settlements**

- Find and click on the relevant settlement report to view its details

 

 

### Step 2. Contact the Payments team

After you have confirmed that the missing amount is correct, contact the Payments team to investigate the issue. 

You can do this by using the regional "Treasury Payments" macro to create a side conversation in Zendesk from the merchant's ticket.

 

### Step 3. Advise the merchant of the outcome

The Payments team will investigate the issue and communicate with the relevant bank. 

Once they reply to Merchant Care with their findings, you are responsible for informing the merchant of the case's outcome and closing the ticket.
