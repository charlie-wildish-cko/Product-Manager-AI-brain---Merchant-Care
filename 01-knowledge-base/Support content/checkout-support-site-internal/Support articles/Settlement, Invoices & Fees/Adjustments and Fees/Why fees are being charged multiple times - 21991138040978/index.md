## Process Steps

When merchants contact Merchant Care asking for help with fees for which they have been charged multiple times, Merchant Care’s first port of call should be the [NAS Financial Reporting & Reconciliation Confluence page](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/MER/pages/5509021735/NAS+Financial+Reporting+Reconciliation+FAQ#01.-Reporting-ownership-for-better-support-triage). If this page does not provide sufficient guidance to assist the merchant, Merchant Care can:

- Use the [Breakdown types page](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types) from the Checkout Documentation to obtain further information on the various fees that merchants are charged

  - 
**Note**: The b[reakdown types page](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types) can be shared with merchants.

- Contact the FTS team for support

**Note**: Tier 1 merchants tend to contact their AMs first with this type of query, rather than contacting Merchant Care in the first instance. As such, Merchant Care does not see a large number of these queries from Tier 1 merchants.
