## Process Steps

If merchants contact Merchant Care asking for what fees they can expect to be charged, Merchant Care can assist them by providing them with information and links to Checkout’s Documentation.

[This merchant-facing document](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Fees) provides information on all fees that merchants can expect, including descriptions as well as Action ID and Payment ID formats.

Merchant Care can further assist the merchant by providing specific details on any specific fees that the merchant is looking for, both by sending them the relevant link below and by researching the fees using the documentation provided and explaining this to the merchant in their reply.

- [Card processing fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Card_processing_fees)

- [Alternative payment method fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Alternative_payment_method_fees)

- [Refund fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Refund_fees)

- [Chargeback fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Chargeback_fees)

- [Authentication fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Authentication_fees)

- [Fraud Detection Fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Fraud_Detection_fees)

- [Bank payout fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Bank_payout_fees)

- [Card payout fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Card_Payout_fees)

- [Integrated Platforms fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Integrated_Platforms_fees)

- [Account Updater fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Account_Updater_fees)

- [Intelligent Acceptance fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Intelligent_Acceptance_fees)

- [Client Settlement fees](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/breakdown-types#Client_Settlement_fees)
