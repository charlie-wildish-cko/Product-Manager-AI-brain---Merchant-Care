**Use this article when**

A merchant needs to transfer funds manually and you need to confirm the correct banking details.

When a merchant needs to transfer funds manually to cover a negative balance or top up their account, it is critical to use the correct banking details. Following this process ensures we remain compliant with regulatory safeguarding requirements and prevents internal accounting breaches.

PROCESS STEPS 🖊️

## 1. Identify the Request and Entity Details

Gather the following information from the merchant's request:

- **Merchant Name** and **Client ID**

- **Entity ID**: If not in Zendesk, use the CAT or Looker dashboard to find it

- **Checkout Legal Entity** (e.g., MENA)

- **Account Structure** (e.g., NAS)

## 2. Document the Purpose and Currency

Before reaching out to Treasury, you must clearly document:

- **The Purpose**: Why is the merchant transferring funds? (e.g., covering a negative balance)

- **The Currency**: What currency is the merchant sending?

## 3. Request Confirmation from Treasury

⚠️ **Warning**: Never share bank letters or details with a merchant without Treasury's prior approval. 

- Start a Zendesk Side Conversation addressed to **Mena Treasury Payments**

- Provide the entity details and the specific purpose of the transfer

- Request the specific banking details for that transaction

## 4. Provide Details to Merchant

Once Treasury provides the specific banking details (e.g., AED pay account details), relay them to the merchant. Advise the merchant to provide a **Proof of Payment** once the transfer is complete.

## 5. Finalize Adjustment

- Forward the merchant's proof of payment to the Treasury team

- Treasury will confirm the settlement and perform the necessary internal adjustments to update the merchant's balance

ESCALATION  ⬆️ 

Escalate to the **Treasury Team** via Side Conversation if:

- The merchant has transferred funds to the wrong bank account.

- The merchant requires a currency or transfer purpose for which no standard bank details exist.

- You need an adjustment ID to confirm a balance update.

FAQs ⁇

**Why can't I just give the merchant our standard bank details?**

We do not have "standard" banking details for these scenarios. The correct account depends entirely on the purpose of the transfer and the currency involved.

**What happens if the merchant uses the wrong banking details?**

Receiving funds into the incorrect account impacts our regulatory safeguarding and can result in legal breaches.
