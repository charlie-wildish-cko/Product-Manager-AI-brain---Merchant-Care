**When to use this article**

To support merchants who need to self-serve and obtain custom settlement reports directly from their Dashboard.INTRODUCTION TO CUSTOM SETTLEMENT REPORTS 💬

Merchants can self-serve through the Dashboard to get a detailed breakdown of transactions associated with a specific payout. Provide the merchant with these steps to guide them through the process of downloading a custom settlement report.PROCESS FOR MERCHANTS TO FOLLOW 🖊️ 

Here is how a merchant can download a **Financial Actions by Payout ID (Transaction Breakdown report)** from the Dashboard.

- Log in to the Dashboard

- Navigate to the Funds tab and then click on Settlements

- Select the relevant settlement for which you need the custom report

- 
Once on the settlement page, scroll down to find the Transaction breakdown section

- Click on Download CSV to download the report

💡**Tip:** While you can change the timezone on the Dashboard, all reports and settlements are generated in the UTC timezone.ESCALATION ⬆️If a merchant is unable to find the "Transaction breakdown" option on their Dashboard, it might not be enabled for their account. In this case, you can follow these steps:

- Check CAT: Go to the Merchant Entity in CAT, then to Reporting Profiles and check under "Financial Actions by payout"

- Escalate: If the report is not enabled, contact the Merchant Configuration team and ask them to enable it for the merchant

- For other issues with custom settlement reports that Merchant Care can't solve, please reach out to the FTS (Financial Technical Support) team
