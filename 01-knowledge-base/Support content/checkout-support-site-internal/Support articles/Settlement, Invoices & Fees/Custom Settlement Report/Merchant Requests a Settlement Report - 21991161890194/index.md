**Use this article when**

A merchant requests a settlement report for audit purposes or for any other reason

## INTRODUCTION TO SETTLEMENT REPORTS 💬

A settlement report is a document that gives detailed information about financial transactions and settlements. It's used by merchants to help reconcile and confirm the amounts processed during a settlement cycle. 

The report usually includes grouped records of transactions, such as the transaction function, message type, and activity type, making it easier to spot any discrepancies.

## PROCESS FOR PROVIDING A SETTLEMENT REPORT🖊️

### For Audit Purposes

If a merchant needs a settlement report for an audit, you must contact the Financial Technical Support (FTS) L2 team. In Zendesk, create a side conversation with the **L2 FTS macro**

The FTS L2 team will perform the following steps:

1. Obtain the necessary balance confirmation from the Billing team

2. Generate the required Financial Action Report (settlement report)

3. Follow up with you once the report has been provided

For Other Purposes

Merchants can self-serve by following the instructions in the external knowledge article ['Download settlement report'.](https://support.checkout.com/hc/en-us/articles/14326530425234-Download-settlement-report)
