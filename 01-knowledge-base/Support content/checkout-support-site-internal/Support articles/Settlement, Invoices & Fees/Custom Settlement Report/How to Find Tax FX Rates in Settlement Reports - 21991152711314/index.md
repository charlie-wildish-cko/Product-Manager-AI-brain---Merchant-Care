**When to use this article**

You need to support a merchant with a step-by-step guide to finding the tax foreign exchange (FX) rates within their settlement reports. These rates can be located for each settlement directly in the Dashboard.

## INTRODUCTION TO TAX FX RATES 💬

Tax foreign exchange (FX) rates refer to the foreign exchange rates used to convert tax amounts from one currency to another. These rates are crucial for accurately calculating and reporting taxes in different currencies, especially when dealing with international transactions.PROCESS FOR MERCHANTS TO DOWNLOAD TAX FX RATES 🖊️

Provide the merchant with these steps to self-serve through their dashboard.

### Step 1: Navigate to Settlements

- From your Dashboard, find and click on the **Business account** section in the right-hand menu

- Then, select **Settlements** from the dropdown menu:

### Step 2: Select a Settlement Report

- On the **Settlements** page, find and choose the specific settlement report you need to view:

### Step 3: Download the Report

- On the **Settlement** **details** page, scroll down to the **Transaction** **breakdown** section

- Click the **Download CSV button** next to it to download the report:

### Step 4: Locate the Tax FX Rate Column

- Open the downloaded CSV file in a spreadsheet program. Look for the Tax FX Rate column - this is typically column AH in the spreadsheet

- If a tax FX rate were applied to the settlement, the rate would be listed in the Tax FX Rate column of the CSV report
