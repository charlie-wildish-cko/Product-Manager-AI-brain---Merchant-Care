**When to use this article**

To help merchants with billing and invoice inquiries, specifically those related to Minimum Monthly Billing (MMB) fees.

## MINIMUM BILLING FEE INTRODUCTION 💬

- 
**MMB Purpose**: To cover setup costs and maintain a minimum revenue threshold each month

- 
**Logic**: The fees are based on the difference between the revenue generated by the client and the minimum billing threshold set by the service provider. If the revenue is below the threshold, **the difference **is charged as an MMB fee

- 
**Calculation**: The charge is determined by the variance between the calculated Checkout fees (excluding pass-through fees like tax, payouts, scheme, and interchange fees) and the minimum billing threshold established by the service provider  
Minimum Billing calculation of fees is based on net revenue to Checkout, not the gross amount of fees

- 
**Application**: Minimum billing fees are calculated and applied at the beginning of each month for the previous month's transactions

- 
**Currency**: The currency used for minimum billing should match the currency specified in the Monthly Minimum Billing (MMB) agreement

- 
**Reports**: Minimum billing information can be found in your invoice report, balance report, payout reports, and financial actions reports

For additional details about the Checkout Fees that are used to determine the minimum billing, you can refer to this page: [Minimum Billing Fee](https://www.checkout.com/legal/minimum-billing-fee)
The core issues involve validating correct charges, such as checking if minimum billing fees were applied accurately, and clarifying specific pricing amendments. 
Additionally, users frequently seek a detailed breakdown of fees applied in a particular month and explanations for negative balances resulting from minimum billing. The overarching goal is to provide clear confirmation and a comprehensive understanding of all charges and adjustments on their invoices.

## PROCESS TO CALCULATE MINIMUM BILLING FEE🖊️

📌 **Note:** Remember that the minimum billing fee reflects the fees applied to events on the **previous month**
Here is an example to help clarify the billing process: If the minimum billing commitment is 5,000 EUR and customer fees in month X are 1,000 EUR, the difference of 4,000 EUR will be charged as a minimum billing fee in month X+1.  
**Example metadata:** Check the correctness of the August 2025 MMB with these values:

- MMB commitment: 5000

- Month to check: August 2025

- Financial Action Report: July 2025

**Step 1. Check the current minimum billing settings of the merchant**

- Log in to CAT and at the client level select the Minimum Billing tab on the side bar menu

**Step 2. Check the actual amount for the minimum billing from the invoice**

- Download the invoice of August 2025 from the Dashboard. The amount in the MMB for August is Euro 2804.23, as shown in the Invoice

- 
**Dashboard view**: Select Dashboard > Business account > Invoices from the left menu bar:-

- 
Invoice view: In the invoice, you can find the value that the customer has paid for the MMB under the "Other fees and adjustments" section:-  

**Step 3. Download the Financial Action Report by Date Range report (FAR)**

- Download the July Financial Action Report (previous month) in UTC from Dashboard Reports:- 

**Step 4. Apply filters in the downloaded Financial Action Report (FAR)**

- Enable Filters for all columns

- Select the  "Breakdown Type" column and apply a filter

- Select all the fees available:  [Minimum Billing Fee](https://www.checkout.com/legal/minimum-billing-fee)

⚡️ Fast approach: Select all fees, excluding pass-through fees such as tax, payouts, all scheme fees and interchange

- Select the "Holding Currency Amount" column to view the overall sum, which is **-2195.769586**

 
**Step 5. Calculate the minimum billing**

- The amount calculated in the Financial Action Report of July is -2195.769586

- The MMB commitment is 5000

Example calculation: 5000 - 2195.769586 = **2804.23**0414
**Step 6. Compare with the invoice**

- The amount calculated in step 5 is **2804.23**0414

- The amount in the invoice of August 2025 is Euro **2804.23**

Invoices display amounts in the [major unit](https://www.checkout.com/docs/payments/accept-payments/format-the-amount-value) of the holding currency, rounded to two decimal places, so they match 
**Calculate the minimum billing using Looker**
For the calculations above, we used the Financial Actions report from the Dashboard; however, Looker is often much easier to use, especially for merchants with a high volume of monthly transactions.  
The results in Looker show the Checkout fees charged for the month.  
  
The same results as above are obtained using the Looker report below:  
  
****[Looker](https://checkoutinternal.eu.looker.com/explore/ledger_financial_actions/finance_financial_actions_report?qid=duc13wzwKjG3XpwBaiuyPj&toggle=fil)

 

## RESOLUTION ⚒️

- Once you have completed the reconciliation and found a match, confirm to the merchant that the MMB fee was calculated correctly

- The amount on the invoice will be the rounded value of your calculation, as invoices are displayed in the major unit of the currency with two decimal places

- When the customer is not sure about what fees are affecting MMB-> Send them the external article: [Minimum Billing Fee](https://www.checkout.com/legal/minimum-billing-fee)

What is Minimum Billing (MMB)**Minimum Billing Fees**, also known as Minimum Monthly Fees or Minimum Monthly Charge (MMC), refer to a fixed charge imposed by payment processors or merchant service providers to ensure a minimum revenue threshold each month, regardless of the actual transaction volume processed by the customer. If a customer does not meet the specified transaction volume, they are charged the difference as a minimum billing fee.
The concept of minimum billing involves charging customers extra if they do not meet a specified processing volume. This strategy helps service providers recover setup costs and maintain a minimum revenue level monthly.Where can I find the Minimum Billing settingsAvailable for NAS configurations only.

Follow this link to have the updated list of Checkout fees, [Minimum billing Fees](https://www.checkout.com/legal/minimum-billing-fee)When do **changes** take effect on the next minimum billingIf any of the minimum billing config options need to be adjusted, this is relatively simple to do. Minimum billing calculations are undertaken at the end of a calendar month billing cycle on the following day at around 2 am UTC. So, as long as changes are made before this cutoff time, then the changes to the minimum billing configuration will occur in the next run. 
For instance, if a modification is made on January 15th, the changes will not take effect immediately. Instead, they will be applied during the next billing cycle, which begins at 2:00 AM UTC on February 1st. This means that any adjustments made mid-month are scheduled to be implemented at the start of the upcoming billing period to ensure accurate billing and account management.What **currency **is set for minimum billing?The Minimum Billing Currency **MUST** be set to the same currency as that of the Minimum Income Currency as displayed on the pricing schedule. To do this, you must change the **‘Contract Currency’** at the top of the contract object. The default is USD.
