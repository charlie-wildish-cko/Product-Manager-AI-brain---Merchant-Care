**When to use this article:**

Use this guide when the **Scheme Compliance** team asks if a fine can be passed on to a merchant.

## INTRODUCTION TO SCHEME FINES** 💬**

**Why are fines applied?** Card schemes impose rules to comply with regulations, if a merchant does not follow these rules, the scheme may issue a fine.

 

## CORRECT ACTION ✅

Advise the Scheme Compliance team to speak directly with the **Checkout.com Legal Team **if they wish to pass on fees to the merchant.

- The Legal Team is responsible for reviewing the merchant's contract to see if passing on a fine is permitted

- Once you've responded to the Scheme Compliance team, close the case as a 'non-merchant request'

## WHAT NOT TO DO ⚠️

- 
**Do not contact the merchant** to ask for their consent or approval

- 
**Do not make a commercial decision** or offer a waiver yourself

The decision is strictly a legal and contractual matter, so must be escalated to legal by the scheme compliance team.

 

## FAQs** ****❓**

What's the difference between a fee and a fine

A fee is a standard charge for using a Checkout.com product or service, such as processing a transaction. A fine is a monetary penalty passed through from a Card Scheme (like Visa or Mastercard) for breaking their rules. Fees are for services rendered; fines are penalties for rule violations.
