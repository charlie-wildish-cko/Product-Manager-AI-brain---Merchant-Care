## Process Steps

If a merchant wants to include their VAT number in their invoice, agents must follow the below steps:

1. 
Go on CAT to cross-check what is already configured for the merchant. To check this:

  1. Go to the merchant’s account in [CAT](https://client-admin.cko-prod.ckotech.co/web/nas/) and go to “Entities”

  2. Under “Entity details” you will see the Tax number field which is optional unless the entity is contracting with CKO LLC, e.g. in the below example, there is no TAX number registered for the merchant.

2. Request the correct VAT number from the merchant

3. Contact the Merchant Configuration team requesting that they add the merchant’s VAT registration number to the merchant’s CAT configuration

  1. To contact the Merchant Configuration team, use the relevant regional macro on Zendesk to create a side conversation. For further information, please see [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CHEC/pages/5991727266/Case+Handling+-+SOP#Emailing-internal-partner-teams%3A)

4. Once the Merchant Configuration team confirms that the VAT number has been added to the merchant’s account, agents should contact the Billing team requesting that they regenerate the merchant’s invoice with the VAT number included

  1. To contact the Billing team, use the relevant regional macro on Zendesk to create a side conversation. For further information, please see

5. Once the Billing team has regenerated the invoice, they will send this to Merchant Care who is then responsible for sending this to the merchant and closing the case
