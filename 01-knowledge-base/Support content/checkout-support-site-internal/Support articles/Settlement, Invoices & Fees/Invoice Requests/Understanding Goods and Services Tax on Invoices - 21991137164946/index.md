**Use this article to**

Understand tax on goods and services present on invoices and where the merchant can locate them on their financial actions by date range report.

## INTRODUCTION 💬

For entities in some regions, such as Australia, Goods and Services Tax (GST) is applied to processing fees.

- Invoices for these entities will include an additional row in the summary and fee breakdown, showing the tax amount in the invoice holding currency.

- If a merchant's invoice holding currency differs from their local tax currency, the exchange rate used is specified at the end of the summary page.

- This rate is the average point-in-time exchange rate between the two currencies for the financial actions within the invoice period.

- The exact tax amount in their local tax currency is detailed in the Tax Currency Amount column of the financial actions by date range report.
