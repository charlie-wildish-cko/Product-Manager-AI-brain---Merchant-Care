**Use this article when**

You're an Level 1 Merchant Care agent and a Merchant reaches out to request a breakdown of fee charges on their invoice.
INTRODUCTION TO INVOICE  QUERIES 💬 

If a merchant requests a detailed clarification about fees on their invoices, the Merchant Care L1 team should not handle the request directly. 

Instead, escalate the case to the FTS L2 team. The FTS L2 team will help with these detailed queries and will communicate with the merchant directly to resolve the issue.
PROCESS FOR ESCALATING AN INVOICE QUERY 🖊️

- 
**Escalate to FTS L2:** In Zendesk, create a side conversation with the **L2 FTS macro**

- 
**Case Handling:** The FTS L2 team will manage the request directly with the merchant and will close the loop with them once the case is resolved
