**When to use this article**

For troubleshooting steps for a merchant who cannot find their invoices on the Dashboard.

⚠️ A Gateway Services only Merchant who is not configured for Gateway pricing model will not generate invoices on DashboardINTRODUCTION TO INVOICES ON THE DASHBOARD 💬 

The merchant can find their invoices on the dashboard by following these steps:

- Go to the Dashboard

- Select Funds from the navigation menu on the left side of the screen

- Click on Invoices

💡Once on the Invoices page, merchants have two options for downloading:

- Download a single invoice: Click the "Download" button on the right side of a specific invoice line

- Download multiple invoices: Tick the box on the left side of the screen for each invoice you want to download, then click the "Download Invoice" button

 

⚠️ If the merchant contacts us because they can't find these, the primary issue is usually the absence of a "Default Invoice Report" in the merchant's account settings, but it could also be due to the merchant's pricing model.PROCESS TO TROUBLESHOOT THIS ISSUE 🖊️

If the merchant can't find their invoices, it could be due to a configuration issue with the account's reporting profile. 

### Step 1: Check the Default Invoice Report Setting

- Go to the Client Admin Tool ( CAT)

- Search the merchant account name

- Open the relevant Entity

- On the left panel and look for the reporting profiles

- Under reporting profiles, check if ( default invoice report) is set to YES

### Step 2: Take Action Based on the Report Status

- This is a known configuration issue

### Step 3: Check for Gateway Pricing Model

Where the merchant is not configured for a Gateway pricing model, invoices will not be generated on the Dashboard. You can check this by following these steps:

- Go to CAT > Merchant Account Name > Entity > Pricing Profiles > Payment > Card Fees

- Look for Payment Gateway fees

- Where zero Gateway fees for all card payment methods shows, the merchant is not able to generate invoices
