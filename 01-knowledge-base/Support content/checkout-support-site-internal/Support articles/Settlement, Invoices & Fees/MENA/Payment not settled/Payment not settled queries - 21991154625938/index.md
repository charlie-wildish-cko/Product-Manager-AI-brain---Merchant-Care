## Process Steps

1. Check if the processor of the transaction is gateway or non-gateway by following the guidance provided in the section above 

2. If the processor of the transaction is Non-Gateway: 

  1. Contact the Payments team for them to investigate this, 

  2. Once the Payments team has performed their investigation, they will come back to the Merchant Care agent, who is then responsible for communicating the outcome with the merchant and closing the case. 

3. If the processor of the transaction is Gateway: 

  1. Advise the merchant that they must raise the issue with their acquirer since they are the ones managing their settlements. 

  
**Note**: Merchant Care could start the conversation with the acquirer on the merchant’s behalf using the TPA contact information [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ipGueNYij7yEEr1y9S-0Ba0lqS9Ia3Wbsnm6YuvwoaQ/edit#gid=1449834641), however, it is best practice to encourage merchants to contact their acquirer directly so that in the future they reach out to them directly in the first instance.
