**Use this article when**

The Reconciliation team contacts you about a transaction discrepancy and a lack of settlement to a merchant.INTRODUCTION TO ADJUSTMENT REQUESTS 💬

This article explains how to handle a certain type of payment discrepancy that happens when a payment is received from a bank, but the matching settlement from Checkout.com to the merchant hasn't been completed. 

Our Merchant Care L1 team will check with the merchant to confirm if the service or goods were provided to the customer, as this helps us decide the right next steps.PROCESS 🖊️ 

### Step 1: Contact the Merchant

- Reach out to the merchant to confirm if they delivered the service or goods to the customer for the transaction in question.

- You can also ask the Customer Success Manager (CSM) to assist in sharing the transaction file with the merchant for confirmation.

- This step is crucial because even if a transaction failed on the dashboard, it may still have been captured by the payment gateway (MPGS), and the merchant might have fulfilled the order without realizing the dashboard issue.

### Step 2: Take Action Based on Merchant Response

There are two possible outcomes based on the merchant's confirmation:

**✅ If the merchant **_**did**_** deliver the service/goods:** 

- The transaction needs to be settled manually by Checkout.com

- You must contact the Payments team and request a manual adjustment use the Transfer Macro - Treasury - Confirm on CAT to which region the payment belongs to

- Once the Payments team confirms the adjustment is complete, they will notify you. You can then inform the merchant that the payment has been settled.

**❌ If the merchant **_**did not**_** deliver the service/goods:** 

- The merchant may want to refund the customer to prevent dissatisfaction or a potential chargeback

- You must contact the Reconciliation team and inform them of the refund request via Reconciliation transfer macro in Zendesk

- The Reconciliation team will check the transaction status, ensuring a refund is processed correctly and preventing any duplication of payments. They will verify if the payment was settled, if a chargeback is in progress, or if a refund has already been issued.

- Once all checks are clear, the Reconciliation team will process the refund and notify you

- You can then inform the merchant that the refund has been processed, which helps reduce the risk of chargebacks

ESCALATION ⬆️

If you encounter a complex situation not covered in this guide, or if the Reconciliation team requires additional information, escalate the issue to your team lead. Be prepared to provide the following:

- The merchant's name and ID

- The transaction ID(s) and amount(s)

- A clear, concise summary of the issue

- The steps you have already taken
