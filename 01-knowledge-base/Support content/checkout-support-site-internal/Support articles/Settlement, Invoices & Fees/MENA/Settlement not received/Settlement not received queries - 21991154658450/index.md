## Process Steps

**Commonly seen queries: **

Merchants who see in the Dashboard that the status of their settlement is “Completed” but who have not received the settlement would come to Merchant Care asking for help with this. 

**Solving the queries: **

1. 
**Gateway merchants:**

  1. CKO is not able to assist with this for gateway merchants since we do not handle their settlements.

  2. Merchant Care should advise the merchant that they must raise the issue with their acquirer since they are the ones managing their settlements. 

2. 
**Non-gateway merchants:**

  1. For non-gateway merchants, Merchant Care should

    1. Take into account the payout cycle to ensure enough time has elapsed for the payment to have taken place since the statement was generated: 

      1. By default, CKO pays all its merchants using the following payout cycle (where S is the statement generation date): 

        1. For AED, payments are made on S+2 days

        2. All other currencies’ payments are made on S+1 day

          1. Note: In addition, please remember that international payments take one day to reach the beneficiary account  

    2. If enough time has elapsed since the statement generation date, raise the issue with the Payments team and ask for proof of payment or a SWIFT copy - this would act as proof that CKO has indeed sent the settlement to the merchant’s account 

    3. Once the payments team replies to Merchant Care with the relevant information/proof of settlement, Merchant Care is then responsible for transmitting this information to the merchant and solving the case on their end.   
 

**For other non-gateway settlement not received queries, please see the ******[ROW section](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/sections/21991150886162-Settlement-not-received)** in the Support Center.**
