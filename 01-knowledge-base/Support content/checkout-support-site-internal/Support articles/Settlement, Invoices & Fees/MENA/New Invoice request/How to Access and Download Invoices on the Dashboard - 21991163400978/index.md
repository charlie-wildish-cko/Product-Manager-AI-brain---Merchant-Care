**Use this article to**

Talk the merchant through how to access and download an invoice in their dashboard.INTRODUCTION TO INVOICES 💬

Merchants might reach out if they're having trouble finding or downloading their invoices. They can easily access and download their invoices themselves through their dashboard. This article will help you guide a merchant on how to view and download their invoices directly from their dashboard.

💡 See the [merchant facing article: invoices](https://www.checkout.com/docs/funds-management/retrieve-financial-reports/invoices) for more detail on how to understand the content of an invoicePROCESS FOR ACCESSING AND DOWNLOADING AN INVOICE THROUGH THE DASHBOARD🖊️

### Step 1. Accessing an invoice

Merchants can access and download their invoices from the dashboard without needing to contact support. Talk the merchant through these steps:

- 
 
**Log In**: First, log in to your dashboard

- 
 
**Navigate to Invoices**: In the top-left corner, click **Business account** and then select **Invoices** from the dropdown menu

- 
 
**View Invoices**: A list of all your available invoices will appear

### Step 2. Downloading an Invoice

- 
 
**Single Invoice**: To download just one, click the **"Download"** button on the right side of that specific invoice's line

- 
 
**Multiple Invoices**: To download several at once, check the box on the left side of each invoice you want, and then click the **"Download Invoice"** button
