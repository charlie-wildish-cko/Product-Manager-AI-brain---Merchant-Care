## Process Steps

1. If the merchant says that the **invoices found on the Dashboard are not what they are looking for**:

  1. The Merchant Care team should contact one of the below teams so that they can assist and create the required invoices for the merchant:

    1. [menataxinvoices@checkout.com](mailto:mena.receivables@checkout.com)

    2. [mena.receivables@checkout.com](mailto:mena.receivables@checkout.com)

  2. The relevant team will generate the invoice and reply to Merchant Care with the invoice

  3. Merchant Care can then send the invoice to the merchant and close the ticket on their side.

2. If a merchant is after a **Tax/VAT invoice**:

  1. Merchant Care should contact [menataxinvoices@checkout.com](mailto:menataxinvoices@checkout.com) so that they can generate this invoice

  2. The Tax Invoices team will generate the invoice and reply to Merchant Care with the invoice

  3. Merchant Care can then send the invoice to the merchant and close the ticket from their side. 

  
**Note**: Sometimes the MENA Tax Invoices and the MENA Receivables teams do reply to the merchant directly but the standard way of working for these requests is that the respective team generates the invoice, sends this to Merchant Care and Merchant Care closes the loop with the merchant by sending them the required invoice.
