**When to use this article**

A merchant has contacted us to request a confirmation of their account balance, this is a standard request and they haven't asked for a formal signed document.

## BALANCE CONFIRMATION REQUESTS 💬

Always encourage the merchant to self-serve through their **Dashboard** in the first instance, but be aware** **there are times when they ask for specific types of balance confirmations - see the [balance confirmation introduction](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23046501473042) article for more information.
 

## KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- Merchants can typically self-serve their balance information through the Merchant Dashboard

- Merchant Care team handles requests for standard balance information and educates merchants on how to use the self-service tools

- The Billing team handles formal requests that require signed documents or are addressed to auditors

- Provide month-end balance confirmation two business days after the requested month to ensure accuracy

## BALANCE REQUESTS PROCESS 🖊️

Helping Merchants Find Their Balance Details

The easiest way for both agents and merchants to check their current balance is to follow the below steps:

-  Log in to the Dashboard

- In the Dashboard, go to **Business Account** and then select **Balances**

- Under **Available** you can see the merchant’s balance and if there is a negative balance this will be shown here

 

- Select the **Download **icon next to the report to download it as a CSV file

## TROUBLESHOOTING⚒️

- If the merchant is having issues accessing their dashboard, check the articles in the [user management and access](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/sections/26930276318610-User-Management-Access) section of the knowlesge base.

## ESCALATION** ⏫**

- 
[The merchant explicitly requests a formal, signed document](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23046298190738)
