## Process Steps

If the merchant asks when they will receive the funds, the Merchant Care would need to consult with both the Risk team and the Payments team. 

1. The Risk team can advise if the funds can be released, e.g. at times it could be that the funds can be released early if no more refunds and/or chargebacks are outstanding. Note: if the Risk team provides a specific date for funds to be released, the agent can tell the AM/merchant about this. 

2. 
The Payments team can assist with the release of the funds once the Risk team has given the go ahead. 

  1. 
To contact both teams, use the relevant team’s macro in Zendesk to create a side conversation from your merchant’s ticket. For further information, please see [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CHEC/pages/5991727266/Case+Handling+-+SOP#Emailing-internal-partner-teams%3A). 

3. Once the risk & payments team come back to the Merchant Care agent with a response, the agent is responsible for replying to the merchant and closing the case.
