**When to use this article**

To address merchant queries regarding rolling reserves and security deposits. 

**Problem:** A merchant is questioning a rolling reserve or security deposit on their account. **Solution:** Contact the appropriate internal team to get the necessary information and resolve the merchant's query.
DESCRIBE THE ISSUE 💬

A merchant or their Account Manager (AM) has contacted Merchant Care to ask about a rolling reserve or a security deposit being held on their account. 

KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- Rolling reserves and security deposits are financial measures to manage risk

- For rolling reserve queries, the correct team to contact depends on the merchant's Annual Processing Volume (APV) and/or Exposure

PROCESS FOR HANDLING MERCHANT RESERVE AND SECURITY DEPOSIT QUERIES 🖊️
Step 1. Identify the nature of the query and the relevant team

Determine whether the merchant is asking about a **Rolling Reserve** or a **Security Deposit**

- If the query is about a **Rolling Reserve**, check the merchant's Annual Processing Volume (APV) and Exposure.

  - For merchants with APV $25m and/or Exposure $300k, the relevant team is **Credit Portfolio Management**

  - For merchants that do not meet these thresholds the relevant team is **Risk Monitoring**

  - 
**I**f the query is about a **Security Deposit **the relevant team is the **Payments team**

Step 2. Contact the appropriate team using a Zendesk side conversation

Use the relevant macro to create a side conversation on the merchant's ticket

- For Credit Portfolio Management or Risk Monitoring use the relevant macro

- For the Payments team, use the regional “Treasury Payments” macro

- See the Zendesk guide for [further information on side conversations](link_to_zendesk_guide).

Step 3. Await a response from the internal team

- The internal team will provide the necessary information to address the merchant's query

Step 4. Respond to the merchant and close the case

- Once you have the information, reply to the merchant with the resolution, ensure the merchant's query is fully resolved and then close the ticket

FAQs** ****❓**

What is a rolling reserve?

A rolling reserve is a risk management tool that withholds a percentage of each transaction processed by a merchant. The withheld funds are released after a specified period to mitigate financial risk from potential issues like fraud or bankruptcy.What is a security deposit?

A security deposit is a lump sum of money held to secure a merchant's account against potential financial liabilities. It is different from a rolling reserve as it is not a percentage of each transaction.Why can't I solve this issue myself?

Information regarding rolling reserves and security deposits is sensitive and requires a specific understanding of financial risk and treasury operations. Only the specialized teams have access to the necessary tools and information to provide an accurate and secure response.
