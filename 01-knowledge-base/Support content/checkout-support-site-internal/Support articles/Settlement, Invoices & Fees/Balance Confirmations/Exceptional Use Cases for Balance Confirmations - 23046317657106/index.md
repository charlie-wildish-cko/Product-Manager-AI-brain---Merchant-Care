**When to use this article**

To provide a one-off screenshot or CSV export of a merchant's balance details. It is intended for exceptional cases when a merchant is unable to self-serve, and you have received approval from a manager.

You receive a request from a merchant for a screenshot or CSV export of their account balance information. 

While self-service is the standard procedure for all users, there are rare exceptions where you can provide this to the merchant. See the [balance confirmation introduction](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23046501473042) article for more information.

 

## KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- Encourage merchants to use [self-service articles](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/21991189996434-What-is-my-balance) for assistance whenever possible

- Only provide a screenshot or CSV export as a one-off service in exceptional circumstances

- Always seek guidance and approval from a Team Leader or Manager before proceeding

- Never store local copies of the merchant's balance information

This process outlines the steps to retrieve and provide a merchant with a balance confirmation, either via a screenshot or a CSV export.

## ESCALATION** ⏫**

**Situations Requiring Escalation:**

- The merchant requires a [legally binding or signed document](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23046298190738-Balance-details-on-a-signed-document)

- You are unable to locate the merchant's balance data or the data displayed in the dashboard appears to be incorrect or inconsistent

- 
**Required Information:** Case ID, merchant entity ID, reason for the request, and a summary of the steps already taken. Use the appropriate escalation macro.

- 
**Instructions:** After escalating, stay on the case, notify the merchant of the escalation and monitor for updates

 
 

## FAQs** ****❓**

Why can't I always provide a CSV or screenshot for the merchant?

Merchants are encouraged to use the self-service portal to access their own data. Providing one-off services should only be done in exceptional cases and requires approval. This approach ensures security, consistency, and proper data governance.What if the merchant needs a signed balance confirmation?

Any request for a signed or legally binding document must be escalated to the Billing Team and are not handled by Merchant Care. [Escalate the case by following this process.](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23046298190738-Balance-details-on-a-signed-document)Why must I get manager approval for this?

Manager approval is required to ensure that the request is genuinely exceptional and to maintain a record of manual interventions. This helps to enforce the self-service policy and ensures proper oversight of sensitive data handling.
