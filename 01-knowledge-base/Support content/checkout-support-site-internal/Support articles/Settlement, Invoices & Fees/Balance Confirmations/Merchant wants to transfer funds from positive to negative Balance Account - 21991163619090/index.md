## Process Steps

Merchant Care would need assistance from the Payments team for this request, however before sending the request to the Payments team, the agent should ensure that one of the merchant’s account does indeed have a positive balance.

1. To do this, please follow the steps to check balances in the Dashboard in the “[Terminated merchants who want to know their balance & reserves](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/21991166449170-Terminated-merchants-who-want-to-know-their-balance-reserves)” section.

2. Once this has been confirmed, contact the Payments team by using the relevant regional macro in Zendesk to create a side conversation from your merchant’s ticket. For further information, please see [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CHEC/pages/5991727266/Case+Handling+-+SOP#Emailing-internal-partner-teams%3A).

  1. If the merchant has an AM, make sure that you add the AM in CC for their visibility

3. Once the funds transfer has been done, the Payments team will advise the Merchant Care agent and the agent can advise the merchant and close the case.
