**When to use this article**

A merchant requests a signed balance confirmation document, this must be transferred to the Billing team for handling.

## DESCRIBE THE ISSUE 💬

Agents receive a request from a merchant or an external auditor asking for a formal, signed document confirming account balances. Since Merchant Care is not authorized to provide signed documents, these requests must be escalated to the Billing team.

⚠️ Merchant Care cannot sign or provide formal financial documents

## PROCESS FOR FORMAL BILLING REQUESTS 🖊️

**Step 1. Identify the Formal Request**

- Recognize requests that require a signed document or are addressed to an auditor or regulatory body, examples include balance confirmations, audit confirmations, or other legal/financial documentation.

**Step 2. Transfer the Case to Billing from Zendesk**

- Use the appropriate macro to send the case directly to the Billing team, this is based on the client entity

- APAC>Transfer>Billing

- MENA>Transfer>Billing

- NORAM>Transfer>Billing

- UK>Transfer>Billing

## RESOLUTION ⚒️

- Following these steps ensures that the merchant's request for a formal, signed document is handled by the appropriate team. The case is now in the hands of the Billing team, who will work directly with the merchant to resolve the issue.
