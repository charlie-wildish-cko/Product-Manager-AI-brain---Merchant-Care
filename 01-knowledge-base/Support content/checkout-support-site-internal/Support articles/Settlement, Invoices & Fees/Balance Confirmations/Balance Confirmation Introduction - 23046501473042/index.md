## INTRODUCTION TO BALANCE CONFIRMATIONS** 💬**

Merchants sometimes need confirmation of the balance Checkout.com held for them on a specific date, usually for auditing purposes at the end of the month. This is called a **Balance Confirmation**. 

Always encourage the merchant to self-serve through their **Dashboard** in the first instance, but be aware** **there are times when they ask for specific types of balance confirmations.

**⚠️ ****Important Note:** Minimum Billing Fees and Tiered Pricing Adjustments are applied in NAS on the 1st of the month.  TYPES OF BALANCE REQUESTS & WHO HANDLES THEM 🖊️

| Type of Request | Action/Process | Team Responsible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Balance Confirmation Document | Educate the merchant on [how to access their balance details](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/21991189996434-What-is-my-balance) and export a CSV file from their dashboard. | Merchant Care |
| Past End-of-Day Balance Confirmation | A [balance from a past date](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23046333610898-Past-End-of-Day-Balance-Confirmation), which can be provided using the internal Currency Account Balances tool. | Merchant Care |
| Exceptional Support | Provide a one-time screenshot or CSV export of the balance as merchant unable to self-serve. | Merchant Care |
| Signed Balance Confirmation | Provide a [formal signed document](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23046298190738-Balance-details-on-a-signed-document) confirming the merchant's balance. | Billing Team |
