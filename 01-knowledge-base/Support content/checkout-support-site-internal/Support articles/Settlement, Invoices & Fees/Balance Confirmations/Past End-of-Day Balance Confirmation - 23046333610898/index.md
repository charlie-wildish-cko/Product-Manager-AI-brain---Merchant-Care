**When to use this article**

To confirm a merchant's end-of-day balance for a past date using the internal Currency Account Balances tool. This is for users who require a statement of a previous day's balance.
PAST END-OF-DAY BALANCE CONFIRMATIONS💬

The merchant has requested confirmation of their end-of-day balance from a specific past date. This request is typically for reconciliation purposes or to verify transaction records. 

PAST END-OF-DAY BALANCE CONFIRMATION PROCESS🖊️
Step 1. Access the Currency Account Balances Tool 

- Navigate to the [Currency Account Balances app on Retool](https://retoolprod.mgmt.ckotech.co/apps/6840ab28-c4b7-11ec-909b-bf9c18a51b41/techfinance-finlab/Currency%20Account%20Balances)

Step 2. Locate the Currency Account ID

- If you don't have the currency account ID, find it in the Merchant Dashboard

- Go to **Business account** > **Balances** > **List view**

- Click on the relevant entity name, then select **Account details**

- The currency account ID will be displayed there

Step 3. Search for the Balance

- In the Retool app, enter either the **entity ID** or the **currency account ID** into the search field

- Select **End of Day Balances**

Step 4. Confirm the Date

- Choose the specific date for which the balance confirmation is required

Step 5. Screenshot the Balance

- Take a screenshot of the balance and send it to the merchant
