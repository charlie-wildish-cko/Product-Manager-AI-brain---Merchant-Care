## Process Steps

In these situations, Merchant Care can assist the merchant by following the below steps:

1. 
Merchant Care guides the merchant on how to download their Settlement report from the Dashboard. To access this:

  1. In Dashboard, click on Funds

  2. Select Settlements

  3. Select the respective settlement report and click on it to open it.

2. When providing this information, the agent should also explain the report to the merchant as much as possible.

3. If the merchant insists that they should have received a different amount, Merchant Care should ask the merchant where they obtained the amount from, e.g. which report the value came from. This is because the merchant could be checking the incorrect report and that is why they obtained the wrong number.

  1. Another commonly seen issue is merchants not using filters correctly on the report to obtain the information that they need.

4. In addition to the above, Merchant Care can also talk the merchant through the information in the “[Reconciliation with Checkout.com](https://www.checkout.com/docs/business-operations/retrieve-reports/reconciliation-reports/reconciliation-with-checkout-com)” Documentation page and send this page to the merchant for additional information.

5. If the merchant insists that there is an issue, Merchant Care should contact the Payments team for assistance.

  1. To contact the Payments team, use the relevant regional “Treasury Payments” macro to create a side conversation in Zendesk from your merchant’s ticket. For further information, please see [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CHEC/pages/5991727266/Case+Handling+-+SOP#Emailing-internal-partner-teams%3A).

6. After the payments team has investigated the issue, they will reply to Merchant Care. The Care agent is then responsible for advising the merchant of the case’s outcome and closing the loop with the merchant. 

  
**Note: **If there is an issue with the Settlement report variance, Merchant Care should contact the FTS team.
