**Use this article:**

To explain to a merchant why their disputed transaction appears as negative on the settlement report.

## INTRODUCTION TO THE ISSUE 💬

When a customer disputes a transaction, it'll appear as a negative amount on the settlement report. This guide explains why this happens and what to advise the merchant.

A **disputed transaction**, also known as a **chargeback**, appears as a negative amount on a merchant's settlement report because the funds are **immediately debited from the account** as soon as the dispute is initiated. 

This deduction isn't just the transaction amount itself; it also includes a **separate dispute fee** levied by the card network. This process ensures that the funds are held while the dispute is being investigated, which is a standard part of the chargeback lifecycle.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

**Negative Balance:** A negative amount on your settlement report for a disputed transaction is a **temporary deduction**, not a final loss of funds.

**Immediate Action:** The funds are debited immediately to protect the consumer and hold the money during the investigation.

**ADJM Code:** Look for the **ADJM** (Adjustment) code and "Chargeback" in your settlement reports or financial actions report to confirm the disputed transaction.

**Reversal:** If you win the dispute, the funds will be returned to you, appearing as a positive credit.

## IDENTIFYING A DISPUTED TRANSACTION 👀

You can identify a disputed transaction by checking the **financial actions report**. These transactions are typically marked with a specific indicator code.  A common code you might see is **ADJM**, which stands for **Adjustment**. Check the breakdown type under column AD, it will be marked as Chargeback (ADJM).  

## RESOLUTION ⚒️

If the dispute is resolved in the merchant's favor (meaning the merchant wins the case), the funds, including the original transaction amount and any associated fees, will be returned to their account. This will show as a **positive movement** on a future statement or settlement report.
