**When to use this article:**

To assist a **terminated merchant** with updating their bank account details in CAT when funds have **bounced back** or the merchant proactively reports an issue. It outlines processes for both minor bank detail changes and setting up a completely new bank account.

**Problem:** Terminated merchants lose dashboard access, preventing them from updating bank details, which can cause settlement funds to bounce back. 

**Solution:** Follow the specific workflow (self-service for detail updates or MCR process for new accounts) to update bank details and successfully release the funds.

## DESCRIBE THE ISSUE 💬

A terminated merchant needs to update their bank account details in the CAT system because a settlement payment has bounced back, or they have been informed by their bank that the account is inactive.

Terminated merchants lose access to the dashboard, meaning they cannot update a new bank account themselves. 

## KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- Terminated merchants lose dashboard access, which restricts their ability to update bank details.

- All bank account updates for terminated merchants (new account, inactive account or detail change) must be handled by an Merchant Change Request (MCR) SME who will raise a non-dashboard MCR case.

- Merchant Care agents must transfer the case to the MCR queue after collecting the required updated details.

- 
If the bank account is the same, with the _exact same details_ and funds still bounce, the Treasury team must investigate the root cause.
 

 

## PROCESS FOR CHANGING BANK ACCOUNT POST-TERMINATION 🖊️

### Step 1. Identify Issue and Request Updated Bank Details

**Action:** The case is initiated either by the Treasury team identifying a bounce or the merchant reporting it.

**Instructions:**

- 
**M**erchant Care/AM contacts the merchant to advise that a bank issue (bounce) has been identified.

- Inform the merchant that due to their terminated status they no longer have access to the dashboard, all bank updates  must be handled internally via a specialist team.

- Request the full, new/updated bank account details and any necessary supporting documentation (e.g., a recent bank statement or letter).

### Step 2. Escalate Case to the MCR Queue

**Action:** Collect the necessary information and prepare the case for the MCR SME.

**Instructions:**

- The Merchant Care agent must transfer the case to the MCR queue. 

- The MCR SME will use the collected information to raise a non-dashboard MCR using this process: [NAS MCRs where merchant care is the requestor.](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/x/lQAVZAE)

_⚠️_If the bank account remains the same with the exact same details and the bounce still happened, proceed to the escalation section

## ESCALATION ⏫

- If the bank account remains the same with the exact same details, and the funds bounce again the **Treasury team must investigate the root cause**. 

- 
**Agents can check with the Treasury team** to see if there are any comments from the merchant's bank that could help with the investigation. 

**Instructions for the agent working the case:**

- If transferring to the MCR queue, **notify the merchant** of the next steps and expected timeframe.

- If the Treasury team needs to investigate a repeat bounce, monitor the case for their findings and **notify the merchant** of any updates.

**How to follow up and when to chase:**

- Follow up with the MCR SME or Treasury team based on the agreed-upon SLA for the respective queue/task.
