## Introduction

The process is similar to when a terminated merchant wants to have their account re-activated, but instead of fully reinstating the account, we are only providing the merchant with access to; refunds where the transaction has already been captured as well as access to manage chargebacks.

As a result, the merchant will not be fully operational with us again; they will only have access for refund and chargeback purposes. 

## Process Steps

1. Please send the request to the Risk Monitoring team via a side conversation in Zendesk (cc the AM/CSM/requester for tiers 1, 2, and 3) to inquire whether this is feasible. Here, we are seeking their approval before proceeding to the Merchant Configuration team

2. The Risk Monitoring team will review the request. If it is determined that the termination request originated from another team, Risk Monitoring may instruct you to consult that team as well. In such cases, please initiate a separate side conversation with the relevant team.

3. 
**If approval is granted**, please submit the request via a side conversation to the Merchant Configuration team, who will take the necessary actions. Once they have completed the request, they will notify you. Please ensure that you relay this information to the merchant and close the ticket accordingly.

4. 
**If approval is not granted**, please inform the merchant and close the ticket.
