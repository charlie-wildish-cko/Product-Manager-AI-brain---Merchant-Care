## Process Steps

If terminated merchants contact Merchant Care asking to have their account reactivated, Merchant Care should: 

1. Reach out to the team that requested the merchant’s termination and provide them with the merchant’s reactivation request. For Tiers 1, 2 and 3, please make sure that you CC the AM when reaching out to the team that requested the termination. 

  1. Agents can do this by using the transfer macros in Zendesk to create a side conversation with the relevant team. For further information, please see [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CHEC/pages/5991727266/Case+Handling+-+SOP#Emailing-internal-partner-teams%3A). 

    1. When doing this, agents should ask the relevant team to contact Merchant Configuration directly if the reactivation request is granted from their side. 

2. Once the team has reviewed the merchant’s request, the next set of actions depend on whether the request has been approved or denied: 

  1. If the request is denied: 

    1. For unmanaged merchants: The relevant team will advise Merchant Care of the decision. Merchant Care should advise the merchant that their account cannot be re-activated and solve the ticket.

    2. For managed merchants: The relevant team will advise the merchant’s AM of the decision and the AM should then advise the merchant that their account cannot be re-activated. 

      1. If this happens, Merchant Care can solve the ticket when they hear back on the decision as the AM will communicate with the merchant outside of Zendesk. 

  2. If the request is granted: 

    1. For managed merchants: Once the Merchant Configuration team has reactivated the merchant, they will revert back to the merchant’s AM who will then be responsible for informing the merchant of this.

      1. If the AM wants to manage all communication with the merchant, it is best practice to do an internal note on the case to the AM and close the ticket, leaving the AM to inform the merchant. 

      2. If the AM is in CC but no clear indication has been given on them having sole communication with the merchant, the agent can inform the merchant of the outcome, keep the AM in CC, and solve the ticket. 

    2. For unmanaged merchants: Once the Merchant Configuration team has reactivated the merchant, they will revert to the Merchant Care agent who will then be responsible for informing the merchant of this and solving the ticket.
