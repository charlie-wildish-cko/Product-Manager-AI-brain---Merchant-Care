        How to Get the Most out of Fin Copilot
      
      

        A simple guide to using our intelligent assistant to find the answers
        you need, quickly and accurately.
      
    
    
    
      

## 🤔 1. Ask Good Questions

      

        The quality of the answer depends on the quality of the question.
        Be specific and provide context.
      
      

        
- 
          **Relate to support content:**
          Questions should be answerable by the information sources provided
          to Copilot.
        
        
- 
          **Be clear and detailed:**
          Use specific terms and avoid vague language to prevent confusion.
        
        
- 
          **Provide context:**
          Add background information to your query for a more relevant
          and accurate response.
        
        
- 
          **Use follow-ups:**
          Refine your search and get more detailed information by asking
          follow-up questions.
        
        
- 
          **Think conversationally:**
          Unlike a traditional search, Copilot generates a coherent response
          by processing your full query.
        
        
- 
          **Avoid vague queries:**
          Specificity narrows the information pool and leads to more accurate
          results.
        
      

    
    
    
      

## 🔍 2. Verify the Answer

      

Always confirm the information is correct and trustworthy.
      

        
- 
          **Check the source:**
          Hover over the references to see the original source link and
          ensure the information is reliable.
        
      

    
    
    
      

## ❓ 3. Follow Up Questions

      

        Continue the conversation to refine your search and get more detailed
        information.
      
      

        
- 
          **Maintain context:**
          Copilot remembers previous questions in the thread, allowing
          you to ask related follow-up questions.
          
            **Example:**
            

              
  - 
                **Agent:** How do I issue a refund?
              
              
  - 
                **Copilot:** You can issue a refund
                by doing XYZ.
              
              
  - 
                **Agent:** But what if the order is
                more than 60 days ago?
              
              
  - 
                **Copilot:** If the order is more than
                60 days ago, you can XYZ.
              
            
          
        
      

    
    
      
        

## ✅ 4. What Fin Copilot Can and Can't Do

      
      
        
        
          

## Can do

          

            
- 
              ✅
              Find answers from our knowledge we have given it
            
            
- 
              ✅
              Filter the knowledge source to find the answer
            
            
- 
              ✅
              Summarise ticket comments into a short paragraph
            
            
- 
              ✅
              Paste knowledge answers into a ticket reply to then edit
            
            
- 
              ✅
              Translate reply answers into any language
            
          

        
        
        
          

## Doesn't do

          

            
- 
              ❌
              Look up any data (not planned)
            
            
- 
              ❌
              Report any feedback in the app, we have asked for this
              feature
              though
            
            
- 
              ❌
              Identify out of date knowledge - this is what we need
              you
              to to!
