**When to use this article **

To understand what the Agent Consultant is, what it does and why you should use it. 

Agent Consultant is an AI assistant built into Zendesk. It works alongside you as you handle tickets - surfacing relevant data, suggesting responses, and over time, completing routine tasks on your behalf. You review and approve; it does the legwork.

## Why it matters ✨

Every minute spent switching to another system, searching for policy docs, or looking up payment data is time not spent resolving. The Consultant brings the right information to where you are, in the flow of the ticket, so resolution is faster and more consistent.

## What it does today ▶️

| **Capability** | **What you see** |
| --- | --- |
| Payment data retrieval | When a ticket contains a payment ID, the Consultant automatically posts an internal note with payment metadata - status, outcome, and key details so you don't need to leave Zendesk to look it up. |
| Knowledge retrieval for payins | Relevant answers from public and internal knowledge sources surfaced in context as you work a ticket. |

## What's coming next ⏩

- 
**Automated lookups** — TPA payment status queries without manual searching

- 
**Automated actions** — Refund reversals triggered from within Zendesk, with your review and approval

- 
**SOP-driven answers** — Explanations for Payins and Payouts queries pulled directly from agent SOPs

## How it connects to your existing tools 🔄

The Consultant builds on the Agent Toolkit you already use:

- 
**User Profile** — requester identity, dashboard role, SSO status, merchant relationship

- 
**Payment Tool** — payment context from ticket content (this is the current data retrieval layer)

The Consultant adds the AI layer on top: suggestions, summarisation, automated actions.

For any action that changes data like refunds, reversals, third-party API calls - you will review and approve before anything is sent or executed. The Consultant is an advisor and executor under supervision, not an autonomous agent.
