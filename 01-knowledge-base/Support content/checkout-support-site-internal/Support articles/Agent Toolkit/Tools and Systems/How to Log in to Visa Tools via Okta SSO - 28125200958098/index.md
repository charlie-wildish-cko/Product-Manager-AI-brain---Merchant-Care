**When to use this article:**

This article explains the process for logging into Visa Access and Visa Resolve using Okta Single Sign-On (SSO).

## INTRODUCTION TO VISA TOOLS  LOGIN METHOD** 💬**

To improve security and streamline access, the login method for Visa Access and Visa Resolve must be done through Okta Single Sign-On (SSO) credentials. This login process aligns with our enhanced security protocols.

 
**KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑**
The login method for Visa Access and Visa Resolve has switched from LastPass to **Okta SSO**. Your old LastPass credentials will no longer work for these tools.

**PROCESS FOR LOGGING INTO VISA 🖊️**

Follow these steps to access Visa Access and Visa Resolve using the new Okta SSO method

💡 **Tip:** You may have received an account activation email from Visa. Please disregard this email, as access is now managed directly through Okta.
**Step 1. Log in to Okta**

- Navigate to your Okta dashboard

- Log in using your standard Checkout.com credentials

**Step 2. Access the Visa Application**

- From your Okta dashboard, go to the **My Apps** section

- Find and click on the application named **Visa**

- You will be automatically logged into the Visa Access portal

✅ **Best Practice:** Once in Visa Access you can switch between regions by clicking on your name in the top menu bar
**Step 3. Open Visa Resolve**

- Inside the Visa Access portal, locate the **My Services** section

- Click on the **Visa Resolve** tool to open it and begin reviewing transaction information

## ESCALATION** ⏫**

**When to Escalate via Jira:**

- You can't log in to Visa Access via the Okta application

- You can log in to Visa Access, but the Visa Resolve tool is missing or will not open

- You do not have the expected permissions or access levels within either tool
