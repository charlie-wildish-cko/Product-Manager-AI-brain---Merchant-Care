Understand Gateway, Chechout.com's productINTRODUCTION TO GATEWAY 💬

**Gateway** is the core technology that lets businesses securely accept online credit and debit card payments. Think of it as a secure bridge that sends a customer's payment information from a website to the bank for approval.

What makes Checkout.com's Gateway unique is that it's part of a single, integrated platform. The Gateway works seamlessly with our other services—the **Processor** and **Acquirer**—to provide a full-stack solution with one simple integration. This single connection means a more reliable, efficient, and secure payment experience for you and your customers.KEY FEATURES & BENEFITS OF GATEWAY⭐️

### Simplified Integration

We offer several ways to connect to our Gateway, so Merchants can choose the best option for their business:

- 
**Unified Payments API:** For larger businesses with a technical team, our powerful API allows for full control over the payment process.

- 
**Hosted Payment Page:** A secure, pre-built payment page that we host. It's the easiest way to accept payments and simplifies your security compliance (PCI).

- 
**Payment Links:** Quickly create and share secure payment links with your customers via email, text, or social media—no website needed.

- 
**E-commerce Plugins:** Easily connect to popular platforms like Shopify and Magento to manage payments directly from your store.

- 
**Virtual Terminal:** A simple online interface that lets you manually process payments over the phone.

### Security and Reliability

Our Gateway is built to keep payments secure and protect the merchant's business. It uses **tokenization** to replace sensitive card data with a secure "token," reducing fraud and simplifying security compliance. It also works with our fraud detection and risk management tools to help identify and block risky transactions before they're completed.

### Optimized Performance

The Gateway is a key part of our payment optimization technology, which helps increase approval rates. It intelligently routes transactions, handles retries, and manages other optimizations in the background to improve the chances that a customer's payment will be successful.
