**When to use this article**

If an account manager or a merchant asks how the Intelligent Acceptance product will perform for them.

**Problem: **Merchants and sales teams need to know the expected performance uplift for Intelligent Acceptance.

**Solution: **Do not provide specific performance predictions; instead explain the variable factors and **propose a risk-free trial **to demonstrate value.DESCRIBE THE ISSUE 💬

Merchants and commercial teams often ask for an estimated performance improvement or a comparison with similar merchants for the Intelligent Acceptance product before deciding to use it. 

Since every merchant's setup is different, we can’t guarantee a specific acceptance rate (AR) increase, and making such promises could affect trust if the expected results aren’t achieved.

⚠️ Instead, propose a risk-free trial as the most accurate way to demonstrate the product's valueKEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- Avoid providing specific predictions or benchmarks for Intelligent Acceptance performance

- Explain that performance is highly variable and depends on unique factors for each merchant, such as settings, traffic type and transaction volume

- Propose a risk-free trial as the most accurate way to demonstrate the product's value

- Shift the conversation from "what will it do" to "what can it potentially do" by analyzing the merchant's traffic for optimization opportunities

MANAGING IA PERFORMANCE EXPECTATIONS 🖊️

This process outlines how to respond to requests for performance predictions and benchmarking and set clear, accurate expectations for the Intelligent Acceptance product. 

We don’t have a way to predict performance for a merchant in a reliable way. 

It’s really important not to try to project optimizations not make commitments about authorization rates. If they still insist L2 should escalate. 

**Acknowledge and Redirect**

- Politely inform the requestor that we cannot provide a precise, upfront estimate of the AR or revenue boost Intelligent Acceptance will deliver for a specific merchant.

**Explain the Variability**

- Clarify that performance is highly variable due to factors unique to each merchant:

  - Merchant-specific settings and setup (e.g., permissions, add-ons)

  - Type of traffic (e.g., recurring vs. customer-initiated)

  - Transaction volume

  - Merchant Category Code (MCC)

  - Use of third-party orchestrators

- Explain that because of these variables, one merchant's performance is not a reliable indicator for another 

**Propose a Risk-Free Trial**

- Position a free trial as the most accurate way to determine the product's impacts o the merchants can observe their own volume and this should be driven by AM.

- Explain that a trial allows the merchant to see the actual performance and value without financial commitment

- Recommend a trial period of at least one month to gather statistically significant data

**Analyze and Shift to Potential**

- Instead of offering a number, use available tools to analyze the merchant's traffic and identify specific areas for potential optimization - this builds a business case without making a promise.

- Investigate questions like:

  - Could they benefit from Network Tokens, AFTs, or RTAU?

  - Can their current 3DS setup be upgraded?

  - Could Intelligent Acceptance manage exemptions or retries for them?

RESOLUTION ⚒️

The expected result is that the merchant and commercial team will have a clear understanding of why performance cannot be predicted upfront and will agree to a risk-free trial to accurately measure the product's value.
