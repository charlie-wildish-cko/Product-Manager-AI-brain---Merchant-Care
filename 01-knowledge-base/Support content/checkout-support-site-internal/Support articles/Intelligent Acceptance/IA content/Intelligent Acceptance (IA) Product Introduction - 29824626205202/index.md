**When to use this article**

To understand our Intelligent Acceptance product, how it works and who it's suitable for.INTRODUCTION TO INTELLIGENT ACCEPTANCE 💬
Intelligent Acceptance (IA) is a product designed to optimize payment performance by maximizing the proportion of successful payment attempts, known as the **Acceptance Rate (AR)**, while minimizing associated costs and risks. It uses a live machine-learning (ML) model to optimize payment flows based on a merchant's goals. 
**Function and Role in the Payment Flow: **Intelligent Acceptance is a key part of the payment authorization process, working together with other Checkout.com products like Fraud Detection and Authentication..
 
**Strategy and Modification: **The main module of Intelligent Acceptance sets the strategy for each payment, while other modules guide how to adjust the payment at each step of the process..

**Optimization Stages:** Intelligent Acceptance applies improvements both __before__ payments are sent to card schemes and issuers (pre-processing) and after declines (post-processing), including optimized retries..
 
**Maximizing Performance:** The best payment performance comes from reducing failures, which usually fall into three groups:

- Technical Issues

- Risk Assessment (such as risky or fraudulent transactions)

- Customer Related issues (like incorrect information, limits, or drop-offs)

Intelligent Acceptance offers strategies to improve performance in all these areas. HOW INTELLIGENT ACCEPTANCE WORKS 💬

Intelligent Acceptance integrates into the payment authorization flow, where it analyzes and modifies transactions. It applies a main module that determines the strategy for each payment, while other modules provide instructions on what to change at each step. The tool applies and combines multiple types of optimizations, categorized as either pre-processing or post-processing features.

**Pre-processing features** modify payments within the Checkout.com flow before they are sent to card schemes and issuers. These include:

- 
**Routing**: Finds the best path for each transaction based on goals like cost and conversion

- 
**Messaging**: Optimizes data and formatting to meet the latest scheme and issuer preferences

- 
**Network Tokens**: Uses continuous testing to balance cost and conversion by staying up-to-date on issuer token adoption

- 
**SCA 3DS**: Determines if Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) is needed and selects the best-performing protocol and exemptions

**Post-processing features** are performed in the case of declines.

- 
**Retries**: Automatically attempts to recover transactions that were declined in real-time based on the probability of success.
INTELLIGENT ACCEPTANCE  FAQs ⁉️ Who is Intelligent Acceptance for?

**Non-Experts:** For merchants without the resources or expertise to develop and maintain an advanced payment strategy, Intelligent Acceptance allows them to be hands-off while maintaining compliance and top performance.

 

**Experts:** Merchants with advanced knowledge and dedicated payment teams can leverage IA on top of their existing rules and internal solutions. IA can optimize data fields not accessible to merchants, respond dynamically to changes in the payment ecosystem, and use data from Checkout.com's network.

 

 Why Choose Intelligent Acceptance?

Merchants should choose Intelligence Acceptance because it offers:

**First Attempt Optimization**: IA optimizes payments from the first attempt, aiming to maximize acceptance rates and minimize the need for retries and associated costs.

 

**Clarity and Partnership**: The solution provides detailed insights into the boosts it provides, without a "black-box" approach.

 

**Dynamic and Live ML Model**: IA uses a machine-learning model that continuously evolves to cover more use cases.
