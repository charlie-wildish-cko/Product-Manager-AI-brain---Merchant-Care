## Process Steps

_This SOP outlines the process for handling queries related to schemes._

1. 
**Log the Details of the Inquiry**

  1. When a scheme-related inquiry is received, the Merchant Care team will log the merchant's name and details of the transaction in question.

2. 
**Access Looker to Locate the Transaction**

  1. Use the Looker link to find the relevant transaction: [Looker Link](https://checkoutinternal.eu.looker.com/looks/11421).

  2. Apply appropriate filters, such as:

    1. Transaction date

    2. Merchant identification (name/descriptor/client name)

    3. Scheme type

3. 
**Responding to the Inquiry**

  1. If the merchant’s contact details are available:

    1. Draft a clear and concise email addressing the scheme inquiry.

    2. Include transaction details, resolution steps taken, and any other pertinent information.

4. 
**Forward to the CSM if Merchant Details are Unavailable**

  1. If the merchant is within Tier 1 to Tier 3 and their contact details are unavailable:

    1. Forward the inquiry to the appropriate Customer Success Manager (CSM).

    2. Provide the CSM with all relevant information to facilitate their follow-up with the merchant.

5. 
**Request Additional Information if Necessary**

  1. If a transaction cannot be located due to insufficient information, respond to the sender requesting more details to help identify the merchant and the specific transaction.

_Note: that inquiries related to schemes have been minimal. This SOP is based on recent cases that required Merchant Care's input. The handling process may evolve depending on the specifics of future inquiries._
