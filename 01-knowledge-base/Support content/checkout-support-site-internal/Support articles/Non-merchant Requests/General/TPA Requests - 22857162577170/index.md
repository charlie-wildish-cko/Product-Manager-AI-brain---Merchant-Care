## Process Steps

_This SOP outlines the process for managing inquiries related to Third-Party Agents (TPAs)._

1. 
**Log the Inquiry Details**

  1. When a TPA inquiry is received, the Merchant Care team will capture the merchant’s name along with the specific transaction details.

2. 
**Locate the Transaction in Looker**

  1. Use the Looker link to find the relevant transaction: [Looker Link](https://checkoutinternal.eu.looker.com/looks/11421).

  2. Apply appropriate filters such as:

    1. Transaction date

    2. Merchant identification (name/descriptor/client name)

    3. Encrypted card number

3. 
**Request Additional Information if Transaction Cannot Be Located**

  1. If the transaction cannot be found due to insufficient information, respond to the inquiry by requesting more details from the sender to assist in identifying the correct transaction.

4. 
**Respond if Merchant Contact Information is Available**

  1. If the merchant's contact details are available:

    1. Draft a clear and concise email addressing the TPA inquiry.

    2. Include all relevant transaction details and any necessary resolution information.

5. 
**Refer to Primary Contacts for Unmanaged Merchants**

  1. For unmanaged merchants, refer directly to the primary contact(s) as needed to resolve the inquiry.

6. 
**Escalate to Customer Success Manager (CSM) if Contact Information is Unavailable**

  1. If contact details are unavailable for merchants within Tier 1 to Tier 3:

    1. Escalate the inquiry to the appropriate CSM.

    2. Provide the CSM with a complete overview of the inquiry and all collected information to facilitate their follow-up with the merchant.

_**Note**: that inquiries related to TPAs have been infrequent. This SOP was developed based on the most recent cases requiring input from Merchant Care. The approach may vary depending on the specific situation._
