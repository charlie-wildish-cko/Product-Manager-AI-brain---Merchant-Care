**When to use this article**

This article outlines the process for managing non-standard compliance alerts-including Merchant Category Code (MCC) misclassification and clarifies the role of the Scheme Compliance Team in resolving these cases.

## INTRODUCTION **💬**

The **Scheme Compliance Team** is responsible for investigating and managing miscellaneous non-compliance cases that fall outside of the regular compliance programs (like BRAM/VIRP) but still represent a breach of card scheme rules or Checkout.com's acquiring obligations.

**Problem: **The merchant's assigned MCC is incorrect and does not accurately reflect their primary line of business or services. This can result in:

-  Incorrect interchange and processing fees

- Misapplied card scheme rules (e.g., incorrect risk parameters)

- Misleading classification of the merchant to the card networks

See an [example ticket here.](https://checkout1360.zendesk.com/agent/tickets/101353)

### **How Cases are Identified**

These cases are typically identified through:

- Formal scheme notifications from card networks

- Internal monitoring and audits

- Escalations from internal business teams (e.g., Underwriting, Risk, Account Management)

## ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES 💫

The Merchant Care Team's role is to escalate incorrect MCC notifications from the Card Schemes to the Scheme Compliance Team.

The Scheme Compliance Reactive Risk Team's role is to act as the internal expert and primary driver for resolution:

- 
**Investigation:** They conduct a case-by-case assessment and gather evidence to validate the non-compliance claim.

- 
**Remediation:** They develop and execute corrective action plans (e.g., correcting the MCC, ensuring TPA registration, or updating merchant data).

- 
**Liaison:** They coordinate communication and documentation internally with relevant teams and externally with the card schemes.

- 
**Closure:** They ensure all identified issues are addressed in a consistent, timely, and auditable manner, applying appropriate corrective actions to mitigate ongoing risk and prevent fines or penalties.

 

## ESCALATION** ⏫**

- Escalate the case to [schemecompliance@checkout.com](mailto:schemecompliance@checkout.com) via a side conversation in Zendesk.

- The Scheme Compliance analyst will review the case and determine if the assigned MCC was correct, potentially contacting Underwriting team if needed

- 
The Scheme Compliance Analyst will responds to the card scheme directly and owns and own any further follow-up.
