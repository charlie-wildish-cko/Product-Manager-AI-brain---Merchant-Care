## Process Steps

1. 
**Refrain from Direct Communication**

  1. Do not reply directly to the police body making the request.

2. 
**Locate the Transaction**

  1. Use the provided information to try and identify the transaction if sufficient details are available.

  2. Use this Looker link to find the transaction: [Looker Link](https://checkoutinternal.eu.looker.com/looks/11421).

  3. In case the variables you need is not present in the actual visualization under Filters, click on ‘’Explore from here.’’

  4. Next, search for the variable you need, as shown in the screenshot below. Once you locate it, click on the "Filter by field" icon, and the variable will appear in the Filter section. In this example, I used reference:  

3. 
**Forward the Request**

  1. Once the transaction is identified, forward the complete request and transaction details to the Merchant Reviews team. To do this, follow the steps below:

    1. Click on “macros” at the bottom of the Zendesk ticket and select “Merchant Reviews.”

    2. A side conversation with the Merchant Reviews team will open. Choose “Ticket comments” to send the entire communication trail to the team.

_**The Merchant Reviews team will handle the ticket and respond directly to the police body. **_**Merchant Care should solve the ticket immediately after sending the email in the side conversation. **
