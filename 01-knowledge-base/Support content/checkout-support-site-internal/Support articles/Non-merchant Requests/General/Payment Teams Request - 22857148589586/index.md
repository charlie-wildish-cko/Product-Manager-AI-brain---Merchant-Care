## Introduction

_Occasionally, the internal Payments team may send tickets requesting additional information regarding a payment._

1. To put it simply, think of a payment system as a digital cash register. Occasionally, this system may require additional information to complete a transaction or resolve an issue. In this case, our bank (acting as the payment system) has requested more details about a payment.

2. Our task is to reach out to the merchant (the store or business) to gather the necessary information. Once we obtain these details from the merchant, we relay them back to the bank to resolve the issue. Essentially, when the payment system needs more information to ensure everything is accurate, we step in to retrieve it.

## Process Steps

1. 
**Locate the Merchant’s Contact Information**

  1. Find the merchant’s name in the [sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1HWI9aqXvWJ4AHcA2BteR-otSXcn7bBsTiwYzp9VY2vs/edit?gid=0#gid=0) and email the listed contacts.

2. 
**Follow Best Practice if the Merchant’s Name is Absent:**

  1. Depending on the merchant's tier, proceed as follows:

3. 
**(i) Tier 1-3 Merchants:**

  1. Typically, the Payments team contacts the CSM or merchant directly, but if a request is sent to you:

    1. Forward the request to the CSM using the Zendesk case.

    2. The CSM will handle contacting the merchant for the required information.

    3. Once the merchant responds, the CSM will share the information with you.

    4. Update the Payments team with the information and close the ticket.

4. 
**(ii) Tier 4/Unmanaged Accounts:**

  1. Access Salesforce and search for the merchant account.

  2. Click on “Contacts” on the right-hand side.

  3. Email the primary contact(s) directly.

  
 

1. 
**Transmit Information to the Payments Team**

  1. Once you receive the requested details from the merchant, share it with the Payments team on the same Zendesk ticket.
