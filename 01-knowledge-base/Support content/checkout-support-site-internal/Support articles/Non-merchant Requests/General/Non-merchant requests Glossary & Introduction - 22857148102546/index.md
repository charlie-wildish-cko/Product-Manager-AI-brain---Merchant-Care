## Key Terms & Definitions

| **Term** | **Definition** |
| --- | --- |
| **IDEAL** | iDEAL is a payment method that allows users to make online payments directly from their bank accounts. It is widely used in the Netherlands and requires the user to have a current account with access to the online channel of their bank |
| **Bounced Refund** | A Bounced Refund occurs when a refund is initiated by a merchant, but the credit cannot be posted to the cardholder's account due to some reasons,e.g closed bank account |
