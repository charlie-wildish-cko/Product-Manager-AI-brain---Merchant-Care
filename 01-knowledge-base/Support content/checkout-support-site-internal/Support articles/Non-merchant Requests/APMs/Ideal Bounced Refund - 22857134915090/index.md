## Introduction

_A bounced refund in the context of iDEAL happens when a refund fails, often due to the customer's account being closed or blocked. In these cases, the APM Reconciliation team will issue a request to Merchant Care, which follows the process outlined below:_  
  

## Process Steps

1. 
**Locate Merchant Contact Information**

  1. Find the merchant’s name in the [sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1HWI9aqXvWJ4AHcA2BteR-otSXcn7bBsTiwYzp9VY2vs/edit?gid=0#gid=0) and email the listed contacts.

2. 
**Follow Best Practice if the Merchant’s Name is Absent:**

  1. Depending on the merchant's tier, follow these steps:

3. 
**(i) Tier 1-3 Merchants:**

  1. Typically, the APM Reconciliation team contacts the CSM or merchant directly, but if a request is sent to you:

    1. Forward the request to the CSM using the Zendesk case.

    2. The CSM will manage communication with the merchant and gather the requested information.

    3. Once the merchant responds, the CSM will share the information with you.

    4. Update the APM Reconciliation team with the provided details and close the ticket.

4. 
**(ii) Tier 4/Unmanaged Accounts:**

  1. Access Salesforce and search for the merchant account.

  2. Click on “Contacts” on the right-hand side.

  3. Email the primary contact(s) directly.

  

1. 
**Transmit Information to the APM Reconciliation Team**

  1. Once you receive the requested information from the merchant, send it to the APM Reconciliation team using the same ticket.
