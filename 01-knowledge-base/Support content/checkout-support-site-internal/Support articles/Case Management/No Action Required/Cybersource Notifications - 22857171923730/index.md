## Process Steps

### **Notifications on Cybersource **

Cybersource notifications are typically system-generated alerts or updates related to MID on expired credentials. In most cases, no direct action is required from Merchant Care.

If a notification is received from Cybersource and no action is required, the agent should:

1. 
**Review the notification **to confirm that it is an automated update regarding expired credentials, with no further action required.

2. 
**Send a side conversation to Merchant Configuration** for FYI. 

3. 
**Add a private note to the case **explaining that no action is required from Merchant Care and that the case has been sent to Merchant Configuration for FYI., e.g. _“No action required from Merchant Care, the case has been sent to Merchant Config for FYI.”_

4. 
**Submit the ticket as Solved. **

### **Notifications on Downtime **

Notifications regarding downtime typically provide information about system outages or maintenance periods. These notifications are often for awareness and do not require action from Merchant Care unless specified otherwise.If a notification on downtime is received and no action is required, the agent should:

1. 
**Review the notification** to confirm that it pertains to downtime or maintenance and does not request any specific action or intervention from Merchant Care.

2. 
**Select the macro “No Action Required”** from the “Dispatch” macro folder. Upon submission, this macro will:

  1. 
**Automatically add the following private note** to the ticket:  
_No action required from Merchant Care, hence solving the ticket.”_  
__**Note**: If the notification includes details about expected resolution times or impacts on merchant services that need to be noted for future reference, include this context in the private note before submitting the case.

  2. 
**Automatically close the ticket.**  
**Note: **Only use this macro and submit the ticket if no further action is necessary. If the downtime impacts ongoing issues or requires follow-up, ensure that relevant details are logged appropriately.

### **Notifications for FYI only **

****Notifications marked for "FYI" (For Your Information) are typically sent to inform Merchant Care of updates or changes without requiring any specific action.If a notification for FYI only is received and no action is required, the agent should:

1. 
**Review the notification** to confirm it is intended solely for informational purposes and does not require any direct response or action from Merchant Care.

2. 
**Select the macro “No Action Required”** from the “Dispatch” macro folder. Upon submission, this macro will:

  1. 
**Automatically add the following private note** to the ticket:  
_No action required from Merchant Care, hence solving the ticket.”_  
__**Note**: If the notification includes important updates or information that could impact ongoing or future cases, document this context in slack or private note.

  2. 
**Automatically close the ticket.**  
**Note: **Only use this macro and submit the ticket if no further action is necessary. Ensure any pertinent information is logged if it might affect other cases or processes.
