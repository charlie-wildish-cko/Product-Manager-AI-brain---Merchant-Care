## Introduction

Auto-reply emails are automatically generated by a system. No further action is required by Merchant Care in response to these messages. 

Typical auto-reply emails include:

1. Out-of-office replies,

2. Delivery confirmation emails,

3. Automated support responses, etc.

## Process Steps

**If an auto-reply email is received, the agent should:**

1. 
**Check for relevant context:**  
Confirm that the auto-reply email is indeed a system-generated response and not a follow-up or escalation request. Review the content briefly to ensure no critical information has been provided.

2. 
**Add a private note to the case** explaining that the ticket is an auto-reply and does not require any Merchant Care Input, e.g. _“This ticket is an auto-reply and does not require any Merchant Care input.”_

3. **Submit the ticket as Solved.**
