**When to use this article**

To understand what Quality Assurance QA is and why we do it.

**Quality Assurance (QA)** isn’t about catching people making mistakes, it’s about ensuring we provide a good experience for every merchant, every time.

## **What exactly is QA?**

At its core, QA is the systematic process of reviewing our customer interactions (tickets, chats, or calls) against a set of established standards.

When we audit a ticket, we are looking at the **whole picture**:

- **Accuracy:** Did the merchant get the right information?

- **Tone & Empathy:** Did we sound like humans who care, or like robots following a script?

- **Efficiency:** Was the problem solved quickly without unnecessary back-and-forth?

- **Compliance:** Did we follow security protocols to protect customer data?

## **Why is QA useful? **

QA is a tool designed to support **you** and our merchants. Here’s why we do it:**It Identifies "Knowledge Gaps"**

Nobody knows everything. If audits show that several people are struggling with a new product feature, it tells us that our _training_ or _documentation_ needs to be better. It’s a signal to us to provide more resources, not a reason to blame.**It Ensures Fairness**

QA provides a way to see how we are performing. Instead of "feeling" like a team is doing well, we have data to prove it. This makes performance reviews more objective and ensures that top-tier work is recognized and rewarded.**It Protects the Brand**

Consistency is key to trust. If a customer gets one answer on Monday and a completely different one on Tuesday, they lose faith in us. QA acts as the guardrail that keeps our voice and our facts consistent across the entire team.**It Improves the Merchant Experience**

By reviewing tickets, we spot "friction points" - steps in our process that frustrate merchants. We use these insights to improve our processes.
