## Introduction

Calibration sessions are fundamental to ensuring quality remains consistent and fair for teammates but also allow us to identify critical things such as process gaps or training issues. Below are recommended guidelines for successful calibrations.

## Process Steps

1. Select your calibration type based on your intended goal:-

  1. 
**QA Scoring**: a standard calibration of select tickets to ensure the QA scoring is applied in the same way

  2. 
**Peer (Self-Learning)**: Teammates review each other’s tickets, discuss different approaches and align on best practices and standard operating procedures. Facilitated by Senior Analysts

  3. 
**Onboarding**: Review new hire tickets

  4. 
**Scenario-Based**: Review a specific set of tickets to align on handling and best practices. Helpful in high-impact situations e.g. escalations, dependencies, complaints

  5. 
**Blind**: Review tickets solely based on interaction and resolution, removing the identity of the teammate to eliminate bias

  6. 
**Trend-Based**: Review tickets related to specific trending issues that are recurring to improve consistency and effectiveness

  7. 
**Performance-Based**: Review tickets of teammates who are exceeding or underperforming, to identify best practices to share with the wider teams, or identify areas for improvement for low-performers

  8. 
**Customer Satisfaction**: Review tickets with specific CSAT scores and customer feedback to identify gaps between internal quality and customer perceptions

  9. 
**Incident-Based:** Review tickets to align on response strategies during specific incidents or crises (e.g. service outages, merchant experienced fraud)

2. To set up a successful calibration session:-

  1. Select a **sample** of tickets, and ensure the sample size is a representative view

  2. Remove any **identifying information** to prevent bias (recommended but optional)

  3. 
**Distribute** the tickets to the calibration team 2 days before the calibration session

  4. 
**Add all scores and notes** to the calibration master sheet, highlighting/colour-coding similarities and disparities in evaluations

  5. Calibration team members should note any questions or concerns they might have during their review

3. In the calibration session, begin with a brief review of the quality standards and ensure everyone is aligned on expectations, and:

  1. Discuss **similarities** and refer to reviewer comments to ensure consensus

  2. Discuss **discrepancies** between scores, focusing on understanding different perspectives

  3. Agree on examples of **good** **practices** and **areas** **for** **improvement**, addressing any ambiguities or areas of disagreement

  4. Aim to reach a **consensus** on the final score for each ticket by the end of the discussion

  5. Document any **agreed-upon adjustments** to the scoring rubric or quality standards

  6. Identify and agree on any **remediation** steps required, identifying recurring issues or patterns to address

  7. Discuss potential** root causes** and suggest actionable steps to address these issues

4. At the end of the session, identify post-calibration actions, such as:-

  1. 
**Teammate**:

    1. Adjust scoring and notes if necessary

    2. Feedback downstream to teammates

    3. Remediate any tickets where applicable

  2. 
**Support**

    1. Share process gaps identified with the Process Architect

    2. Share training needs with the Training Business Partner

    3. Share tooling issues with the Product team

  3. 
**Governance**

    1. Adjust the program standards from insights gained (should be signed off at the director level)
