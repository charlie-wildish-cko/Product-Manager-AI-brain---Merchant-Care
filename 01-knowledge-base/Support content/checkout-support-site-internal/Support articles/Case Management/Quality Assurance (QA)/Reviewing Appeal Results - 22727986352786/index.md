## Introduction

Once your appeal has been reviewed, the ticket will be solved and sent to you to inform you of the appeal outcome, via email notification. You can access this by clicking on the link in the email or proceeding to the steps below.

## Process Steps

1. In Zendesk, under “Views”, click on the "My QA" view to see the result for your appeal:-

1. Once you open the ticket, you will see that you are now in the form “QA - Final Results”. Here you will have the below displayed in greyed-out boxes for your review: 

  1. ZD ticket ID for reference

  2. Appeal results/scores of the 4 key areas tested: Communication and Soft Skills, Process Adherence, Resolution, and Critical/Compliance

  3. Total Appeal Score

  4. Result (this is the final QA result after the appeal review)

  5. Reviewer comments (on the right-hand side in the internal note section)

2. As you can only appeal once per QA ticket, this is your final score, the ticket has already been set to Solved and there is no further action you can take from within the ticket. If you are dissatisfied with the final appeal result, please discuss it with your Team Leader.
