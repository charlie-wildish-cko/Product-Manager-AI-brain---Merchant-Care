## Introduction

When completing a review of an appeal, the key things to remember are to thoroughly review the teammate's appeal comments, score each section, and then submit the final overall score.

## Process Steps

1. In Zenesk, click on “Views”, and select the “QC Appeal Queue” view (appeal tickets have an "Open" status):-

1. Locate the ticket that needs an appeal review and open it, at this point, you will note the following: 

  1. The form on the ticket will now be called "QA - Final Review” - this form is identical to the “QA - Initial Review” form, however, it is to be used for appeals only

  2. There will be an internal comment to inform you of who the first reviewer was. This is so that the Team Leads can if they wish, ensure that a different Lead reviews the appeal

2. Review the teammate's appeal comments to understand why they are appealing the QA result

3. Locate the “ZD ticket ID” and review the teammate’s original ticket

4. In the QA review, go through each of the 4 sections and score them just as you did in the original review form:-

  1. Communication and Soft skills

  2. Process Adherence

  3. Resolution

  4. Compliance

    1. Note: If required, you can check the previous scores scored by changing the form to “QA - Initial Review”

5. If you need to make any corrections to a score that you have just submitted:-

  1. Go back to the relevant “Ticket stage”

  2. Change the specific score

  3. Tick the "Re-score" box to update the scores

  4. Click “Submit as **Open**” to resubmit the updated score

6. Once each of the 4 sections has been scored:-

  1. Select “Scoring” in the Ticket “Stage” drop-down box to see:-

    1. A re-cap of the scores/results for each section

    2. The total score, including a percentage of the score obtained

    3. The overall appeal “Result”

7. If the overall score is now a "Pass", leave comments in the internal note section advising the teammate of the score change and why

8. If the original score remains the same, please advise why in the internal note section (e.g. whether you are aligned with the previous score, or there were some improvements but not enough for an overall pass)

9. Change the ticket status to "Solved" to complete the appeal review process
