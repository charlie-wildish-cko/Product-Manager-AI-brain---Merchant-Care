## Introduction

When a QA review is completed on your ticket, you will receive an email notification of your score. You can access this review by clicking on the link in the email or proceeding to the steps below.

## Process Steps

1. In Zendesk, under “Views”, click on the "My QA" view to see QA results for your tickets:-

1. When you open the ticket, you will see that both the Reviewer and the Assignee are under your name - this is normal because: 

  1. The reviewer is always the person whose work is being reviewed, e.g. your ticket is being quality-reviewed 

  2. The assignee is the person working on the ticket. Since your QA results have been sent to you for your review, you are also the assignee

2. Review the initial QA result, including the following:-

  1. Details of the **Assignee** (i.e. you) and **Requester** (i.e. the person whose work is being reviewed, also you)

  2. 
**ZD ticket ID** is the ticket that you worked on and is being quality-reviewed,

  3. Results/scores of the 4 areas tested:-

    1. Communication and soft skills

    2. Process adherence

    3. Resolution

    4. Critical/Compliance (please note this can only be pass or fail)

  4. Total Score

  5. QA result (pass or fail)

  6. Fail Reason

  7. Reviewer comments (on the right-hand side in the internal note section)

1. If you agree with the QA result, please skip to step 9. If you disagree with the QA result, please continue to step 5:

2. If you disagree with the QA result, tick the "Appeal Review Result" box

  1. 
**Note**: You can only appeal once on a QA ticket

3. In the “Appeal Reason” drop-down box, select why you believe the QA assessment was incorrect

  1. If you have multiple reasons for your appeal, please select “Other”

4. Under “Internal note”, provide specific reasons and justification as to why you are appealing:-

1. 
Submit the appeal as “Open” to have the ticket reviewed again:-

2. If you agree with the QA result, mark the ticket as "Solved" and submit it
