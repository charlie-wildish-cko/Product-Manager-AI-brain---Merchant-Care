**When to use this article **

Use this article as guidance when scoring quality cases, it will help you understand the scorecard sections, what good and bad looks like and the outcomes/actions.

 SCORING 📋

The quality scorecard is divided into four core sections to ensure a balanced evaluation of every interaction:

- Resolution

- Process adherence and case handling

- Merchant experience

- Compliance

| **Rating** | **Criteria** | **Action** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pass** | Meets all core standards with no errors | N/A |
| **Pass with Feedback** | Meets core standards but has up to 2 minor issues | Feedback provided; coaching if it becomes a repeat issue. |
| **Fail** | Major error with material impact or failing more than 2 minor items | Feedback provided |
| **Non-Compliant** | Any critical regulatory or policy breach | **Instant Fail** and mandatory After Action Review AAR |

RESOLUTION ✅

This section evaluates the accuracy and completeness of the solution provided.

| **Sub-Section** | **What Good Looks Like (Pass)** | **What Bad Looks Like (Fail)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Core Resolution** | The agent provides a timely, comprehensive, and effective solution. No re-working of the case is required. | The agent misunderstood the request or provided an incomplete/ineffective resolution. The merchant had to repeatedly contact us - which was avoidable. |
| **Managing Expectations** | "Next Steps" are clearly explained (who, what and when). Realistic turnaround timeframes are provided. | Next steps are vague or missing. Inaccurate timeframes leave the merchant uncertain about what to expect. |
| **Future Prevention** | The agent proactively provides self-service advice or educates the merchant on how to resolve the issue independently next time. | The agent misses the opportunity to empower the customer with self-service solutions or relevant advice. |

**⚠️ Major Markdown (Fail):**

- **Inaccurate Solution:** Providing the wrong information that leads to a merchant taking the wrong action.

- **Incomplete Resolution:** Failing to address all parts of a merchant’s multi-part query.

- **Poor Expectation Setting:** Providing no timeline or a significantly incorrect timeline for a follow-up.

**💡Minor Markdown (Pass with Feedback):**

- **Missed Empowerment:** Resolving the issue but failing to mention a self-service tool that could prevent the issue next time.

- **Vague Next Steps:** Telling the merchant "we will be in touch" instead of specifically stating "we will email you within 24 hours."

PROCESS ADHERENCE & CASE HANDLING 💫

This section ensures agents follow Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and use internal tools correctly.

| **Sub-Section** | **What Good Looks Like (Pass)** | **What Bad Looks Like (Fail)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Case Handling** | The issue is correctly classified. The correct dependency team is engaged using the appropriate macro. Correct macro used to respond to the merchant. | The issue is misclassified. The agent fails to escalate to the right team or fails to use macros to populate required info. |
| **Documentation & SOPs** | Accurate data entry verified within systems. Comprehensive notes are left to satisfy audit requirements. | Documentation is incomplete or the ticket is misrouted. The agent deviates from process, risking inconsistent handling. |
| **Troubleshooting** | The agent accurately diagnoses the root cause using tools efficiently without "triple checking" or providing excessive evidence. | The agent uses the tools inefficiently, resulting in delays or incorrect handling. |

**⚠️ Major Markdown (Fail):**

- **SOP Deviation:** Skipping a required step in a standard workflow (e.g., failing to update a specific field in the CRM).

- **Tool Misuse:** Using an internal tool in a way that causes a delay or requires a colleague to redo the work.

- **Failing to Escalate: **Not identifying and escalating a Complaint is a major markdown.

- **Failing to Classify: **An IDV 'data privacy' case correctly.

**💡 Minor Markdown (Pass with Feedback):**

- **Incorrect Escalation:** Routing a ticket to the wrong department or failing to use the mandatory escalation macro unless a complaint is missed - this is a Major Markdown.

- **Minor Macro Issue:** Using the correct macro but failing to delete the "placeholder text" within it.

- **Incorrect Case Classification: **Selecting the wrong case classification, unless it's an IDV case that should be 'Data Privacy' - this is a Major Markdown.

MERCHANT EXPERIENCE 🗣️

This section focuses on the "human" element of the interaction, including tone and clarity.

| **Sub-Section** | **What Good Looks Like (Pass)** | **What Bad Looks Like (Fail)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Language** | Tone is professional and friendly. Explanations are easy to understand and free of excessive jargon. | Spelling or grammar errors affect clarity. Language is jargon-heavy, inappropriate, or unprofessional. |
| **Relationship Building** | Includes a polite greeting and uses the customer's name at least once. Demonstrates empathy for the situation. | The greeting is missing. The agent misses opportunities to empathize with or acknowledge the concern. |
| **Interaction Flow** | The agent manages the flow well and ends with a professional closing that offers further assistance. | The agent fails to offer further assistance or uses an unprofessional closing statement. |

**⚠️ Major Markdown (Fail):**

- **Incomprehensible Communication:** Grammar or spelling is so poor that the merchant cannot understand the instructions.

- **Total Lack of Empathy:** Handling a high-stress merchant complaint with a cold, robotic, or dismissive response.

**💡 Minor Markdown (Pass with Feedback):**

- **Etiquette Lapses:** Failing to use the merchant’s name or forgetting a professional closing statement ("Is there anything else I can help you with?").

- **Jargon Usage:** Using internal acronyms that a merchant wouldn't understand, even if the solution is correct.

COMPLIANCE ✅

**Note:** Any failure in this section results in an **immediate Fail rating** for the entire scorecard.

- **Pass:** Agent actions fully comply with all regulations and policies. Identity verification is performed and documented, and required approval verifications are obtained.

- **Critical Fail:** The agent breaches regulation, policy or creates a legal risk. 

**Examples Include**

- **Security Breach:** Failing to perform Identity Verification before disclosing account details.

- **Data Protection (GDPR/Privacy):** Sending one merchant’s data to another merchant.

- **Regulatory Failure:** Misrepresenting terms and conditions or legal rights.
