## Payments Team Support Guide

This guide outlines the types of support the **Payments team** provides, when to escalate issues to them, and what falls outside their scope.

The Payments Team is the primary point of contact for several critical financial and operational queries:

 

### Funds Tracing and Payment Execution

- 
**Settlement Queries:** The team handles requests to trace funds, provide payment confirmations, request a GPI tracker, and recall payments.

- 
**Test Payments:** Any test payment must be approved by a **Payments Manager** before processing (usually followed by a debit adjustment if successful).

 

### Banking and Treasury Operations

- 
**Banking Queries:** All types of queries received from banks, such as JPM, Barclays, and Banking Circle, should be directed to the Payments Team.

 

### Adjustments and Negative Balances

- 
**Adjustment Queries:** The team carries out adjustments related to proof of refund fees, chargebacks, rejected transactions, negative balances, returned settlements, and prefunding. They can also provide details related to adjustments that merchants query.

- 
**NAS Migration (Negative Balance):** Merchants can clear their negative balance on MBC either by sending funds to CKO's account or by requesting the Payments Team to move their balance from MBC to NAS.

- 
**Adjustments (from Billing):** If Merchant Care doesn't have enough information on a billing adjustment, queries may be directed to the Payments Team first.

 

### Reserves and Security Deposits

- 
**Security Deposit / Rolling Reserve (RR):** The Payments Team can set and release **fixed deposits**. For a release of any security deposit or RR on MBC, the Payments Team raises a ticket to **FTS**. Note that **Credit Portfolio Management** holds and releases the RR.

- 
**Refund Settlements:** When a settlement is returned to CKO, the Payments Team adjusts it back to the merchant's balance. **Merchant Care** is then notified after two returns.

 

### Reconciliation and Reporting

- 
**Reconciliation Queries:** These are usually investigated by the Payments Team. If the discrepancy cannot be explained or identified, agents must create a side conversation with the **FTS Support team**.

- 
**Reports:** The Payments Team can provide **settlement reports** and **rolling reserve reports**. For other reports on the SFTP service (FAR, BR, PR) not available on the Dashboard, raise with the **FTS team**.
