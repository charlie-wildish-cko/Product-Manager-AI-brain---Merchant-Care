**When to use this article**

When completing a non-standard, merchant-originated Third-Party Risk Assessment Questionnaire. DESCRIBE THE ISSUE 💬

   
The merchant has initiated a case requiring Checkout.com to complete a Third-Party Risk Assessment Questionnaire. This questionnaire is not a standard template; it has been supplied by the merchant based on the requirements of their 3rd party risk tool provider.   
   
The request often occurs when the merchant is evaluating us against other vendors, particularly in areas like fraud detection tools.

⚠️The merchant's deadline is critically short (less than 48 hours) requiring immediate priority assignment.KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- The Financial and Anti-Bribery sections are pre-completed by SAS Compliance

- The Introduction tab is to be completed by the Corporate Secretary

- The Data Protection section requires specialized input from the Legal Team

- The Customer Impact Assessment section is assigned to the Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Team

PROCESS FOR RESPONDING TO A MERCHANT-PROVIDED RISK ASSESSMENT 🖊️  
   
**Step 1. Triage and Verify Initial Completion**

- 
**Action:** Receive and confirm the questionnaire has been reviewed and partially completed by **SAS Compliance**.

- 
**Guidance:** Ensure the **Financial** and **Anti-Bribery** sections are marked as complete before moving forward. If incomplete, follow up with SAS Compliance.

**Step 2. Assign the Introduction Tab**

- 
**Action:** Forward the **Introduction tab** of the questionnaire to the **Corporate Secretary**.

- 
**Guidance:** This section typically covers administrative details, company registration, and contact information.

**Step 3. Assign the Data Protection Section**

- 
**Action:** Forward the **Data Protection** section to the **Legal Team**.

- 
**Guidance:** The Legal Team must ensure responses comply with relevant data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) and reflect current data processing agreements.

**Step 4. Assign the Customer Impact Assessment Section**

- 
**Action:** Forward the **Customer Impact Assessment** section to the **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)** team.

- 
**Guidance:** GRC is responsible for assessing customer-facing risks such as Conduct Risk and Complaints Management.

**Step 5. Collate and Final Review**

- 
**Action:** Once all teams confirm completion, collate all sections into the final document and perform a high-level review for consistency and completeness before returning to the merchant.
RESOLUTION ⚒️

   
The expected result is a **fully completed, accurate, and compliant Third-Party Risk Assessment Questionnaire**, which can be returned to the merchant to satisfy their risk assessment requirements.

Verify that all five major sections (**Financial**, **Anti-Bribery**, **Introduction**, **Data Protection**, and **Customer Impact Assessment**) are filled out and contain specific content, sign-offs, or answers.ESCALATION ⏫
  
**Examples of situations requiring escalation**

- Failure of an assigned team (Legal, GRC, Corporate Secretary) to complete their section by the internal SLA or the merchant's deadline

- Case Number and Merchant Name

- The specific section/tab pending (e.g., Data Protection)

- Name of the responsible team that is non-responsive

**Instructions for the agent working the case**

- 
**Stay on the case** and monitor communication between teams

- Notify the internal stakeholder who initiated the request about the delay

- Escalate immediately to the manager of the non-responsive team

**How to follow up and when to chase**

- Chase the designated team daily for progress updates until the section is complete
WHAT TO TELL THE MERCHANT 🗣️

   
**Upon initial receipt:**

"Thank you for sending your Third-Party Risk Assessment Questionnaire. We have initiated the process to complete it. 

As this is a comprehensive document that requires input from multiple specialized teams (like our Legal and Compliance departments), we will ensure all sections are completed accurately and compliantly. We anticipate returning the fully completed document to you by **[Date/SLA]**."

**If asked about the completion process:**

"We follow a strict internal process. For example, our Legal Team handles the **Data Protection** section to ensure full compliance with regulatory requirements, and our **Governance, Risk & Compliance** team assesses the potential **Customer Impact**."FAQs ❓

**Who is responsible for the Data Protection section?**

The Legal Team is responsible for completing the Data Protection section, ensuring all responses align with regulatory and contractual requirements.  
 

**Which internal team completes the Customer Impact Assessment?**

The Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) team is responsible for assessing and completing the Customer Impact Assessment section.  
 

**Why do we have to fill out a non-standard questionnaire?**

We complete these questionnaires to meet the merchant's compliance requirements for their choice of 3rd party risk tool, often for services like fraud detection.
