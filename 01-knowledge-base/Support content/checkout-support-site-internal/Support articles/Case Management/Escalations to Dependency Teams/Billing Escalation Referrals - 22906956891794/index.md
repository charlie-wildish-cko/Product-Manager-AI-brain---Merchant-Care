## Billing Team Support Guide

This guide outlines the types of support the Billing team provides, when to escalate issues to them and what falls outside their scope. Type of query:

- Tiered pricing

- Adjustments (NAS & MBC)

- Minimum billing 

- Balance confirmation

- Invoices 

- Network token fees

- Process out

- Bespoke reports for specific merchants

- Reporting (Klarna Inc & Newday Inc)

- GST fees & invoice

- Revenue share

- CCP (Chanel Channel Partner)
