Use this article to see how to escalate tickets for immediate action**Need to Prioritize a Ticket? Use the Ask Merchant Care Channel ⏫**

Got a super urgent or critical case that needs immediate attention? You've got this! The `ask_merchant_care` Slack channel is your go-to spot to get those tickets prioritized and ensure speedy resolutions. It helps us all collaborate better and reduces response times.

 

## How to Escalate a Ticket to the Channel 💥

No need to send a direct message to one person—post it in the channel for the whole team to see and act on!

1. 
**Find the Channel**: Head over to Slack and look for `#ask_merchant_care`.

2. **Start a Prioritization Request**: Once you're in the channel, click on the "Merchant Care Ticket Prioritisation Request" button. 

3.** Fill in the Form**: A pop-up form will appear. Make sure to fill in all the important details: 

- 
**Ticket Number**: Pop in the Zendesk case number. 

- 
**Merchant Name**: Who's the client in question? 

- 
**Merchant Tier**: Let us know their tier (Tier 1, 2, 3, 4, N/A) as seen in Zendesk/Salesforce. 

- 
**Reason for Escalation**: Choose from the dropdown why this needs immediate attention. Common reasons include "High Business Impact," "Prolonged Resolution Time," "Reputation or Compliance Risk," or "Lack of Communication."

- 
**More Context (Optional)**: Add any extra deets here, like if an SLA was breached or if it's a critical issue like downtime.

4.** Hit Submit!**

 

## What Happens Next? ⏭️

Once you've submitted your request, our awesome shift leads and care agents aim to respond within 15-30 minutes! 

They'll assess how critical the incident is and determine if immediate action is needed. Critical cases, like high declines, get top priority!

## Responding to Escalations ⏫

Every single post in this channel needs a response—no exceptions!

1. 
**Acknowledge**: Click "Reply in thread" and let the requester know you're on it and looking into their request. This builds trust and keeps everyone in the loop.

2. 
**Provide Updates**: Once you've investigated or moved the case to another team (like L2), provide a final update in the thread. This confirms the issue is being handled!
