## Introduction to Complaints Handling 💬

This article outlines the standardised procedure for managing merchant complaints. It is designed for complaints specialists to ensure a consistent, thorough and compliant approach to resolving merchant dissatisfaction.

At Checkout.com, Merchant Care is responsible for addressing and resolving complaints from our merchants. This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes a clear process for investigating complaints to provide effective resolutions.

A complaint, for the purpose of this SOP and the Complaints Policy, is defined as an expression of dissatisfaction from a Checkout customer—a business with a valid Merchant Services Agreement.

 

## Complaints Handling Process 🖊️

### Step 1. Complaint Initiation

**Acknowledge & Assign**: Send the acknowledgment macro: Timeline for Complaints to the complainant, including the Zendesk ticket number for reference and any next steps.

**Initial Review**: Thoroughly review the complaint, any supporting documents and internal notes. Understand the allegations, the impact (financial loss, distress, inconvenience) and the desired outcome.

**Identify Merchant Region**: Note the region of the merchant, as this may affect the timeframes, outcome and reporting requirements.

**Complete Zendesk Fields**: Complete the asterisked fields in Zendesk. 

💡 Note: Field requirements will vary depending on region.

**Identify Stakeholders**: Note all internal and external parties who may have relevant information (e.g., Account Managers, Underwriting, Commercial, Legal). 

### Step 2. Evidence Gathering

**Review Internal Records**: Access and review relevant internal systems and records (e.g., Zendesk, Salesforce, transaction logs, pricing policies, internal policies).

**Engage with AM/TAM**: Contact the Account Manager (if managed) to report the complaint and gather relevant information or details about prior discussions with the merchant.

**Obtain External Information**: If necessary, request specific supporting documents from the complainant (e.g., bank statements, proof of loss).

**Document Evidence**: Maintain a detailed log of all evidence in the Zendesk ticket, including the source, date obtained, and a description. This can be documented within the Zendesk ticket as an internal note, using the macro: Plan of Action.Step 3. Analysis & Findings

**Identify Root Causes**: Determine the underlying reason(s) for the complaint (e.g., system error, human error, policy gap, misunderstanding).

**Engage stakeholders - **please see the escalation matrix below.

**Evaluate Impact**: Quantify any financial loss and assess material distress or inconvenience experienced by the complainant.

**Formulate Preliminary Findings**: Conclude whether the complaint is likely to be upheld, partially upheld, or not upheld.Step 4. Resolution & Response Formulation

**Determine Outcome**: Formally decide if the complaint is upheld, partially upheld, or not upheld.

**Propose Redress (if applicable)**: Offer fair and proportionate redress, such as an apology, financial compensation (see waiver and write-offs below), corrective action, or an explanation of prevention steps.

**Draft Clear Response**: Provide a clear, empathetic, and fact-based final response. State the decision, any redress offered, and the complainant's right to escalate to their regional regulator. Use the macro: Resolution - Final Response and remove any regional-specific outcomes as needed.

**Ensure Compliance**: Ensure the proposed resolution and communication meet all regulatory requirements and deadlines.Step 5. Communicate Outcome

**Deliver Response**: Send the final response within the respective regional SLA 

**Explain Findings**: Be prepared to clarify points if the complainant has further questions.Step 6. Implement & Follow-up

**Action Redress**: Quickly carry out agreed redress (e.g., refunds, payments) and confirm when done.

**Implement Corrective Actions**: Recommend and implement systemic improvements or training based on root causes to prevent recurrence.Step 7. Documentation & Closure

**Record All Details**: Document all investigation steps, evidence, findings, decisions and communications in the complaint management system. Attach the final response and supporting documents, for all internal notes on documenting the actions taken, use the macro: Complaints Closure.

**Update & Close**: Update the complaint status to "Solved" in the system, noting the outcome and date.

**Final Administrative Tasks**: Complete any remaining administrative actions.

## Escalations ⏫

**Complaints Escalation and Referral Matrix** 

| **Complaint Scenario** | **Relevant Internal Team(s)** | **Rationale for Escalation** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contractual or Regulatory Disputes** Merchant raises concerns about a specific clause in their contract, a breach of a commercial agreement, or an issue related to regulatory requirements (e.g., PSD2, AML). | **Legal - Commercial or Regulatory** | This team handles all legal issues concerning commercial agreements and regulatory matters, providing expertise on contract interpretation and legal compliance. |
| **Litigation Wording** Regulatory or legal triggers:       Mentions of "regulator" or "ombudsman" or "legal action" or "breach of contract" or "legal action" or "lawsuit" or "litigation" or "summons" or "complaint filed" or "court" or "arbitration" and "proceedings" or "legal counsel" or "attorney" or "breach of contract" or "violation of terms" or "statutory rights" or "non-compliance" or "Letter Before Action (LBA)" or "formal demand" or "notice of dispute" or "cease and desist" or "reserv* of rights" or "damages" or "material impact" or "financial loss" or "negligence" and "reputational damage" or “Letters before Claim,” or "Pre-Action Conduct” or "Civil Procedure Rules" | **Law Enforcement** | Law enforcement, part of our legal team, handle all cases of litigation. These cases should be forwarded to [lawenforcement@checkout.com](mailto:lawenforcement@checkout.com) |
| **Transaction Declines & Chargebacks**  Merchant is complaining about a high rate of transaction declines, incorrect fraud flagging, or a large number of chargebacks, which they believe is impacting their business. | **Fraud Detection** | This team specializes in transaction decline reasons, rule customization, and performance analytics.  They are best equipped to investigate and modify rules to improve acceptance rates and manage risk. |
| **Underwriting Decisions**  Merchant is questioning the reason for their account's initial approval, a change in their risk profile, or has a complaint directly related to the underwriting process. | **Underwriting** **Platform Underwriting** (if platform-related) | The Underwriting team is solely responsible for credit risk analysis, compliance reviews, and underwriting decisions.  Merchant Care does not handle these decisions, so escalation is required for any related issues. |
| **Billing Discrepancies & Fee Waivers**  Merchant disputes an invoice, claims a negative balance is incorrect, or requests a fee waiver or write-off due to a system issue or goodwill. | **Billing Team** **Strategic Finance & Planning (SFP)** **Accounting** | The Billing team addresses queries related to invoices, minimum billing, and balance confirmations.  Fee waiver/write-off requests are handled by a senior team member who will route the request to the relevant finance stakeholders as per the Waiver & Write-off SOP. |
| **Card Scheme Rules & Compliance**  Merchant believes their account is being held to an incorrect standard or is complaining about a penalty imposed by a card scheme (e.g., Visa, Mastercard) that they feel is unjustified. | **Scheme Compliance** | This team is responsible for ensuring adherence to card scheme rules and minimizing organizational risk.  They can provide guidance and investigation into scheme-related rule enforcement. |
| **Internal & External Security Concerns**  Merchant reports a security vulnerability or has concerns about how their data is being monitored or accessed. | **Risk Monitoring** | The Risk Monitoring team handles continuous risk assessment, vulnerability management, and regular monitoring of access to network resources and cardholder data. |
| **Disputes Process**  Merchant is complaining about the dispute process itself, such as a dispute not being handled in the correct timeframe or a dispute outcome they do not agree with. | **Disputes** | This team is the primary owner of the dispute process.  Escalating to them ensures the team with specific expertise on the process can investigate and provide a resolution. |
