**When to use this article**

Use this document when a merchant, partner, or individual contacts Merchant Care to request a copy of their personal data or to exercise their data privacy rights. This guide ensures you route these requests to the correct internal team for processing.

### Introduction

A Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) is a formal request made by an individual to see the personal data an organisation holds about them. At Checkout.com, these requests are managed by our Data Protection Office team (DPO) team to ensure compliance with global data protection regulations.

### General Questions

- **What is a DSAR?** A DSAR is a request from an individual (the "data subject") to access the personal information a company processes about them.

- **Who handles these requests at Checkout.com?** All data privacy enquiries and DSARs are handled exclusively by our DPO team.

- **What is the role of Merchant Care in this process?** The role of the Merchant Care team is strictly to identify the request and refer it to the DPO team immediately.

### Specific Questions

- **How do I handle a DSAR?** You must forward the request to **dpo@checkout.com** directly via side conversation. Our Data Privacy team will then compile the necessary information and respond to the requester.

- **Is there any further action required after referral?** No. Once the request has been referred to the DPO, there is no further involvement required from the Merchant Care team. We can inform the requester that their DSAR has been referred to our specialist team for review.

- **What if the requester asks for an update on their DSAR?** You should direct them to follow up with the DPO team, as they manage the end-to-end communication for these requests.
