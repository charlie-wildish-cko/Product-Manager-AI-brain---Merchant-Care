**When to use this article**

This article is intended to act as a supplementary guide to our complaints handling procedure. This article is to be used where there are significant variances to the main complaints handling process with regional specificity to consider. 

This article outlines the nuances involved in the complaints process for the following regions:

**- **Canada

**- **USA

**- **Japan

 
  
**Canada **

## **Code of Conduct Complaints**

A code of conduct complaint in Canada relates to an alleged breach of specific, published industry standards or commitments designed to protect financial consumers and merchants.

If the merchant’s complaint relates to one of the specific Code of Conduct complaint types, please ensure this is logged within the Zendesk fields as per the image below.

 

### **Code of Conduct Complaint Types:**

- Transparency and Disclosure by Acquirers and Downstream Participants

- Card Processing Applications

- Merchant Agreements

- Merchant Statements

- Ability to Cancel Agreements without Penalty

- Notice to Merchants of Fee Changes

- Negative Option Acceptance Not Allowed

- Notice to Acquirers of Payment Card Network Operator (PCNO) Core Fee Changes

- 
Renewal of Merchant Agreements and Related Service Agreements
 

**Expand to see more information on each of the Code of Conduct types****1. Transparency and Disclosure by Acquirers and Downstream Participants**

**Code Requirement (CoC 1):** Merchant agreements and statements must include a sufficient level of detail, be readily available, and be easy to understand.

| **Case Example** | **Complaint Type / Category** |
| --- | --- |
| **"My statement shows a 'Network Fee' but doesn't explain what it is or how it's calculated."** | **1c Merchant Statements:** Lack of sufficient detail/breakdown of fees. |
| **"When I signed up, the quote only gave me a blended rate, not the individual interchange or network fees."** | **1a Card Processing Applications:** Missing required cost per transaction or fee disclosure on application/quote. |
| **"I can't find a summary of the contract's key terms anywhere in my agreement."** | **1b Merchant Agreements:** Merchant Agreement is missing the required Summary of Key Elements cover page. |

 
**3. Ability to Cancel Agreements without Penalty**

**Code Requirement (CoC 3):** Merchants have a cancellation right, without penalty, within 70 days of the effective date of any new or increased fees.

| **Case Example** | **Complaint Type / Category** |
| --- | --- |
| **"You increased my fee last month, and when I tried to cancel my contract this week (45 days later), I was told I'd have to pay a termination fee."** | **3 Ability to Cancel:** Merchant was denied the right to cancel without penalty within the 70-day window following a fee increase. |

 
**4. Notice to Merchants of Fee Changes**

**Code Requirement (CoC 4):** Notice must be provided between 30 and 60 days prior to the effective date of any new or increased fees.

| **Case Example** | **Complaint Type / Category** |
| --- | --- |
| **"I just saw a new monthly account fee on my statement, and I didn't receive any notification about it beforehand."** | **4 Notice to Merchants:** Failure to provide notice for a new fee (or fee increase) within the required 30 to 60-day window. |
| **"I received a notice about a fee increase, but the increase was effective only 15 days after the notice date."** | **4 Notice to Merchants:** Notice was provided, but the timing (less than 30 days) was non-compliant. |

 
**6. Negative Option Acceptance Not Allowed**

**Code Requirement (CoC 6):** Merchants must provide express consent to accept any new payments product or service.

| **Case Example** | **Complaint Type / Category** |
| --- | --- |
| **"I was suddenly charged for a new 'Fraud Monitoring Service' this month. I never agreed to add this service to my account."** | **6 Negative Option Acceptance:** A new product/service was activated and billed without the merchant's clear, documented, express consent. |

 
**7. Renewal of Merchant Agreements and Related Service Agreements**

**Code Requirement (CoC 7):** Fixed-term agreements cannot automatically renew for the full initial term, but may convert to extensions of six months or less. Merchants may provide notice of non-renewal at any point up to 45 days before agreement renewal.

| **Case Example** | **Complaint Type / Category** |
| --- | --- |
| **"My one-year contract expired last month, and without warning, I've been auto-renewed for another full year."** | **7 Renewal of Merchant Agreements:** Automatic renewal for a term longer than six months (for fixed-term agreements). |

 
**5, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 (Checkout Not Applicable)**

Elements 5, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, and 13 are generally **not applicable for Checkout's standard acquiring services** (e.g., relating to limited acceptance, discounting, co-badged cards, card issuance, or wallets).

- 
**Action for Agent:** If a complaint clearly relates to one of these elements, confirm the merchant's business model (e.g., if they are also an issuer or device provider). If they are not, advise the merchant that the matter relates to a PCNO, issuer, or a different participant, and is outside of Checkout's direct CoC obligations.

 

### **Complaints Process (CoC 14)**

**Code Requirement:** Merchants must have access to a clear, simple, and transparent complaints handling process.

| **Case Example** | **Complaint Type / Category** |
| --- | --- |
| **"I submitted a formal complaint 8 business days ago and haven't heard anything back yet."** | **Complaints Process - Acknowledgment:** Failure to acknowledge the complaint within 5 BDs. |
| **"It's been 25 business days since I filed my complaint, and I still haven't received a final decision or an update on why it's delayed."** | **Complaints Process - Resolution/Timeline:** Failure to resolve the complaint within 20 BDs or inform the merchant of the delay and new expected response time. |
| **"I got a final decision email, but it only said 'Complaint closed as unsubstantiated' without any detailed explanation."** | **Complaints Process - Final Decision:** Failure to provide a final decision that includes an explanation and escalation path. |

 

### **Language Requirements:**

- 
Quebec has specific language requirements, these are:

  - If a merchant submits a complaint in French, CKO’s response must also be in French

  - A merchant can request a French translation of a response that has previously been provided to them in English.

- 
To comply with these requirements, the below configuration has been done in Zendesk:

  - All agents that are comfortable working in French have been given the French Skill in Zendesk. This means that agents comfortable working in French will have French cases routed to them through OCR.

  - If you receive a phone call in French and you are not comfortable working in French, please pass the call on to a colleague who is comfortable working in French.

  - The automated first response email sent to merchants after submitting a complaint is available in French and English - this will be sent in French or English, depending on whether the case is in French or English

  - Most complaint macros are available in French and English - when selecting a macro, the French or English will be provided depending on whether the case is in French or English

  - Merchant chasers when CKO is waiting for further information from the merchant are available in French and English - these will be sent in French or English, depending on whether the case is in French or English

  - Triggers for spam and end-customer emails sent to the complaints queue are available in French and English - these will be sent in French or English, depending on whether the case is in French or English

**Japan**

  
In Japan, the Japan Consumer Credit Association (JCA), an approved association under the Instalment Sales Act to which Checkout Kabushiki Kaisha (Checkout KK) is a licensed acquirer, mandates that acquirers properly address consumer complaints concerning malicious merchants and their activities that violate user protection.

Japanese acquirers are required to record these complaints within the JDM system, an adverse media screening and reporting tool managed by the JCA.

Complaints originate from cardholders who have made purchases at merchants acquired by Checkout KK. These complaints will be routed to Checkout KK from cardholder issuers, the JCA, or JCB, and must be reviewed per JCA regulations.

### 1. Complaint Intake

Consumer complaints directed at Checkout KK (Kabushiki Kaisha) will originate from the following sources:

- Issuers of the cardholders (complainants)

- The Japan Consumer Credit Association (JCA)

- JCB

Upon receipt, all complaints must be immediately **forwarded to the Merchant Care team via ******[complaints@checkout.com](mailto:complaints@checkout.com)

### 2. Merchant Care Information Gathering

Once the complaint is received by Merchant Care, the assigned agent is responsible for collaborating with Account Managers (AMs), Technical Account Managers (TAMs), Sales, or other relevant internal teams to collect comprehensive information about the merchant and the specific complaint. This information includes:

- 
**Business Relationship**: Details of the merchant's current or past relationship with Checkout KK.

- 
**Merchant Status**: Whether the merchant is currently "live" or has been "terminated."

- 
**Financial Status**: Any known bankruptcy or insolvency status.

- 
**Disputed Transaction**: Specifics of the transaction(s) in question.

- 
**Contract Structure**: Whether the merchant is a direct entity or a sub-entity.

- 
**Complaint Receipt Date**: The exact date Checkout KK received the complaint.

- 
**Contract Date**: The date Checkout KK formally entered into a contract with the merchant.

- 
**Sales Method/Channel**: How the product/service was sold to the consumer.

- 
**Product/Service**: Description of the item or service purchased.

- 
**Nature of Complaint**: Detailed description of the consumer's grievance.

- 
**Merchant Conduct**: Specific information regarding the merchant's actions that led to the complaint.

- 
**Actions Taken**: Any past or present actions taken against the merchant (e.g., termination, warnings).

- 
**Guidance Measures Implementation Date**: Date any corrective or guidance measures were implemented for the merchant.

### 3. Compliance Referral

Once all necessary information has been gathered, the Merchant Care team must **email the Japan Compliance team** to request JDM registration, this can be done by creating a side conversation within the Complaint ticket and emailing [Compliance Japan](mailto:compliance.japan@checkout.com)

- 
**Exception**: If the original complaint requester is JCB, the Japan Compliance team is not required to register the complaint in JDM but will still conduct the complaints investigation and outcome

No further action is required from Merchant Care until the response is received from Compliance Japan. 

### 4. Result Communication & Case Resolution

After completing the JDM registration steps (or determining no registration is needed), the Japan Compliance team will **email the result of the complaints investigation back to the Merchant Care team**.

Finally, the Merchant Care team will then **respond to the original requester** (cardholder issuer, JCA, or JCB) with the outcome and **resolve the case**. Utilise the Final Response macro to help structure the response.
