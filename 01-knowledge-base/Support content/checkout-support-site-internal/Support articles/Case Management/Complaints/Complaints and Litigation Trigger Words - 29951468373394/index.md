**Use this page when:**

You're checking if a customer's language indicates they're making a complaint, this page shows specific trigger words or phrases that show a strong probability of a compalint.

| Strong Complaint Indicators## |
| --- |
| Words/phrases that indicate the customer is complaining:       - “Complaint”  - “Unacceptable”  - “Disgusting”  - “Worst”  - “Terrible”  - “Poor service”  - “Bad experience”  - “Rude”  - “Scam” / “Fraud”  - “Shocking”  - “Disgraceful”  - “Unprofessional”  - “Negligent”  - “Incompetent”  - “Appalling”  - "Angry"  - "Outraged"  - "Frustrated"  - "Annoyed"  - "Irritated"  - "Distressed"  - "Ignored"  - "Unimportant"  - "Neglected"  - "Abandoned"  - “Unreliable”  - “Rip-off”  - “Dishonest”  - “Corrupt”  - “Abusive”  - “Insensitive”  - “Mistreated” / “Mistreatment”  - “Humiliated”  - “Ignored”  - “Disrespected”  - “I want to speak to a manager”  - “Escalate”  - “Compensation”  - “Fix this immediately”  - “Sort this out now”  - “Ombudsman”  - “Legal action”  - “Breach”  - “Violation”  - “Regulation”  - “Contractual” |

| **Litigation Indicators **## |
| --- |
| Regulatory or legal triggers: Mentions of "regulator" or "ombudsman" or "legal action" or "breach of contract" or "legal action" or "lawsuit" or "litigation" or "summons" or "complaint filed" or "court" or "arbitration" and "proceedings" or "legal counsel" or "attorney" or "breach of contract" or "violation of terms" or "statutory rights" or "non-compliance" or "Letter Before Action (LBA)" or "formal demand" or "notice of dispute" or "cease and desist" or "reserv* of rights" or "damages" or "material impact" or "financial loss" or "negligence" and "reputational damage" or “Letters before Claim,” or "Pre-Action Conduct” or "Civil Procedure Rules" |
