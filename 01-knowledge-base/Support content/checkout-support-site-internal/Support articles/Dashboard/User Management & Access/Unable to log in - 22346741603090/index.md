## Process Steps

**Note:** It is always worth checking the #ask-iam slack channel to see if the case is a recurring issue. 

### **Hub platform:**

1. 
**Case Verification:**

  1. Confirm with the merchant that they are attempting to access the old platform (Hub).

  2. Gather details about the specific information or action they wanted to take on the old platform.

2. 
**Internal Collaboration:**

  1. Resetting the merchant's password will not resolve the issue as the platform is decommissioned. Due to the platform's decommissioned status, standard support options are unavailable.

  2. As such, agents must route the case and collaborate with the internal team best suited to assist the merchant. This might involve retrieving data from the old platform.

3. 
**Merchant Communication:**

  1. Inform the merchant that the old platform is no longer accessible.

  2. Explain the steps being taken to address their request through internal collaboration.

  3. Set clear expectations regarding the timeframe for obtaining the requested information or alternative solutions.

**Additional Resources:**

- If the merchant is looking for reports or invoices, you may find useful articles within the [Settlements, Invoices & Fees section](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/categories/21991158830610-Settlement-Invoices-Fees) of the Internal Support Center.   
 

### **NAS Platform:**

Issues on the NAS platform with specific errors can occur - We can generally categorize them into two scenarios:

1. 
**Pre-Login Issues: **If the user is facing difficulties before logging in, resetting their password is usually the solution.

2. 
**Post-Login Issues: **When the user is having trouble navigating the dashboard, agents need to delve deeper into the specific steps outlined in our troubleshooting guide.

  1. In these cases, resetting the password won't resolve the issue. 

  2. Before proceeding with the common resolution step of resetting the user password on the dashboard, it can also be helpful for the merchant to proceed with troubleshooting.
