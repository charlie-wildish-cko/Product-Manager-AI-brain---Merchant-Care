Introduction

As part of our efforts to improve the merchant experience in Dashboard and drive self-service, merchants will be able to see and review details of business owners, signatories and other stakeholders in Dashboard. Initially, this feature will mainly be available for Admin/Owner roles that are signatories.

- This display feature is live as of May 27, 2025

- Editing capabilities will not arrive until Q3 2025 (approx)

Process Steps

If a merchant reaches out about their key business stakeholders on Dashboard, they can find them as follows:-

1. Log in to [Dashboard](https://dashboard.checkout.com)

2. Click on Settings (gear icon on the top right)

3. Click on "Key business stakeholders"

  1. 
**Please note**: This feature will only be visible on the Settings page to users with the 'View business stakeholders' permission

4. Under "Select entity", use the dropdown box or "search by name" field to see stakeholder details for specific entities:- 

5. Click on a stakeholder to see their specific details, including stakeholder role, personal details, and ownership percentage
