**When to use this article:**

 To guide a merchant to self-serve and manage their settlements on the Dashboard

## 

INTRODUCTION TO DASHBOARD SETTLEMENTS & FINANCIAL REPORTS 💬 

The Dashboard is a web app that helps merchants self-serve, manage daily tasks, reconcile and access data and insights.

The merchant needs to have their own Dashboard account access to view and manage their settlement reports and they'll need their username and password to log in.MERCHANT PROCESS STEPS 🖊️

 

## Step 1: Guiding the Merchant to Find a Settlement

Talk the merchant through these steps to locate their settlement reports:

1. 
**Log in** to the Dashboard account

2. From the left navigation panel they should select **Business account**

3. In the dropdown menu they should select **Settlements**

Explain that on the next page, they'll find all of their generated settlement reports. They can filter these reports by:

- 
**Settlement ID** directly in the search bar

- 
**Entity **if they have multiple entities

💡 The settlements page lists all of their settlements from most recent to oldest

## Step 2: Explaining Settlement Details

When a merchant asks about the information available explain that for each settlement, they'll see the following details:

 

- 
**Amount**: The total amount of the settlement

- 
**Status**: The current status, which can be **REJECTED**, **RETURNED**, **PENDING**, or **COMPLETED**

- 
**Payout ID**: A unique ID for the specific payout

- 
**Settlement date**: The date the payment was settled or the expected settlement date

To view more details for a specific settlement, the merchant should simply select it from the main list. The

**Settlement details** page provides a more granular view, including the settlement amount paid, status, timezone, and creation date.

💡 The merchant can download a transaction breakdown in CSV or PDF format and view a history of the settlement’s progression

## Step 3: Explaining Financial Actions Reports

If the merchant is having trouble reconciling their payments and fees, you can introduce them to Financial Actions Reports.

Explain that these reports provide a clear overview of all actions that impact their currency account's available balance throughout a payment's lifecycle. When used with payout reports, they can reconcile payments and associated fees with the payouts they receive.

There are two types of financial actions reports to be aware of:

- 
**Financial Actions by Date Range**: This report contains all financial actions that occurred within a specified period

- 
**Financial Actions by Payout ID**: This report contains all financial actions related to a specific payout ID

💡Both reports list records chronologically and feature a unique financial action ID in every row - this ID can be used to link a specific action to a payment or a settled payoutESCALATIONS ⬆️

**Missing Reports:** If a merchant is unable to find a settlement report they are expecting, especially after following the steps outlined above

**Discrepancies:** If a merchant reports a significant discrepancy in the amounts or status listed in their settlement reports

When escalating, ensure you have the following information to include:

- The merchant’s name and account ID

- The specific **Settlement ID** or **Payout ID** they are concerned about

- The date range they're looking for

- A clear description of the issue or discrepancy

FAQs 🤔

 How do I view specific settlement details?

To view a specific settlement's details, select it from the main list. The **Settlement details** page provides a more granular view of the following:

- Settlement amount paid

- Settlement status

- Settlement timezone

- Settlement creation date

- A link to download the transaction breakdown in CSV or PDF format

- A history of the stages the settlement has progressed through

 

 What are Financial Actions Reports?

Financial actions reports provide a clear overview of all actions that impact your currency account's available balance throughout a payment's lifecycle. When used alongside payout reports, these reports allow you to reconcile payments and any associated fees with the payouts you receive.

There are two types of financial actions reports:

- 
**Financial Actions by Date Range:** This report contains all financial actions that occurred within a specified period.

- 
**Financial Actions by Payout ID:** This report contains all financial actions related to a specific payout ID.

Both reports list records chronologically and feature a unique financial action ID in every row. This ID can be used to link a specific action to a payment (using the payment ID or adjustment ID) or to a settled payout (using the payout ID).
