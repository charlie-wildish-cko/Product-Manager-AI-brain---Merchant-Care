Introduction

As part of our efforts to improve the merchant experience in Dashboard and drive self-service, merchants will be able to:-

- 
**View** their business details on Dashboard, such as their registered business name, trading name and other pertinent details

**Important Live Dates:-**

- The **view** business details feature is live as of April 7, 2025, for Tiers 1-4
Process Steps

If a merchant reaches out about their business details on Dashboard, they can find them as follows:-

1. Log in to [Dashboard](https://dashboard.checkout.com/)

2. Click on Settings (gear icon on the top right)

3. Click on "Business details" to review the business details and ensure they are correct:-

4. 
Please note that the business registration number field may have a different name depending on the region. See examples below:- 

5. If any details are incorrect, the merchant cant contact their Account Manager or click on the "request support" option under **Business details**

6. If the merchant is having trouble with business details being displayed, please reach out to [#ask-merchant-change-requests-product](https://checkout.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C06AXSFML79) on Slack with the merchant details for support
