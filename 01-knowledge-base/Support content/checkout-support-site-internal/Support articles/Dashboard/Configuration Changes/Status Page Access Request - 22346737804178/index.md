**Use this article when**

You need to grant a merchant access to the Checkout.com Status Page

## INTRODUCTION TO THE STATUS PAGE 💬

The Status Page is a dedicated portal where merchants can find real-time information about platform outages, scheduled maintenance and other service disruptions that may affect their operations.

## PROCESS STEPS 🖊️

Here are the steps to grant a merchant access to the Status Page:

- 
**Request Initiation:** Any internal Checkout.com employee can initiate a request to grant a merchant access to the Status Page.

- 
**Submit the Request:** Use [Jira](https://checkout.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/111/create/582) to submit the access request on behalf of the merchant.

- 
**Provide Merchant Information:** In the request form, enter the merchant's email address. The system will automatically grant them immediate access upon submission.
✅ Always verify the merchant's email address before submitting a request 

- 
**Notify the Merchant:** After submitting the request, a member of the Merchant Care team should inform the merchant that their access has been granted.

- Use the "Status page - access granted" macro, which is located in the "Access & Permissions" macro folder to send this notification.

## ESCALATION ⬆️

If you run into any problems during this process, or if the merchant isn't able to access their account after following the steps, please reach out to the Senior Agents in the Merchant Care team. 

Make sure to share the merchant's email address and any important details regarding the issue.
