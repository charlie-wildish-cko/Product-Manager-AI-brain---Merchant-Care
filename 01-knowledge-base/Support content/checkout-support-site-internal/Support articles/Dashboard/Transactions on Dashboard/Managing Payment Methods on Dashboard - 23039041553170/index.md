**When to use this article**

To help merchants who want to understand how to manage their payment methods on the dashboard. It highlights how to view, and request new payment methods.
DASHBOARD 💬

Merchants can view their currently enabled payment methods and request new ones directly from their dashboard.

- Merchants can view both enabled and available-to-request payment methods in the dashboard

- The "Request available payment method" feature is available for Tiers 1, 2, and 3. Tier 4 availability is yet to be determined.

- When requesting a new payment method, merchants will be asked to provide their expected annual processing volume and average transaction value

- After a request is submitted, an Upsell opportunity is created in Salesforce for the Account Manager - this takes around 3-5 business days to complete

- A payment method listed as "Partially Enabled" indicates that not all processing channels for that method are configured and ready for use

PROCESS FOR MANAGING PAYMENT METHODS 🖊️

Agents can use these steps to guide merchants through the dashboard interface.

- Log in to [Dashboard](https://dashboard.checkout.com/)

- Click on Settings (gear icon on the top right)

- Under Product Settings

- Click on "Payment methods" to see the list of payment methods configured

- When in Client View, use the "Select entity" dropdown box to see payment methods for specific entities (this feature will be hidden if you are already in Entity View)

- Any payment methods currently enabled will be visible under "Enabled"

- When you click on an enabled payment method, you can see the processing channels configured for it

💡 Where it says "Partially Enabled" it means not all processing channels listed are enabled

- If the merchant would like to see what new payment methods are available to them these will be visible under "Available to request" on the "Payment methods" tab

- If the merchant chooses to request a new payment method, e.g., Diners Club, the following pop-up screen will appear to complete and submit

- 
After submitting the request, an Upsell opportunity is created in Salesforce for the Account Manager. Completion takes about 3-5 working days. Once done, the new payment method will appear in the Enabled section, e.g., Diners Club.

ESCALATION** ⏫**

- 
**Situations requiring escalation:**

  - The merchant reports that payment methods are not being displayed correctly

  - The merchant is unable to request available payment methods

  - The request for a new payment method has taken longer than 5 working days to be completed

- 
**Required information to include:**

  - Merchant's details (e.g., merchant ID, entity name).

  - A description of the specific issue (e.g., "payment method not visible," "request button is not working")

- 
**Instructions for the agent working the case:**

  - Reach out to the [#ask-merchant-change-requests-product](https://checkout.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C06AXSFML79) Slack channel for support

  - Include all required information and the merchant’s details in the request

  - Stay on the case and monitor the Slack thread for updates

  - Notify the merchant of the escalation and provide updates as they become available
