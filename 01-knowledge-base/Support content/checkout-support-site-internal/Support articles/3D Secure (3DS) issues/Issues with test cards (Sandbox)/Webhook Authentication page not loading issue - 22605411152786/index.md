## Introduction

The below article covers the issue of a 3DS page not loading due to a webhook issue. 

## Process Steps

1. Check that the merchant has been configured successfully within the Sandbox account

2. Navigate to the Relevant merchant

3. Go to the "Developer" tab and click on the "Webhooks" tab, to ensure the Webhooks have been enabled

4. Check the notification permissions to see if they are configured correctly

5. If notification permissions are configured correctly, inform the merchant to clear the browser cache

**Resolution**Below are resolution steps you can share with the merchant to resolve the issue:-_To eliminate any potential browser-related issues, we would greatly appreciate it if you could follow the below preliminary troubleshooting steps and attempt to access the page once more:_

1. _Clear your cache and cookies: This action will remove any stored data that may be causing conflicts, allowing the page to load fresh content. To do this, navigate to your browser settings, find the privacy or history section, and select the option to clear cache and cookies._

2. _If the issue persists, kindly open the page in an incognito window: This mode disables extensions and uses a clean slate for loading pages, which can help bypass any lingering issues._
