## Introduction

Issue: Commercial cards are used to process transactions in sandbox.

## Process Steps

Unfortunately, our Checkout sandbox environment doesn't support UK-based 3DS commercial cards due to limitations in simulating specific 3DS scenarios and edge cases. These scenarios are best validated in a production environment.

We are continuously working to enhance the capabilities of our sandbox environment, but for now, testing with UK-based 3DS commercial cards would be more effective in a live production environment.

Please see this [ticket](https://checkout1360.zendesk.com/agent/tickets/15568) as an example.
