## Process Steps

Merchant Care team can seek assistance from the Scheme reporting team to resolve a ticket; hence the process of escalation from the Merchant Care team to Scheme Reporting.  
 

| **Process** | **Procedure Details** |
| --- | --- |
| **Initial Contact and Issue Logging** | - Customer Inquiry: Merchant reaches out to Merchant Care with a question, issue, or complaint at [support@checkout.com](mailto:support@checkout.com)   - Issue Logging: The front-line support agent logs the issue in the system, capturing all relevant details such as the customer’s information, the nature of the problem, and any previous interactions |
| **First-Level Support** | - Issue Resolution Attempt: The front-line (or Tier 1) support agent attempts to resolve the issue using resources like knowledge bases, FAQs, and the Security Metrics Portal.  - Time and Scope Evaluation: If the issue is simple and within the scope of Tier 1, the agent resolves it and closes the case. If the problem is more complex, it may require escalation.  - The merchant Care team utilises an internal Slack channel called. ask-pci-questions_merchant-care_scheme-reporting. Details can be found below [here](https://join.slack.com/share/enQtNzY4MTA5NzY3NjM4NS0zNDRkNzM4NmY3YzUzZmNkNzc1MzM2MGIyZmE5ZGYyZmIyOTBhNmVjYzU1N2Q3NGJlODc2NjZkMjVhN2NhZTgw). Alternatively reach out to a Team Leader. |
| **Decision to Escalate** | Criteria for Escalation - Issues are typically escalated if they:   - Are beyond the technical knowledge or authority of the Tier 1 agent.  - Require specialised skills or tools.  - Are time-sensitive or involve high-priority customers.  - Have been unresolved within a predefined time frame (SLA—Service Level Agreement).  Customer Notification: The customer is informed about the escalation, what it involves, and the expected timeline for resolution. |
| **Process of Escalation to Scheme Reporting team.** | The issue is escalated to Scheme Reporting Team support, where agents with more expertise and access to advanced tools or knowledge expertise to handle it. Escalation via Zendesk Macro:   - Utilize the pre-configured Zendesk Macro to Scheme Reporting to [pci.operations@checkout.com](mailto:pci.operations@checkout.com) |

1. Identify PCI Issue

2. Gather Information: Collect the following details from the merchant 

  1. Summary of the issue

  2. Steps Already taken(By merchant or checkout)

  3. Other relevant information (screenshot, ref numbers)

3. Escalation via Zendesk Macro:

  1. Utilize the pre-configured Zendesk Macro for PCI escalations

    1. Option 1: [pci.operations@checkout.com](mailto:pci.operations@checkout.com) for general PCI compliance concerns

    2. Option 2: [scheme.reporting@checkout.com](mailto:scheme.reporting@checkout.com) for issues specifically related to scheme reporting requirements
