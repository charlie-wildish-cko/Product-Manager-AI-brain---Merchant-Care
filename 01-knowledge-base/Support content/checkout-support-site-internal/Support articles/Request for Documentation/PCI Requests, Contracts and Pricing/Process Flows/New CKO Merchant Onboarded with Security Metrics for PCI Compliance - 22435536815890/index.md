## Process Steps

The following is the onboarding and registration process for clients.

- A client is onboarded with Checkout

- 
The scheme reporting team obtains the contact person's details in Salesforce and registers the user on the SecurityMetrics for their PCI DSS validation

- The Security Metrics team emails the client about the SAQ form and its link

- The client logs in to access the Security Metrics portal

- The client completes the SAQ paperwork online (Note: After onboarding, clients have 30 days to finish the PCI DSS SAQ Assessment)

- The Scheme Registration Team can then verify the client's PCI status on the SecurityMetrics portal
