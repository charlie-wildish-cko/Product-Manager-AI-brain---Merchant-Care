**When to use this article**

Merchant requests a copy of their Merchant Service Agreement (MSA) also referred to as a contract.MERCHANT SERVICE AGREEMENT INTRODUCTION 💬

Merchants may request a copy of their Merchant Service Agreement (MSA), which may also be called a 'contract'. A Merchant Service Agreement (MSA) is the legal contract between Checkout.com and a merchant that sets the terms for payment processing services. 

It typically covers things like services provided, fees and pricing schedules, settlement and chargebacks, data/security obligations, liabilities and termination.

⚠️ This document contains sensitive information and must be shared securely with the account signatory onlyKEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- Always verify the account signatory using the **KYC/KYB** tab before sharing the document

- Escalate to the Deal Management team via Zendesk if the contract is missing from Genesis

PROCESS FOR SHARING A MERCHANT'S MSA 🖊️Step 1. Access Genesis

- Log in to [Okta](https://checkout.okta.com/app/UserHome) and open [Genesis](https://genesis.cko-prod.ckotech.co/ob-tool-web)

Step 2. Locate the Merchant

- Use the client name, client ID, entity name, or entity ID to search for the merchant's profile
Step 3. Find the Contract

- Scroll to the bottom of the merchant's profile and select the **Contract** tab

- Here you can view the MSA and pricing details

Step 4. Verify the Account Signatory and Share

- Before sharing, cross-reference the **KYC/KYB** tab on the Genesis profile to confirm the designated account signatory

- This can be sent via Zendesk to the account signatory only

❌ If someone other than the designated account signatory requests the MSA, inform them that only the signatory can receive the contract and pricing details, and ask them to have the signatory contact you.ESCALATION ⏫

Escalate the case if the MSA is not available on the merchant's Genesis profile:

- 
Submit a request to the Deal Management team via Zendesk using the Deal Management macro to obtain the documents
