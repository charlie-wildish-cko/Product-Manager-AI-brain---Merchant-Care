## Introduction

Important: The two most common Internal declines are Internal 12 and Internal 2. If you have any questions or issues regarding these declines or any other declines then reach out to L2.

1. INTERNAL12 - Invalid Transaction: The transaction being attempted is invalid-- for example you are trying to issue a credit against a credit transaction (refunding a refund) or posting a refund more than the terminal limit (see docs [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CAC/pages/1147176344/Visa+Response+and+Error+codes#Acquirer-ResponseCode%3A)).

2. INTERNAL2 - Invalid Value/Amount:If payment transaction value is non-integer, negative, zero, or below the minimum permitted value (see docs [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CAC/pages/1147176344/Visa+Response+and+Error+codes#Acquirer-ResponseCode%3A)).

## Process Steps

### Internal 12:

1. Follow the process listed on confluence [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/MCL2/pages/6402277382/Handling+INTERNAL12+Declines)

2. If you have any questions or issues then reach out to L2 for support.

### Internal 2:

1. Follow the process listed on confluence [here](https://checkout.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/MCL2/pages/6411288620/Handling+INTERNAL2+Declines)

2. If you have any questions or issues then reach out to L2 for support.
