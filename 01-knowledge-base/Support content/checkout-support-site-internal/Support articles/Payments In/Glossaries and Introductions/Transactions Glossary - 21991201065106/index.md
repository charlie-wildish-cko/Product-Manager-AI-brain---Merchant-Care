Use this glossary to understand key terms relating to 'transactions'

## KEY TERMS & DEFINITIONS 📖

| **Term** | **Definition** |
| --- | --- |
| **ROW** | Refers to the "Rest of the World," encompassing geographical regions outside a specific primary operational area. |
| **NAS** | New Account Structure (NAS), is used to manage and configure customer accounts, process transactions, and handle payment instruments within Checkout's payment gateway system. |
| **Gateway Merchants ** | Gateway merchants only use Checkout's technology to be able to process their transactions. Gateway merchants need a third-party acquirer (TPA) to process their transactions and get their settlements. |
| **Non-Gateway Merchants ** | Non-gateway merchants use Cheeckout for both the technology and acquiring services. |
| **TPA** | Third-Party Acquirer (TPA) is a licensed bank or financial institution in card networks like Visa or MasterCard. They manage merchant bank accounts and handle transaction approvals for merchants. TPAs connect issuing banks to merchants, approving or rejecting transactions based on issuing bank and card network data. |
| **Acquirer** | A bank or financial institution that processes credit or debit card payments on behalf of merchants. They sit between the gateway and the card schemes. |
| **SAB** | Saudi Awwal Bank - This institution serves as an acquirer operating within Saudi Arabia. |
| **Mashreq ** | Mashreq Bank - This is identified as our principal acquirer within the United Arab Emirates. |
| **MPGS ** | MPGS, or MasterCard Payment Gateway Services, is a comprehensive payment processing platform provided by MasterCard which our third-party acquirer (TPA) utilizes. |
