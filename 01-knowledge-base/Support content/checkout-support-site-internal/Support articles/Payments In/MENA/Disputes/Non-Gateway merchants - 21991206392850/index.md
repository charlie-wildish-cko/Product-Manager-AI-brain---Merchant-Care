## Process Steps

1. For Non-Gateway merchants, the disputes team will handle this. Care will have to transfer the case to the disputes team.

2. Go into Zendesk, Click on apply macro, click on transfer, click on disputes. 

  1. Once selected a side conversation will be brought up with a template, fill in the template. 

  2. Once done click on ‘insert ticket comments’ and add the previous emails (this provided more context and details for the dispute team). 

**Important**: In your email to the Disputes team, request them to communicate directly with the merchant and ensure you provide them with the merchant contact details. If preferred, CC in the merchant directly but ensure you have removed any information that is for internal use only!
