**When to use this article**

To understand Third Party Acquiring TPA and the role Checkout.com plays in this.

 

## DESCRIBE THE ISSUE 💬

Third Party Acquiring TPA, sometimes called third-party processing, is a business model where an external company (in this case Checkout.com) rather than the merchant or the acquiring bank manages electronic payment processing for the merchant. 

This approach offers several advantages, such as convenience, quicker setup, scalability, and support for multiple payment methods. It also helps merchants expand globally, ease regulatory requirements, and access extra services like fraud prevention and detailed reporting.

## KEY PLAYERS IN TPA 🔑

**Merchant**: A business that sells goods or services online to consumers.

**Third Party Acquirer Processor**: A financial institution or payment service provider like Checkout.com that offers e-commerce payment processing and other services such as transaction processing, fraud prevention, and reporting. These institutions can connect a merchant with multiple acquiring banks.

**Acquiring Bank**: An institution with an acquiring license that authorizes and settles payments to merchants. Acquiring banks also manage chargebacks and take on the liability for potential fraud.

CHECKOUT.COM'S ROLE IN THIRD PARTY ACQUIRING 👀

Checkout acts as a third-party acquirer processor or a payment gateway technology provider in markets where it is difficult to obtain a regulatory license or the business opportunity is minimal. Checkout relies on various payment gateways to process payments for its merchants. These include:

- 
**MPGS (Mastercard Payment Gateway Service)**: A web-based gateway that offers a range of payment products for authentication, tokenization, and payment processing. Checkout uses its Web-API to execute payments directly. MPGS supports both single-message and dual-message processing modes, though some markets, like Saudi Arabia, only use the single-message mode due to local regulations.

- 
**Cybersource (A Visa product)**: This gateway offers both card-present and card-not-present payment products. Checkout uses Cybersource for card-not-present online payment processing.

- 
**Saudi Payment Gateway (SPG)**: A local payment gateway in Saudi Arabia. As per the central bank (SAMA) regulations, all local e-commerce traffic must be processed through SPG. Checkout aims to become a certified Saudi payment gateway to provide a full stack of supported features.

Key Features and Services offered by Checkout

Through its various gateway connections, Checkout offers a range of features to merchants, including:

- Account verification

- Purchase payments (SMS)

- Authorization payments (DMS)

- Full and partial voids and captures

- Full and partial refunds

- Merchant-initiated payments (recurring/unscheduled)

- Network tokens and wallet payments (Apple/Google)

- Dynamic billing descriptor

- Fraud prevention

- Reporting and analytics
