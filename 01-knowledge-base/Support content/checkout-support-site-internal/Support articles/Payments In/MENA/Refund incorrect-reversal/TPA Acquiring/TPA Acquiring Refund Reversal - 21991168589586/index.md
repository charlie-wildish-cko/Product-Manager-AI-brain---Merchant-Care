**Use this article when**

A third party acquirer requests a refund reversal.INTRODUCTION TO THE ISSUE💬

A merchant may request a refund reversal for accidental refunds where they sent the wrong amount to a customer, for example a refund of $10 instead of the intended $5.

The ability to reverse a refund depends on the acquiring bank used for the transaction. This process is only possible if the transaction was processed through a Checkout.com acquirer see 🔗 [Visa and Mastercard Refund Reversals](https://checkoutint.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/27767296982290-Visa-and-Mastercard-Refund-Reversals).

**If the acquiring bank is a TPA (Third-Party Acquiring) processor:** It is not possible to reverse the refund. In this case, you will not be able to undo the credit.
