## Process Steps

1. The third party acquirer may have charged the customer twice which can lead to a duplicate transaction

2. If the transaction has not been captured twice then you should reach out to the acquirer to investigate further.

3. Share the transaction details with the acquirer and ask them to confirm if they have charged the customer twice

  1. If the acquirer confirms they have charged twice, then they will refund the customer directly

  2. If they have not, revert back to the merchant explaining that this is not the case and request the merchant to inform the customer to reach out to their bank for a reversal.
