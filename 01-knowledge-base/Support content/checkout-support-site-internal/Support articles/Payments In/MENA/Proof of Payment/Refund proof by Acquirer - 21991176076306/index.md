## Introduction

**Important**: No proof of refund is requested for the Schemes, Merchant Care only reach out to the acquirer  

## Process Steps

1. Reach out to the acquirer (find the contact details in the [FP spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ipGueNYij7yEEr1y9S-0Ba0lqS9Ia3Wbsnm6YuvwoaQ/edit#gid=1771915325)) as a side conversation on the ticket in Zendesk, with the following details listed below and request them to provide proof of refund:

  1. Auth RRN:

  2. MID:

  3. Card:

  4. Auth Code:

  5. Transaction Date:

  6. Transaction Amount:

  7. Refund Amount:

2. Once the acquirer sends the proof, you can share this with the merchant.
