**When to use this article**   

To handle inquiries from merchants who have a dedicated Account Manager. 

## DESCRIBE THE ISSUE 💬

An account-managed merchant has contacted support with an inquiry, you should identify the product or feature in question and then ensure the case is properly handed off to the merchant's assigned Account Manager. 

## KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- Identify the product or feature the merchant is asking about

- Update the support ticket with a private note for the Account Manager and tag them

- Reply to the merchant, letting them know their Account Manager has been notified and will follow up

## PROCESS FOR HANDLING ACCOUNT-MANAGED MERCHANT INQUIRIES 🖊️

**Step 1. Identify the Product or Feature**                                

- First, determine the specific product or feature the merchant's inquiry is about

- Refer to this [documentation](https://www.checkout.com/docs) under the "Products" tab to find this information

**Step 2. Update the Ticket and Reply to the Merchant**                               

- Before submitting or solving the case, add a private note to the ticket

- In the private note, summarize the merchant's request and include any additional relevant comments

- Make sure to tag the assigned Account Manager

- Next, reply directly to the merchant, informing them that their Account Manager has been copied on the ticket and will contact them as soon as possible

**Step 3. Solve the Case**                               

- After you have replied to the merchant and updated the ticket with the private note, you can solve the case                          

## FAQs** ****❓**

What if I can't identify the Account Manager?

If you cannot find the assigned Account Manager, follow your team's standard procedure for internal inquiries or consult with a team lead.What if the merchant replies again after the ticket is solved?

If the merchant replies, the ticket will reopen. Follow the same procedure and ensure the Account Manager is still aware of the new communication.
