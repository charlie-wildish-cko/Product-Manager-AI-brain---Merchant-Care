## Introduction

Historically, Merchant Care sent tickets to Customer Due Diligence (CDD) enquiring about the status of sub-entity onboarding applications. However, this information lives in the [Requirements Due Dashboard](https://checkoutinternal.eu.looker.com/explore/ra_platform/fct_ra_platform?toggle=fil&qid=lKoPAzIuDVgzDRg1rAQmJ2). Merchant Care can self-serve and obtain this information. All that they will need to obtain from the customer is the **Sub-entity ID,** which is an alphanumeric string (e.g. ent_f6pgwcfmjeiirozv3zxyczusly). This is the unique customer ID to ensure the correct information is being filtered.

## Process Steps

1. Open the [dashboard](https://checkoutinternal.eu.looker.com/explore/ra_platform/fct_ra_platform?toggle=fil&qid=lKoPAzIuDVgzDRg1rAQmJ2)

2. Under **Filters**, locate the **Client Attributes Sub Entity ID** field, and enter the **Sub-entity ID**:- 

3. 
On the top right, click run:-

4. The results will appear below the Filters section:-

5. The pertinent information to share will be under the __**Requirements External Message**__ text

6. If more clarity is needed on the application status, i.e. the information is not clear, please raise your query with the CDD team by emailing [cdd_mu@checkout.com](mailto:cdd_mu@checkout.com) with the exact details
