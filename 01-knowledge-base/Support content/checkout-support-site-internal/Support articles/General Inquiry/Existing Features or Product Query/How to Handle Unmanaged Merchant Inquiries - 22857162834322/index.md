**When to use this article**

When working inquiries from merchants who are not assigned to a specific account manager, they are supported by Merhant Care.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- 
**Ticket Identification:** The first step is to receive an inquiry from an unmanaged merchant.

- 
**Inquiry Classification:** Categorize the inquiry as a straightforward question, a feature request, or a complex issue.

- 
**Actionable Steps:** Follow a distinct process for each inquiry type, including checking documentation, submitting requests, or escalating the ticket.

- 
**Correct Escalation:** Use specific macros for escalation to ensure all necessary information is included.

- 
**Ticket Management:** Do not close the ticket until the merchant's issue is fully resolved by the Merchant Care team.

## PROCESS FOR HANDLING UNMANAGED MERCHANT INQUIRIES 🖊️

Step 1. Review and Triage the Request

- Review the merchant's request to understand the type of inquiry 

**If it's a straightforward question**

- Check the internal and external Zendesk knowledge base for the answer and support the merchant or escalate via slack

- Use the **NPOA macro** when transferring as this will help you include all relevant details

**If it's a feature request**

- For a **Dashboard** feature: Submit the request to the Dashboard team on [Jira](https://checkout.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/111/group/462/create/277) on behalf of the merchant

- For **payment interface** features: Fill out the appropriate feature request [form](https://forms.gle/21VR17YendvEoDZe7) on behalf of the merchant

- **If it's a complex query:** Escalate the ticket to the Merchant Care Level 2 team using the **L1>L2 macro**

**⚠️**** **Do not close the ticket until the Merchant Care team fully resolves the merchant's issue

## FAQs** ****❓**

What is an "unmanaged" merchant?

An unmanaged merchant is a client who does not have a dedicated account manager. Their support inquiries are handled by Merchant Care.
