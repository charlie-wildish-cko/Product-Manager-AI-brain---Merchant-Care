## Tools & Permissions

Below are the tools that Merchant Care uses to handle issues related to General Inquiry:

| **Tool** | **Purpose** |
| --- | --- |
| **Zendesk** | Zendesk is a case management platform that handles support requests, allowing users to raise cases via email or a dashboard. It integrates with systems to enrich support tickets with necessary information for processing by agents. |
| **Salesforce** | Salesforce is a customer relationship management (CRM) platform focused on sales, customer service, and analytics. It helps create customer-centric experiences through automation and cross-functional insight and reporting, optimizing processes and simplifying collaboration. |
