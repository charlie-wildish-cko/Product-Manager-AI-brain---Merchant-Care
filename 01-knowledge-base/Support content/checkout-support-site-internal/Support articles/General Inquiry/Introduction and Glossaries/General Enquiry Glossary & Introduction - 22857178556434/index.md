## Key Terms & Definitions

| **Term** | **Definition** |
| --- | --- |
| **Prospect Merchant** | A prospect merchant is a merchant who is in the process of reviewing whether to be onboarded. |
| **Leads** | Lead merchants are those who are still in discussions with the commercial team, have not yet signed an agreement, and do not have an opportunity created in Salesforce. |
