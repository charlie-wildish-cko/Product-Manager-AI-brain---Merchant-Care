A **Prospect / Lead** is a merchant who is in the process of reviewing by commercial whether to be onboarded or not.

**How to identify whether a merchant is Prospect/Leads ?**

- Access salesforce via OKTA

- After gaining access to Salesforce, enter the prospect/lead account name in the search bar and select the merchant’s account, such as the prospect merchant 'Flutura', as shown below.

- The merchant type will then be listed as Prospect or Lead, as shown below.

The merchant type prospect can be found in the upper left-hand corner, as shown.

The merchant type Lead, labeled as CKO Lead, is located in the upper left-hand corner, as shown.

 

**If an inquiry is received from a prospect or Lead, the agent should:**

- First we should identify the region of the prospect/leads. 

The region of a lead or prospect merchant can be found in Salesforce under the Lead/Prospect account, in the Billing address section.

- Select the macro “Prospect/Lead Merchant Inquiry” found in the “Dispatch” macro folder, select the based on the region

- In the **'Insert ticket comments'** section of the side conversation, ensure the checkbox for the initial email thread is **selected** prior to sending.

-
