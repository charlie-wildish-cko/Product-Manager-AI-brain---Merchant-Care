**How to identify whether a merchant is Partner Prospect ?**

- Access Salesforce, search for the prospect/lead account name, select the merchant’s account, and the merchant type will be listed as Prospect, as shown below.

**When an inquiry is received from a Partner Prospect Merchant or Lead, the agent should follow these steps:**

- Select the "Partner Inquiry" Macro found in the “Dispatch” macro folder

- Ensure the Request is Directed to the Correct Sales Manager/Engineer

Identify the appropriate Sales Manager for the account. This information can be found in Salesforce under the partner account.

- If no Sales Manager is assigned as the opportunity owner, direct the inquiry to Tamara (tamara.lemagnen@checkout.com)

- Once the case has been forwarded to the Sales Manager, close the ticket.
