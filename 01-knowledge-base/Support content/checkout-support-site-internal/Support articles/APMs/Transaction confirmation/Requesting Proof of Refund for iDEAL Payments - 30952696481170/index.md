**When to use this article**   

When a customer reports they have not received funds for an iDEAL payment that is already marked as 'Refunded' - the merchant will request proof of refund.   

**Solution:** L1 team requests the Proof of Refund from the Recon cilliation team and forwards it to the merchant.

## DESCRIBE THE ISSUE 💬

**A merchant** contacts the Support team to report a missing refund for an **iDEAL payment**. The payment status in the system is already recorded as **'Refunded'**, but the customer reports that the funds have not been credited back to their account. The merchant requires a **Proof of Refund** document to help the customer trace the funds with their bank.               

## KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- The L1 team is responsible for obtaining the Proof of Refund, Do not escalate the ticket to the L2 team for this request

- The request for the proof must be sent directly to the Reconciliation team via email

- Do not escalate the ticket to the L2 team for this request.

- A specific email template is available and should be used.

- Once received from Recon, forward the Proof of Refund to the merchant and mark the ticket as "Solved".

**PROCESS FOR OBTAINING iDEAL PROOF OF REFUND 🖊️****    **

### Step 1. Identify the Missing Refund Request

Confirm the issue is a missing refund for an **iDEAL payment** that shows a **'Refunded'** status.

### Step 2. Request Proof of Refund via Email

❌ Do **NOT** escalate the ticket to the L2 team.

Directly contact the **Reconciliation team** from Zendesk using the designated macro below, ensure you include all necessary payment details in the template:

| Zendesk Macro: Transfer>Reconciliations>APM>Ideal Refund |
| --- |

### Step 3. Wait for Proof of Refund and Forward On

-  Wait for the Reconciliation team to reply with the Proof of Refund. 

- Once the Proof of Refund is received, forward it directly to the merchant.         

- Mark the ticket as "Solved".
