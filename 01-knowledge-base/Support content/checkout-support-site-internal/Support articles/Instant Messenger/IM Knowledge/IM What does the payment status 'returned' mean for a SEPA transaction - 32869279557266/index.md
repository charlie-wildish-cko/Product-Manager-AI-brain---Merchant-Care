Use this article when

A merchant is querying a SEPA 'returned' payment status.**Merchant Response 🗣️**

The 'returned' payment status for a SEPA transaction means that after the payment was initially captured, it was then rejected by the customer’s bank, and the funds are being returned to the customer.

[See this knowledge article.](https://www.checkout.com/docs/payments/add-payment-methods/sepa-direct-debit#Payment_returns_and_chargebacks)

 

**提问****：SEPA 交易中的“已退回 (Returned)”支付状态是什么意思？**

**答：****在 SEPA 直接扣款交易中，“已退回 (Returned)”状态表示：**

- **该笔付款在最初成功确认（Captured）后，随后被客户的银行拒绝。**

- **相关资金正被退回至客户账户。**

**注意：这通常发生在款项结算后的追溯阶段。**
