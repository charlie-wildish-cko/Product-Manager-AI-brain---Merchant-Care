**Use this article when**

A merchant asks what the minimum and maximum amount that they can process and capture successfully is.**Merchant Response 🗣️**

Card schemes and payment methods define their own unique limits on the minimum and maximum amounts that can be processed and captured in a single transaction. 

  
To view the limits of a card scheme, see its respective documentation. To view the limits for alternate payment methods supported by Checkout.com, select the payment method on our [website](https://www.checkout.com/payment-method/accept).

单笔交易可处理及确认的最低和最高金额限制，是由各个卡组织（Card Schemes）及付款方式（Payment Methods）自行设定的 。

如需了解特定卡组织（如 Visa, Mastercard 等）的具体金额限制，请参阅其对应的官方文档 。

如需了解 Checkout.com 支持的各类替代付款方式（APMs）的限额，请访问我们的[官方网站](https://www.checkout.com/payment-method/accept)并选择相应的付款方式进行查询 。
