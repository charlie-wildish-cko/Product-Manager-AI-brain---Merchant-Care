**Problem: **The settlement shows as completed on the dashboard, but the funds haven't been received.

 

- **Definition:** "Completed" means Checkout.com has **approved** the settlement.

- **Merchant Note:** This does not mean the money is in the bank yet. Bank processing times are outside of Checkout.com's control and may cause delays.

- **Merchant Knowledge: **[Manage settlements](https://www.checkout.com/docs/funds-management/receive-settlements/manage-settlements#Settlement_statuses)

Merchant Response 🗣️

Completed means Checkout has approved the settlement.   
_Note: The bank may receive it a bit later (we do not have control over that)._  
Refer to the documentation [here](https://www.checkout.com/docs/funds-management/receive-settlements/manage-settlements#Settlement_statuses).

 

**提问：为什么管理后台显示结算状态为“已完成 (Completed)”？**

**答:**

-状态显示为“已完成”意味着 Checkout.com 已批准该笔结算 。  
-**银行处理时间**：请注意，银行收到款项的时间可能会稍晚（这超出了我们的控制范围）。  
请参阅相关文档 [here](https://www.checkout.com/docs/funds-management/receive-settlements/manage-settlements#Settlement_statuses)。
