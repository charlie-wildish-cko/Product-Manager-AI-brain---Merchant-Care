**Use this article when**

The merchant has followed the system prompts to reset their **Security Metrics** account password, but the automated reset email has not arrived in their inbox.Merchant Response 🗣️

IIf you are experiencing a delay in receiving your password reset link, please perform the following checks:

- **Allow for Latency:** Automated emails can sometimes experience delivery delays due to network traffic. Please allow a few minutes for the message to arrive.

- **Check Filters and Spam:** Verify that the email was not automatically routed to your **Spam** or **Junk** folders by your email provider.

- **Verify Recipient Address:** Ensure you are checking the inbox of the email address specifically registered to the Security Metrics account.

If you have completed the steps above and still have not received the reset email, please contact the support team directly for manual assistance: **scheme.reporting@checkout.com**.

 

**提问****：我们按照提示重置了此安全指标 (Security Metrics) 账户的密码，但收件箱一直没有收到重置邮件。**

**答：**如果您已按照提示重置密码但未收到邮件，请尝试以下步骤：

- 由于可能存在网络延迟，请等待几分钟以便邮件送达。

- 请检查您的垃圾邮件文件夹，确保邮件没有被自动过滤到该处。

- 如果仍未收到，请发送电子邮件至 scheme.reporting@checkout.com 获取进一步支持。
