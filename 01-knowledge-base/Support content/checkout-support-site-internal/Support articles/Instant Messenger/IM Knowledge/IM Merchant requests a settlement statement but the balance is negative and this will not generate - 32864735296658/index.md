**Use this article when**

You are supporting a merchant on Instant Messenger (IM) and they have a query about Reporting & Account Balances.Settlement Statement & Negative Balances 🗒️ 

**Issue:** Merchant cannot find or generate a Settlement Statement.

**Root Cause:** Statements are **not generated** if the account balance is negative.

**Solution:** Advise the merchant to use the **Financial Reports** or **Balance Report** in the Dashboard to view specific fund movements within a date range.Merchant Response 🗣️

You can download financial reports by specifying a date range through the dashboard whenever a financial action or movement occurs. You can also use the Balance Report to get an overview of fund movements and balances within a specified time frame.

**提问：我申请了结算单报告 (Settlement Statement)，但账户余额为负数，该怎么办？**

**答:**

**-  当账户余额为负数时，系统不会生成结算单 。**  
**-  建议商户通过管理后台 (Dashboard)，根据特定日期范围下载“财务报告 (Financial Reports)”，以查看任何财务操作或资金变动 。**  
**-  您也可以使用“余额报告 (Balance Report)”来查看指定时间范围内的资金流动和余额概览 。**
