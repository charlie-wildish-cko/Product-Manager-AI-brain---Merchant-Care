**Topic:** Changes to Funds Movements, Settlement Timelines, and Billing Corrections 

**Effective Dates:** Rollouts occurring in February (See Schedule)

### **Overview**

Checkout.com is proactively notifying specific merchants regarding a discrepancy in their billing calculations due to recent changes in how balances and reports are processed. This issue affects Minimum Monthly Billing (MMB) and Tiered Pricing calculations.

We are issuing a financial adjustment in the **January invoice (issued February 1, 2026)** to correct this and ensure the merchant's financial position is accurate.

### **Key Details**

- **Impact Date:** Adjustments will appear on the January invoice, issued **February 1, 2026.**

- **Root Cause:** Earlier changes to balances and reports caused invoices to reflect **Pending** transactions instead of **Available** transactions.

- **The Error:** Minimum billing and tiered pricing calculations did not originally reflect these reporting changes.

- **The Fix:** A financial adjustment will be applied to cover January’s transactions.

### **Affected Merchant Segments**

There are three versions of the communication being sent, depending on how the merchant is billed. You must verify which category the merchant falls into before responding.

| **Segment** | **Description** | **Impact** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Version 1** | **MMB Only** | The adjustment specifically corrects the **Minimum Monthly Billing** calculation |
| **Version 2** | **Tiered Pricing Only** | The adjustment corrects **Tiered Pricing** calculations, including any changes to the merchant's billing tier |
| **Version 3** | **MMB + Tiered Pricing** | The adjustment applies to **both** Minimum Monthly Billing and Tiered Pricing |

### **Agent Talking Points & FAQ**

Use the following points to explain the situation to merchants who contact support.**1. Why is there an adjustment on my invoice?**

You should explain that we recently updated how we handle balances and reports.

- "These changes caused your previous invoices to reflect **Pending** transactions rather than **Available** transactions." 

- "We identified that the calculation for your [Minimum Billing / Tiered Pricing] did not account for this change initially." 

**2. When will this be fixed?**

- "The correction is being made now. You will see a financial adjustment on your **January invoice**, which is issued on **February 1, 2026**." 
**3. What does this adjustment cover?**

- "This adjustment ensures you have the correct financial position for **January’s transactions**."

- _(If Tiered Pricing applies)_: "This also accounts for any necessary changes to your billing tier." 

**4. Is this a recurring issue?**

- No. This is a retrospective fix to ensure their financial position is correct following the system changes.

### **Tone & Empathy**

When speaking with merchants, please acknowledge the frustration this may cause.

- We treat all service issues seriously

- We understand that accurate billing is critical for their business

- Apologize sincerely for the inconvenience
