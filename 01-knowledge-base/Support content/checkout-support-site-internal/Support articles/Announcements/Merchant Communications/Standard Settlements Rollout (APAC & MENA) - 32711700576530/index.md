**Topic:** Changes to Balances and Reports (Funds Movements) 

**Regions Affected:** APAC and MENA 

**Status:** Rollout Scheduled for February 2025

### **Overview 💬**

Merchants with entities in APAC and MENA are receiving communication regarding improvements to how funds movements are displayed in their balances and reports. These changes involve standardizing settlement timelines, which may require merchants to update their settings to avoid delays.

### **Rollout Schedule ▶️**

The effective date of these changes depends on the specific region and entity configuration:

- **APAC + MENA Entities:** The rollout will take place from **February 4th onwards**.

- 
**MENA-only Entities:** The rollout will take place from **February 4th onwards**.

  - _Note:_ For MENA entities, the specific date may depend on the products used (acquiring, Card/Bank Payouts, or issuing).

- **APAC-only Entities:** The rollout will take place from **February 10th onwards**.

### **Critical Merchant Action Required ⚠️**

Merchants in the APAC region (or with APAC entities) have been explicitly instructed to take action to ensure smooth settlements.

- **Action:** Update the entity's settlement time zone to align with the **local time zone**.

- **Deadline:** Merchants are advised to make this update before **January 19, 2025**.

### **Potential Issues & Troubleshooting ⚒️**

**Why is a merchant experiencing settlement delays?**

If a merchant has not updated their settlement time zone to match their local time, settlements may be generated after local banking hours following the rollout. This mismatch can cause a delay in the actual settlement reaching their account.**What happens to "Pending Funds" during the transition?**

If a merchant switches to local time settlements _after_ the changes have already been implemented:

- Pending funds accumulated up to the day of the time zone update will remain pending initially.

- These funds will move into "Available" only **after** the first local time settlement is generated.

### **Agent Talking Points 🗣️**

Use these points when explaining the situation to inquiring merchants:

- **For APAC/Combined Merchants:** "To prevent delays caused by banking hour mismatches, please ensure your settlement time zone matches your entity's local time. Ideally, this should be done before January 19th."

- **For MENA Merchants:** "We are rolling out improvements to how funds movements appear in your reports starting February 4th. The exact date depends on the specific products you utilize (such as acquiring or payouts)."

- **For Merchants noticing "missing" funds during the switch:** "If you recently updated your time zone, accumulated pending funds will move to 'Available' immediately following the generation of your first local time settlement."
