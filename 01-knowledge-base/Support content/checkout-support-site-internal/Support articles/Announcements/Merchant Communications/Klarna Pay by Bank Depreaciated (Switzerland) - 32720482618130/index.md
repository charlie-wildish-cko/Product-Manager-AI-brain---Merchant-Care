**Effective Date:** March 31, 2026 

**Affected Region:** Switzerland (CH) 

**Affected Product:** Klarna Pay by Bank

## Overview 💬

Klarna is officially shutting down its **Pay by Bank** payment method specifically for the **Swiss market**, effective **March 31, 2026**.

Merchants selling to customers in Switzerland must remove this specific payment option from their checkout flows before the deadline to prevent failed transactions and customer frustration.

## Merchant Instructions ⚒️

When communicating with affected merchants, advise them to take the following steps immediately:

1. **Audit Payment Configuration:** Check if "Klarna Pay by Bank" is active for Swiss shoppers.

2. **Disable the Method:** Remove Klarna Pay by Bank from any payment selection pages displayed to Swiss customers.

3. **Deadline:** This must be completed **before March 31, 2026**.

## Agent Talking Points 🗣️

**If a merchant asks why this is being removed:**

"Klarna has made the decision to sunset this specific method in Switzerland due to technical differences in Swiss banking interfaces. 

They found these differences were causing performance issues and wanted to ensure customers didn't experience failed payments or errors at checkout. They are proactively closing the service to protect the consumer experience."
