**OVERVIEW OF SEPA B2B**** 👀**

**SEPA Direct Debit (SDD)** B2B is a direct debit scheme designed specifically for business-to-business transactions. It is similar to SDD Core but tailored for higher-value transactions between businesses.

**What we launched:**

- SEPA Direct Debit B2B scheme designed specifically for **B2B collections** across Europe.

- Provides faster response from banks (2 days after collection) in case of failure or inability to realise collection.

 Click for a description and benefit information

### **Overview**

**Timing:** Similar to SDD Core, the timing is not necessarily instant, although SEPA B2B Direct Debit offers a faster response time from the banks in case of technical failure or being unable to realise the collection (2 days after collection)

 

**Mandates**: The mandate process for B2B is more stringent than for SEPA Core. Besides the customer's authorisation, the paying business needs to confirm the mandate directly with its bank, which adds an extra layer of verification.

- **Refunds**: The payment is deemed final 3 business days after the debit date (there is no ‘no-questions-asked’ refund policy for 8 weeks like there is with Core)

- The cut off time for processing is 1hr before the Core scheme 12:00 D-1 CET for B2B vs Core at 13:00 D-1 CET

### **Benefits**:

- SEPA connects you across Europe – with standardized euro payments that work just like local transfers.

- By harmonizing payment processes, SEPA Direct Debit reduces processing costs and simplifies reconciliation – helping you save time and money.

- SEPA provides predictable settlement timelines and clear mandates – giving you visibility and control over your incoming payments.

- With one consistent experience across all participating countries, SEPA helps you grow across Europe – including non EU markets – without building multiple payment setups.

### **Value to Merchants:**

- Enables secure, automated collections between businesses, reducing manual invoicing efforts.

- **Certainty of cash flow,** funds are guaranteed after 3 days.

- Stronger mandate validation reduces fraud and disputes- as the paying business needs to confirm their mandates directly with their bank, adding an extra layer of validation.

- Ideal for recurring B2B billing, SaaS and high-value invoices.

### **Advantages**

- **High Security:** SEPA B2B Direct Debits are secure due to a mandate check carried out by banks.

- **Reduced Errors:** Automated processes minimize the chances of mistakes in payments.

- **Low Risk of Nonpayment:** Automated processes reduce the risk of missed payments.

- **Cash Flow Certainty:** Businesses can plan with confidence regarding incoming payments, improving cash flow management.

### **Disadvantages:**

- 
**Limited Availability:** Not all banks support SEPA B2B Direct Debits (a minority, and often neo-banks - in such case, the merchant can fallback to SEPA Core)
**❌ Irrevocable Mandates:** Once authorised, direct debits cannot be cancelled, even if an error occurs in issuing the mandate.
