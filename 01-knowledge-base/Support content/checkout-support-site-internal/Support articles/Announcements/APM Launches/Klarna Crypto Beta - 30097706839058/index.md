OVERVIEW OF KLARNA CRYPTO 👀

Klarna’s Pay Now product, already trusted by millions of consumers across Europe is now available to crypto-related merchants. 

Whether you're a trading platform, wallet provider, or blockchain-based service, you can now let customers pay via bank transfer using Klarna’s familiar brand and payment experience.

This is not about accepting crypto. Klarna Pay Now is a fiat-based, account-to-account solution—designed for compliance, speed, and trust.
