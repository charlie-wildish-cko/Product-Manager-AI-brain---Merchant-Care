**OVERVIEW OF VIPPS MOBILEPAY**** 👀**

Vipps MobilePay (VM) is a leading Nordic mobile wallet that allows users to make instant payments and money transfers using their smartphones. It's a digital alternative to traditional cards, enabling users to pay online and in-store and transfer money to friends and family simply using a phone number.
