**OVERVIEW OF TWINT**** 👀**

TWINT is a mobile payment wallet. Users can either make the payment via their bank-provided TWINT app (pass-through wallet) or by using  TWINT prepaid (staged wallet).

With more than **5 million active users, **the number of transactions in physical stores has doubled since the previous year, covering more than **76% of online shops** in Switzerland.

Given the popularity of TWINT as a payment method, there’s an active pipeline of 12 merchants, with a potential processing volume of up $44m monthly.
