**Use this article when**

A merchant that was part of the T4/5 product expansion contacts us about the fees.

If a merchant asks why they are being charged during a free period, explain that:

- The product service fee is waived for 2 months.

- Scheme fees (charged by card networks) still apply to merchants on IC++ pricing and must be covered by the merchant.

If a T4/5 merchant finds their statements confusing. You need to explain why "Free" doesn't always mean "0.00" on a statement. Break down the three parts of a transaction fee:

- 
**Interchange:** Paid to the customer's bank (e.g., 0.2% - 1.5%).

- 
**Scheme Fee (+):** Paid to Visa/Mastercard (e.g., 0.02% - 0.15%).

- 
**Service Fee (+):** Our fee for providing the product.

Explain that during the 2-month intro, we are waiving our Service Fee. However, the banks and card networks (Visa/Mastercard) still charge their standard fees, which are passed through to the merchant at cost.

### **The opt-out process**

Merchants can opt-out at any point.  If a merchant chooses to opt-out:

- Follow the [Opt-Out Procedure](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lXfsIHvBfTlbRW8GSS0gC2Vv6erRXmr_xa5jFYsHOqc/edit?tab=t.0).

- Ensure the request is logged before the April/May activation deadline.

### **Why we’re doing this**

If a merchant asks why these changes are happening now, explain we are providing these tools automatically to help their business grow with minimal manual configuration.
