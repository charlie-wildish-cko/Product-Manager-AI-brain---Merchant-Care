This update explains the February 2026 initiative to enable new products and update pricing for unmanaged Tier 4 (T4) and Tier 5 (T5) merchants.**Overview**

From late February 2026, we are proactively expanding the product suites for our T4 and T5 merchants. We are providing a transparent, value-driven intro period for Value Added Services (VAS) and Alternative Payment Methods (APMs).

**Key dates**

- 
**By 26 February:** All merchant communications sent.

- 
**April to May:** Automatic product activation (unless the merchant opts out).

- 
**2-month intro period:** New products are free to use for the first two months, excluding scheme fees.

**General FAQs****Why are these products being enabled on my account automatically?**

We are evolving our service to be "Better Every Day." By proactively switching on these features, we ensure your business has access to the latest growth and security tools-like Alternative Payment Methods and Fraud Detection, without you needing to manage complex manual setups.

**Is this a free trial?**

It is a **2-month free intro period**. We are providing the service for free for the first two months so you can see the value it adds to your business. After this period, the standard rates outlined in your March communication will apply.**When will the new products go live?**

Products will be activated 2 months after your initial notifiction email. You will receive a specific notification on your dashboard before the technical switch-on occurs.

**Billing & Pricing FAQs****Why am I seeing charges if the intro period is free?**

While we waive the service fees for these products during the first two months, merchants on IC++ pricing are still responsible for scheme fees. These are third-party costs charged by card networks (like Visa or Mastercard) that we pass through at cost.

**Where can I find the pricing that applies after the intro period?**

Full pricing transparency is a priority for this initiative. Your specific rates were included in the communication sent to you via email. If you cannot find this, I can look up your pricing tier for you.

### **Technical FAQs**

**Will Intelligent Acceptance (IA) break my checkout flow?**

IA is designed to improve authorization rates. However, it requires your checkout to handle 3DS step-ups. If your system isn't configured for this, we will not apply IA- to ensure your payments continue to process smoothly.**Do I need to do anything to enable Alternative Payment Methods (APMs)?**

Most are "low-lift" and will be enabled in the background. Some specific methods require background registration with the provider, which we will handle on your behalf.
