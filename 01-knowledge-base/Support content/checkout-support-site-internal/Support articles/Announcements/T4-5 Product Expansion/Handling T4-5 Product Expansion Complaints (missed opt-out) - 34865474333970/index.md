**Use this article when**

A merchant contacts us after the VAS T4/5 Product switch-on to complain that they did not want the new products or missed the opt-out window so they are being charged.

### **Acknowledge and de-escalate**

Start by validating the merchant's concern, avoid suggesting it was their fault for missing the email or the date to opt-out.

- 
**Do say:** "I understand you weren't expecting these products to be active. I can certainly help you manage your account features and look into your billing today."

- 
**Don't say:** "You were sent an email in February with a deadline to opt out."

### **Explain our approach**

Briefly explain that the activation was intended to prevent them from missing out on growth tools, rather than to catch them out.

"Our goal was to make sure you had access to tools like Intelligent Acceptance and Fraud Detection Pro immediately to help protect your revenue. We provided the two-month intro period so you could test the benefits without a service fee."

### **Offer immediate resolution**

If the merchant is insistent on removal:

- 
**Action:** Follow the standard [deactivation process](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lXfsIHvBfTlbRW8GSS0gC2Vv6erRXmr_xa5jFYsHOqc/edit?tab=t.0).

- 
**Billing:** If the merchant has already been charged a service fee (post-intro period), we will refund up to 1 month’s fees where they requested a cancellation and we did not action this in time.

### **Refunding Fees**

- Send requests for fee refunds with appropriate supporting documentation by using the below macro.

| Macro: Transfer>Finance>Approvals |
| --- |

- Once approval is received, you are  responsible for sending the approvals to billing by using the macro: Transfer>Billing>Refund Fees

- The billing team will ensure that this is adjusted for the merchant.
