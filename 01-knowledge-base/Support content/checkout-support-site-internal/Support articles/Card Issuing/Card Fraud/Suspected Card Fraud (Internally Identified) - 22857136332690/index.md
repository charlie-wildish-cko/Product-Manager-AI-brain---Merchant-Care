## Introduction

Checkout could be alerted to fraudulent activity on a client’s issuing account in 2 ways:-

1. Via the client or cardholder

2. Via internal teams e.g. Risk Operations or Compliance Operations

If Merchant Care becomes internally aware of suspected fraudulent issuing transactions, for any reason, please follow the below steps.

## Process Steps

1. Check if the client or cardholder has communicated with Merchant Care about this issue already

2. If they have, check the ticket and communication to see if they have specified which transaction(s) are potentially fraudulent

3. Check if the card is suspended (Dashboard > Issuing > Cards > Search by Card ID > Status)

  1. If the card is suspended, forward the case via email to [issuing_dispute@checkout.com](mailto:issuing_dispute@checkout.com) and CC [fraudsupport@checkout.com](mailto:fraudsupport@checkout.com)

  2. If the card is not suspended, send a request to [fraudsupport@checkout.com](mailto:fraudsupport@checkout.com) to suspend the card

4. Notify the relevant team based on step 3, and provide any additional information e.g. which specific transactions are affected

5. If there is still an open Merchant Care ticket, let the cardholder know you have informed the relevant team and that they will be in contact shortly

For reference: [Process workflow](https://www.figma.com/board/ZUJwwEK2zDnA1uESpMnvYy/Incoming-Fraud-Dispute-Process?node-id=0-1&p=f&t=PNdVWjC6hYbrOR7Y-0)
