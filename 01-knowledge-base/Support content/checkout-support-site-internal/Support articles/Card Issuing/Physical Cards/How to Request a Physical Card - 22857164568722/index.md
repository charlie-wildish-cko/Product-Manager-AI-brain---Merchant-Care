## Introduction

Physical cards can be requested in the Dashboard (or via API). But before creating one, the client must create a cardholder to issue it to, and before they can issue physical cards, the [card design](https://www.checkout.com/docs/card-issuing/design-and-ship-cards) must have been approved by Checkout.com and the card scheme.

If the client has already designed their card, submitted it during onboarding and had it approved, you can advise the client as per the below steps

## Process Steps

1. Sign in to [Dashboard](https://dashboard.checkout.com/)

2. Go to Issuing > Cards

3. Select “_Create cardholder”_

4. Enter the cardholder's details as required

5. Select “Create cardholder” (cardholder's profile then appears on the _Cardholders_ page)

6. Go to Issuing > Cards

7. Select “Create card”

8. Enter the card parameters

9. In the _Cardholder_ dropdown, select the cardholder you created in steps 3-5

10. Optionally, enter a “Display name” and “Reference” to help you identify the card

11. In the “Card product” dropdown, select the [card product](https://www.checkout.com/docs/card-issuing/card-program-setup#Card_products) you want to link the card to

12. Select “Create card”
