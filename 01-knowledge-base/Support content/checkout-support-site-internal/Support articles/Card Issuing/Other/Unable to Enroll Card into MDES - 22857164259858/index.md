## Introduction

MDES is Mastercard's tool that is used for card digitalisation e.g. if a client wants to use their card on Apple Pay or Google Pay. If the client is having issues with enrollment, you will need to escalate it.

## Process Steps

1. Escalate the case 

  1. Slack [#issuing_onboarding_operations](https://checkout.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C08HBJ361TR), or

  2. Email: [issuing_onboarding@checkout.com](mailto:issuing_onboarding@checkout.com)

2. Provide the following details:

  1. Client Entity

  2. Card or Cardholder ID (where applicable)

  3. Currency Requirements

  4. Merchant contact information for setup

  5. At which the enrolment process fails i.e. initiation, processing, completion

  6. Errors/screenshots
