**When to use this article **

This article is a comprehensive guide for resolving client and cardholder queries related to issuing invoices.

## DESCRIBE THE ISSUE 💬

A client or cardholder has contacted support with a query about a charge on their monthly issuing invoice. The agent needs to understand the invoice structure and billing components to accurately address the client's concern.KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑

- Invoices are generated automatically on the 1st of every month

- The billing period covers the previous calendar month

- Key invoice components include Account Information, Issuing Chargebacks, Issuing Other Fees, and Issuing Transactions

- The Dashboard is the primary tool for retrieving and reviewing invoices

- If the issue cannot be resolved, escalate to the #issuing-invoices Slack channel

PROCESS FOR RESOLVING ISSUING INVOICES QUERIES 🖊️ 

**Step 1. Gather Client Information**

- Ask the client for the specific monthly invoice the query relates to

- Request an explanation of the issue from the client's perspective

- Ask for the specific charge or line item that is being disputed or questioned

**Step 2. Access the Client's Account in the Dashboard**

- Log in to the Dashboard

- Use the "Search Clients and Entities" box to find the client's details

- Select the correct "Client" on the left and "Entity" on the right

- Click "Switch View" to access the client's account

**Step 3. Retrieve the Relevant Invoice**

- On the client’s home page, navigate to and expand the “Business account” section

- Click on “Invoices”

- Find and download the invoice corresponding to the client's query

**Step 4. Analyze the Invoice Details**

- Review the invoice, focusing on the sections below. Use the provided definitions to understand each component and explain it to the client.

  - 
**Account Information:**

    - 
**Invoice Currency:** The currency used for processing and invoicing

    - 
**Client Account:** The top-level structure consolidating all payment services for the merchant

    - 
**Entity:** A distinct operational unit within the client's organisation

    - 
**Entity ID:** A unique identifier for each entity

  - 
**Invoicing Information:**

    - 
**Issuing Chargebacks:** Fees for reversed or disputed transactions on issued cards.

    - 
**Issuing Other Fees:** Fees for additional services, such as virtual card issuance.

    - 
**Issuing Transactions:** Fees for processing transactions made with issued cards.

**Step 5. Explain the Charges to the Client**

- Based on your analysis, explain the specific charges or fees to the client clearly and concisely

- Confirm with the client that their query has been resolved

RESOLUTION ⚒️ 

Following these steps will allow you to successfully retrieve, understand, and explain the details of an issuing invoice to a client, resolving their query.

- 
**Remediation Steps:**

  - If a client believes there is a mistake, double-check the billing period and the specific transactions or services listed on the invoice

  - Cross-reference the charges with any documented service agreements or contracts

- 
**Check for Resolution:**

  - Confirm with the client that they understand the charges and are satisfied with the explanation

  - Ensure there are no further questions about the invoice

- 
**Rollback/Recovery:** Not applicable for this process, as you are only providing information and not making any changes to the client’s account

ESCALATION ⏫

- 
**Situations requiring escalation:**

  - The client disputes the charges after you have explained them, insisting they are incorrect.

  - You are unable to locate the specific charges or the invoice itself.

  - The client's query involves complex billing logic not covered in this guide.

- 
**Required Information:**

  - Provide the client's name, entity ID, and the specific invoice number

  - Summarize the client’s query and the steps you have already taken to resolve it

- 
**Instructions for agent:**

  - Stay on the case and monitor the #issuing-invoices Slack channel for updates

  - Notify the merchant that the issue has been escalated to a specialist team
