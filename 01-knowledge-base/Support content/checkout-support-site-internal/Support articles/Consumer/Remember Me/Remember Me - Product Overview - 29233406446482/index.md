**When to use this article**

This article provides an overview of the Remember Me product, explaining what it is, how it works, who it's for, and its key features. It is used to understand the product's core functionality and purpose. This is for the Merchant Care team.

 

## INTRODUCTION TO REMEMBER ME** 💬**

**Remember Me** is a feature designed to reduce shopping cart abandonment and increase conversion rates for merchants. It allows consumers to securely save their payment details once and reuse them across any Checkout.com merchant with the feature enabled. 

This creates a faster, more streamlined checkout process by removing the need for consumers to manually re-enter their information for every transaction.

 
 
**KEY TAKEAWAYS 🔑**

Remember Me is composed of key features for the consumer:

### **Effortless Sign-Up**

- Consumers can sign up for Remember Me and save their payment information with minimal effort directly within the payment flow after a successful transaction: 

### **Effortless Verification**

- 
Post payment, the consumer will receive a welcome email to confirm account creation and request verification:

### **Seamless Returning User Experience**

- 
When a returning consumer is recognised by their email address, they can authenticate via a one-time password (OTP) to access their saved payment methods for a quick checkout:

### **Self-Service Consumer Dashboard**

- A dedicated [dashboard](https://consumer-dashboard.checkout.com/) allows consumers to manage their accounts, view saved payment methods, and see their purchase history, enhancing transparency and trust. The Consumer dashboard can be accessed via:

  - The sign-in button [Save my card details with Checkout.com](https://www.checkout.com/for-consumers)

  - The welcome email under the “Confirm your details” button

## **FAQs ****❓**

 
**Why was Remember Me created?**It was built to address the high rate of online shopping cart abandonment (around 70%) caused by lengthy checkout processes. Remember Me offers a faster, wallet-like experience for consumers across all participating merchants.  
**How does a consumer create an account?**An account is created automatically after a consumer checks the "Save card details" box during checkout and completes a **successful payment**. If the payment fails, the account is not created.  
**Is account verification mandatory?**No. Consumers are not required to verify their email via the welcome email to use the Remember Me feature in subsequent purchases. However, they should be encouraged to do so to ensure their contact information is correct.
