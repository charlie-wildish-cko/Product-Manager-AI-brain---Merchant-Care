## 1. Stripe — Website & Market Positioning

### 1.1 Homepage narrative

- **Tagline**: “Financial infrastructure to grow your revenue.”
- **Positioning**: “Backbone of global commerce” — accept payments, offer financial services, implement custom revenue models “from your first transaction to your billionth.”
- **Audience**: Broad — startups, scale-ups, platforms, enterprises (e.g. Hertz, URBN, Instacart, Le Monde, Shopify, Uber, Slack).

### 1.2 Product pillars (as presented)

| Pillar | What the site emphasises |
|--------|--------------------------|
| **Payments** | Accept and optimize globally — online and in-person; 135+ currencies, 100+ payment methods; unified online + in-person (Terminal). |
| **Billing** | Enable any billing model; 200M+ active subscriptions on Stripe Billing. |
| **Agentic commerce** | “Monetize through agentic commerce” — sell via AI agents (Agentic Commerce Suite, ACP). |
| **Issuing** | Create card issuing programs. |
| **Crypto / stablecoins** | “Borderless money movement with stablecoins and crypto” — Open Issuance (Bridge), CASH (Phantom), etc. |
| **Platforms** | Embed payments in your platform (Connect, marketplace/payouts). |

### 1.3 Proof points (from stripe.com)

- **Volume**: $1.4T processed in 2024; businesses on Stripe generated $1.9T in 2025.
- **Scale**: 135+ currencies, 500M+ API requests/day, 10K+ requests/sec, 150K+ transactions/min.
- **Reliability**: 99.999% historical uptime (published status).
- **BFCM 2025**: $40B+ processed, 99.9999% uptime.
- **Local acquiring**: 46 markets, direct bank setups.

### 1.4 GTM & ecosystem

- **Self-serve**: “Get started” / “Start now” — create account, no code (Payment Links, no-code tools), or build (APIs, SDKs, MCP, docs).
- **Enterprise**: “Contact sales” for custom package; professional services, certified partners, support plans.
- **Programs**: Stripe Startups, Stripe Atlas (incorporate + fundraise + bank + payments).
- **Events**: Sessions (e.g. April 2026, San Francisco) — “internet economy” positioning.

### 1.5 2025 strategy themes (from announcements)

- **Agentic commerce**: Agentic Commerce Suite — sell through AI agents; single integration; Etsy, Urban Outfitters, Anthropologie, Coach onboarding.
- **Stablecoins**: Open Issuance (Bridge) — mint/manage stablecoins; CASH, mUSD, USDH; treasury (BlackRock, Fidelity, Superstate), Lead Bank for cash.
- **In-app payments**: “Process payments outside app stores” (new regulations) — more control, revenue growth.
- **Crypto at checkout**: Crypto.com partnership — pay with crypto balance at checkout.

## 2. Adyen — Website & Market Positioning

### 2.1 Homepage narrative

- **Tagline**: “Fintech you can bank on.”
- **Subline**: “One platform for payments, data, and financial products. Built to scale with the world’s leading businesses.”
- **Positioning**: Enterprise-first — “control, reliability, and expertise global enterprises depend on”; “When certainty matters, Adyen’s the platform of choice”; “If you’re building for scale, there’s no alternative.”

### 2.2 Product pillars (as presented)

| Pillar | What the site emphasises |
|--------|---------------------------|
| **Payments** | Accept payments everywhere — online (web, in-app, recurring, pay-by-link) and in-person; one platform to accept, process, settle. |
| **Unified Commerce** | Connect online and in-person payment data; cross-channel insights, flexible buying journeys, brand loyalty. |
| **Money movement** | Move money across the business — optimized payments, automated payouts; accept, settle, move funds on one platform. |
| **Platforms / embedded** | “Adyen for Platforms” — launch payments and financial products under your brand (payments, accounts, card issuing, capital), single integration. |
| **Data & optimization** | Risk management, revenue optimization, authentication; “actionable insights”; ML-driven. |

### 2.3 Proof points (from adyen.com)

- **Volume**: €1.4T processed annually.
- **Reliability**: 99.999% uptime.
- **Coverage**: 150+ currencies, 200+ local payment methods, 29 global offices; “trillions of euros in transaction data.”
- **Regulation**: US, UK, EU banking licenses; compliance and security (PCI DSS, PSD2/SCA, GDPR, ISAE3402/SOC 1).
- **Customers**: Adobe, Prada Group, Vagaro, Nord Security, L’Occitane, Meta, Rectangle Health.

### 2.4 GTM & ecosystem

- **Enterprise-led**: “Talk to our team” / “Contact us” / “Get in touch” — sales-led; no prominent self-serve signup.
- **Industries**: Retail, travel & hospitality, digital media, SaaS/platforms, food & beverage, financial services.
- **Unified Commerce**: Explicit product and messaging focus — one platform, cross-channel.

### 2.5 2025 strategy themes (from reports)

- **Unified Commerce**: Strong growth driver; Q1 2025 Unified Commerce net revenue €158.8M, +31% YoY.
- **Platforms & SaaS**: Platforms net revenue €55.5M, +63% YoY.
- **Agentic commerce**: Building “merchant-first foundation” for agentic commerce; collaborating with Google, OpenAI, Visa, Mastercard on standards; leaning on existing auth, fraud, payment methods.
- **Merchant-centric protocol**: “Merchant-centric protocol for the next era of AI-driven shopping.”

## 4. Market takeaways (for context)

- **Stripe**: Developer- and product-led; broad segment (startup → enterprise); emphasizes speed to market, APIs, and new frontiers (agentic commerce, stablecoins, in-app). Volume and ecosystem (Sessions, Atlas, Startups) support “internet economy” positioning.
- **Adyen**: Enterprise- and compliance-led; “one platform,” banking licenses, and Unified Commerce as differentiators. Less emphasis on self-serve or crypto; more on reliability, control, and industry-specific use cases.
- **Convergence**: Both push embedded financial products (issuing, platforms), data/optimization (auth, fraud, revenue), and agentic commerce; Stripe more vocal on crypto/stablecoins and developer experience, Adyen on unified data and enterprise governance.
