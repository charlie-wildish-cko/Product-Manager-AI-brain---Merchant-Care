# Merchant Care 2026 Content Strategy

> Owned by the Content team. This document is the reference for the customer-facing content roadmap that feeds both the support website (support.checkout.com, checkout.com/docs, api-reference.checkout.com) and Fin's knowledge base.

**Last Updated**: February 2026  
**Owner**: Content team (Charlie's contact: Content Strategist)


The 2026 content roadmap for Merchant Care aims to reduce cost to serve and improve merchant self-service by optimising content for humans and AI.


## Initiative 1: Proactive Support

**Focus:** Educate and empower merchants to self-serve through onboarding content, proactive communication, and AI-driven contextual answers. Aim to reduce inbound contacts overall.

**Success metrics:** Launch Education Hub with 20 high-priority operational guides that empower merchants to complete routine tasks without contacting support. Promote guide adoption with cross-linking in comms, Dashboard, docs, Welcome Pack etc.

**Action plan:**

- Analyse tickets, Fin conversations, and customer feedback to identify processes that would benefit the most from having guides
- Drive adoption and engagement of guides through different channels including Welcome Pack, merchant comms, Dashboard

## Initiative 2: Reactive Support

**Focus:** Resolve support contacts instantly, consistently, and at scale with AI as the first line of defence.

**Success metrics:** X% prevention or reduction in support tickets and escalations

**Action plan:**

- Analyse existing sources of customer pain points to increase coverage and improve troubleshooting content
- Identify relevant user touchpoints on the Dashboard to surface contextual support through the AI Agent
- Analyse the following areas, identify opportunities for improvement, and update relevant content:

  - Adjustments and fees
  - AFT docs (Fernando)
  - API response codes (Preethy)
  - Disputes (Fernando)

## Initiative 3: Feedback Loop

**Focus:** Formalise two-way intelligence (agent and customer) to drive a better support experience through content.

**Success metrics:**

- **Customer Insight:** Detailed feedback from x customers on support experience and documentation effectiveness
- **Agent Intelligence:** Y roundtable sessions; agents have resources to understand how and when to raise content gaps with the tech writing team

**Action plan:**

**Customer Insight**:
- Contact merchants who've left low CSAT scores for Fin conversations to close the loop and ask for feedback
- Develop a standardised interview guide to ensure consistent data collection across all customer feedback sessions
- Synthesise customer feedback with internal data to identify the top 3 pain points that can be directly addressed by content or product changes

**Agent Intelligence**:
- Stabilise and enhance the agent feedback loop to capture real-time content and product gaps, including socialisation of the content ecosystem for agents to understand when something must be raised as a gap
- Run quarterly roundtable sessions with agents


## Initiative 4: Ongoing Improvement for Fin

**Focus:** Continuously improve Fin's accuracy and coverage to improve the support experience.

**Success metrics:** Contribute to Fin resolution rate of ~70%

**Action plan:**

- Continue monitoring escalations and analyse for patterns
- Build a scalable tagging framework in Intercom for Fin to use Merchant 360 data to only surface relevant content for answers
- Empower tech writers to own their areas in support, including testing for Fin consumability
