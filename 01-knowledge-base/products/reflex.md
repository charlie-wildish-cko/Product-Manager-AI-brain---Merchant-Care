# Reflex

> Support insights solution that analyzes support data to identify root causes and publishes them on demand or on schedule.

## What it is

**Reflex** analyzes support data to identify contact root causes. It can publish insights on demand or on a schedule (e.g. weekly, quarterly).

## Why it matters

Support contacts are a lagging signal of product problems. Today, the data exists — Zendesk tickets, Fin conversations, merchant NPS — but it sits in silos, unstructured, and unread at scale.

Three problems compound this:

1. **No direct line from merchants to PMs.** Product teams want first-hand merchant feedback but have no systematic way to access it. The signal is buried in ticket queues that PMs never read.
2. **No consequences for driving contact volume.** Product teams are not measured on support contacts generated by their features. Without visibility or accountability, support data is not a prioritisation input.
3. **Qualitative and quantitative data are disconnected.** Zendesk tickets tell you*what* merchants contact about; NPS tells you*how they feel*. Joined together — via merchant/client ID — they form a full 360 view of merchant experience that neither dataset provides alone. Today that join doesn't exist at scale.

The result: the same issues recur quarter after quarter, agents handle the same contacts repeatedly, and fixes that would reduce volume never get prioritised because the signal never reaches the people who can act on it.

Reflex closes this loop. By attributing contacts to products, aggregating themes, and surfacing ranked drivers — with both quantitative volume data and qualitative merchant voice — it turns support data into a prevention engine. The business case is direct: a human agent contact costs ~$40; eliminating a contact category entirely costs $0. Every product fix compounds across every future transaction.

Reflex also creates the data foundation for the rest of the Care AI stack.

## Users

- **Care Product team** — understand what issues their products create
- **Product Managers** — see contact drivers and prevention opportunities for their products

## Components

| Component                          | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Data Ingestion Layer**     | BQ pipelines: Zendesk tickets, Fin conversation metadata (Intercom API), Product Catalogue (Airtable), Merchant NPS (Airtable, joined via client ID). Fin conversation content (full text) is TBD — Intercom API export does not include it.                                                                 |
| **AI Engine ("the whale")**  | LLM enrichment layer on BQ: per-ticket root cause summaries, theme aggregation, product team mapping (via taxonomy + product catalogue), spike detection, VoC correlation, content gap identification                                                                                                         |
| **Insights Query Interface** | Self-serve reporting for Support Leaders, PMs, and Product teams: contact driver dashboard, product team views, spike alert log, VoC view, self-serve query (tool TBD)                                                                                                                                        |
| **Reflex MCP**               | Programmatic API:`GET /top-contact-drivers`, `GET /issue-detail/:issue_type`, `GET /product-insights/:product_id`, `GET /spike-alerts`. Enables query interface and other internal teams to query insights. Cross-deliverable dependency for MCD-568 (Fin Procedures) and MCD-564 (Agent Consultant). |
| **Jira Integration**         | Quarterly auto-creation of top 5–10 stack-ranked contact drivers as Jira issues per product pillar; pre-populated with volume, cost, trend, example tickets, recommended team; human review before publish                                                                                                   |
| **Knowledge graph**          | Logically matches the content sources we have which solve our taxonomy to the customer contacts we received                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Data graph**               | Logically matches the data sources and fields/values needed to solve our taxonomy to the customer contacts we received                                                                                                                                                                                        |

Question: do our Knowledge and data graphs live in Reflex or is Reflex a consumer of the graph to match to the contacts it analyses?

## Success Metrics

**North Star**: % volume of support contact reductions post-fix (contact volume reduction attributable to product fixes surfaced by Reflex)

| Metric                              | Type       | Description                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| % contact reduction post-fix        | North Star | Contact volume drop after a product fix is shipped for a Reflex-identified driver |
| % Product Fix Coverage              | Leading    | % of top contact drivers targeted with a fix delivered                            |
| $ reduction in annual support costs | Outcome    | Cost impact of contact volume reduction                                           |
| % increase in Merchant CSAT/NPS     | Guardrail  | Must not decline as automation increases                                          |
| ≥95% data attribution rate         | Health     | % of tickets successfully attributed to a product/issue type                      |
| # active PMs accessing the data     | Adoption   | Number of product managers querying Reflex insights                               |

## Vision: Insight & Prevention solution (2030 end goal)

**End goal by 2030:** Reflex operates like **Stripe Minions** — an autonomous Insight & Prevention solution that identifies root causes, generates action plans (and potentially code/PRs), and escalates to Care & Product teams for triage and resolution as BAU. Humans review and ship; Reflex does the analysis and drafting.

### Stripe Minions (analogy)

[Stripe Minions](https://stripe.dev/blog/minions-stripes-one-shot-end-to-end-coding-agents) are Stripe’s internal AI coding agents: they write code end-to-end in one shot, producing 1000+ merged PRs per week. Humans review and merge; Minions do the work. The parallel for Reflex: autonomous agents that produce actionable outputs (action plans, PRs) for humans to triage and ship — rather than humans doing the analysis and drafting from scratch.
